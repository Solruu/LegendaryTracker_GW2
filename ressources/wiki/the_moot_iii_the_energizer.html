The Moot III: The Energizer is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Energizer, for the Legendary mace, The Moot. It is the final collection needed to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

The Moot III: The Energizer Legendary Weapons 3
After the successful hunt comes the celebration, when all the pent-up energy of the battle is released in food and song. Drink, dine, dance,
and revel across the land of Tyria in your journey to attain the legendary precursor known as the Energizer.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Mace
Reward: Chest of the Celebration

Notes[edit]

  • Heart vendors will sell several of these ales for karma, which totals 6,300 .
  • Buttermilk and Cherries can be bought in bulk for 112 .
  • To complete this collection, you will need a total of 251 Indigo Mushrooms, which you can combine stacks of 25 mushrooms to make a Drop of Indigo Mushroom Milk.

Trivia[edit]

  • The Bowl of Sharkfin Soup and its recipe were originally intended to be used in this collection, but were removed shortly after being released. While the recipe is still available for purchase, the Shark Fins no longer drop.

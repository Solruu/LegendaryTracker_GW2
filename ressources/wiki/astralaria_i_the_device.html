Astralaria I: The Device is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Mechanism, for the legendary axe, Astralaria. This collection is the first step to gain the first tier of the precursor, The Device.

Achievement[edit]

Astralaria I: The Device Legendary Weapons 3
The saying goes: The history of Tyria is written in the stars, and indeed, the tales of the past and hints of the future are inscribed in the heavens.
Help Ogden Stonehealer in revealing and gathering ancient knowledge in your quest to create The Device, the first of the precursors to the legendary axe, Astralaria.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Astralaria Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Ancient Knowledge
Collection
 Collected 19 Items 3

Notes[edit]

  • Zinn's Prize Student collection takes a minimum of 12 days to finish and costs 300 Ley Line Crystals, You must also right click and add to wardrobe in order for it to apply to achievement.
  • Pouch of Jotun Runestones costs 1,848 .
  • The items required for the achievement can be safely destroyed or sold after acquisition.

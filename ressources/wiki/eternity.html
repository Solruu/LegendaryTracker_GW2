Graphics[edit]

Eternity is a greatsword whose appearance switches between Sunrise and Twilight depending on the time of day. Additionally, the pommel of Eternity consists of a multicolored gem which emanates a bright glow.

Day/night cycle
  • Day: Eternity appears as Sunrise and shares all of its graphics excluding footfalls. In addition, the blade emanates a sky-colored smoke/flame.
  • Night: Eternity appears as Twilight and shares all of its graphics excluding footfalls. In addition, the blade emanates a dark themed smoke/flame.
Aura
  • The user's body is surrounded by blue light/darkness that radiates much like smoke or flames.
  • Small blue/dark orbs rise from the user's body.
Draw/Stow effects
  • When drawn, a burst of light/darkness emanates from the user's body.
Footfalls
  • The user leaves behind painterly footprints that act as a window into a starry sky. Orange/purple orbs also rise from the footprints depending on the time of day.
Skill effects
  • Ranged skills will throw rotating crossed Sunrise and Twilight instead of the default projectile. This effect changes the following skills:
    • Guardian:   Whirling Wrath and   Binding Blade.
      • Dragonhunter:   Spear of Justice.
    • Warrior:   Bladetrail and   Throw Rock (downed skill).
    • Ranger:   Throw Dirt (downed skill) .
      • Soulbeast: any projectile from Soulbeast pet skills such as   Narcotic Spores.
    • Mesmer:   Mirror Blade and   Phantasmal Berserker.
    • Revenant:   Essence Sap (downed skill).
      • Renegade:   Citadel Bombardment.
    • Necromancer:
      • Harbinger Shroud:   Tainted Bolts and   Dark Barrage.
      • Ritualist:   Essence Blast

Notes[edit]

  • All accounts that add Eternity to their Legendary Armory will also automatically unlock Sunrise and Twilight if they don't already have them, both to the armory and as skins. This includes accounts that had a bound Eternity prior to the July 13, 2021 update.[1]
  • Crafting Eternity using Sunrise and Twilight will unlock those skins on your account even if you don't bind the Eternity.
  • Eternity created from Memory of Sunrise and/or Memory of Twilight will be automatically bound to your Legendary Armory and can't be sold. Even when in legendary armory, the skin itself will be unlocked only after equiping Eternity itself.
Anomaly:
Creating Eternity in this manner will add the weapon to your Legendary Armory but not automatically unlock the skin. You need to specifically equip Eternity on a character once to unlock the skin for your Wardrobe.

Trivia[edit]

  • The Risen High Wizard protecting the gates of Arah in the Gates of Arah meta event can be seen wielding Eternity.
  • The statue behind the Wall of Champions in the Heart of the Mists is wielding Eternity.
  • Zommoros can be seen dual-wielding two Eternity, one in night form and the other in day form, while trapped inside Qadim's Lamp.

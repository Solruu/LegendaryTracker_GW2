Kudzu III: Leaf of Kudzu is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Leaf of Kudzu, for the legendary longbow, Kudzu. It is the final collection needed to be able to craft the precursor. This collection is unlocked once you've crafted Perfected Longbow.

Achievement[edit]

Kudzu III: Leaf of Kudzu Legendary Weapons 3
Within sight of a great volcano lies a hidden garden that you must make your own. Tame and raise the plants within this hidden garden to master their secrets,
channeling that power into your efforts to create the legendary precursor Leaf of Kudzu.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Longbow
Reward: Chest of the Garden
Collection
 Collected 1 Item 1
Collected 29 Items 2

Notes[edit]

  • Click on the 'Germinate [x]' link to find appropriate level maps for each node. Ex. look for carrot nodes found in level 1-25 areas.
  • You can plant the seeds, fertilize, and water the soil in any order. However, each time you use a bucket, it will be consumed, meaning that you will need to purchase five of them from Milton Book for 1,848  each.
  • After completing this achievement, you will have grown your own personal hidden garden, which you can harvest daily. It will have 2 to 3 nodes of each of the seeds you planted. This personal garden shouldn't be confused with the garden plots in players Home instances.
  • Several seeds can be found at dense Node farm locations that reset daily and can be farmed by multiple characters. Home instance garden plot nodes drop the seeds occasionally as well.

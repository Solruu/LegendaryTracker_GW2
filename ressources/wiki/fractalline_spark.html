Achievements[edit]

  • Farming Fractalline Dust for a Fractalline Spark requires approximately 30 runs of fractal quickplay.
  • The first five runs each week will count double, as each tier of Weekly Fractal Quickplay provides an additional 5 dust.
Incursive Investigation: Infinite Recursion Incursive Investigation 0
Earn fractalline dust by completing quickplay fractals and related Fractal Incursion achievements.
Prerequisite: Incursive Investigation: Third Recursion
Reward: Fractalline Spark
Obtained 150 Fractalline Dust 0

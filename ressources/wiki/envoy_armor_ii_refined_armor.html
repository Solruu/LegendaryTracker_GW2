Envoy Armor II: Refined Armor is a Legendary Armor collection achievement to gain the final tier of the precursor armor for the Legendary armor, Perfected Envoy armor. This collection is the second step to obtain the armor.

Notes[edit]

  • To Unlock: Complete the Envoy Armor I collection, and then speak with a crafter in Lion's Arch who matches your chosen discipline: Muireall (Armorsmith), Giita (Tailor), or Hamil (Leatherworker).
  • A single Crystalline Heart can be used to complete the entire collection.
  • For the Demonic Heart the player must attack Deimos in order to receive credit for the collection.
  • You lose the buff on every death, so make sure to re-apply the buff after every attempt of the raid bosses.
  • The final step of this collection, the Redeemed Heart, must be acquired last, after all the other hearts have been collected. It can be obtained in any completed instance of Bastion of the Penitent.

Trivia[edit]

Notes[edit]

  1. ^ "A Star to Guide Us" Mastery: After completing this achievement you are able to buy Updated Record of Joko's Deeds from Trader Hyacinth.
  2. ^ Big Bluffs Hunter: Doesn't require you to kill the enemies, looting the chest contributes to advancing the achievement.
  3. ^ I Brought a Friend: Lure a branded enemy into combat with the sniper.
  4. ^ Philanthropist: Full completion of the achievement requires a total of 5 gold.
  5. ^ Pod Smasher: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Pod Smasher.

Trivia[edit]

  • "Sniper, No Sniping!" is a nod to "Swiper, No Swiping!", a phrase used in the kids' show Dora the Explorer, which has become a meme.
  • "You Can Certainly Try" is a nod to Matthew Mercer, the Male Norn Pact Commander voice actor. The line is a catchphrase used by Mercer in the series Critical Role.
  • The flavor text for the Jahai Bounty Hunter achievement; "Face the mighty nine! (Or, seven? Wait...)" is also a nod to the characters of Critical Role's Second Campaign.

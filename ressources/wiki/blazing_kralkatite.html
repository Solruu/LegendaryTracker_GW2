Acquisition[edit]

  • Combine 10 Kralkatite Ores and 5 Powdered Rose Quartz at the Beam of Light at the top of The Astralarium.
  1. You must align all the telescopic mirrors, facing into the center of the Astralarium.
  2. You must be on the upper level (where the 8 columns hold up the telescope), standing close to where the light hits underneath the telescope.
  3. Ignore the initial interation dialogue. You must click on and target the Beam of Light before the correct option to combine window appears.

Notes[edit]

  • This step can be done the same day, after step #6 Mirror Calibration Readings has been completed, but Yasna will not accept the Blazing Kralkatite until the following day.

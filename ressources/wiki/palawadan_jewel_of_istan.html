Palawadan, Jewel of Istan is the capital and main port of the Domain of Istan, formerly known as Kamadan, the Jewel of Istan. Palawa Joko's Loyalist forces and Istani nobility based in the city have denied food shipments to the rest of the Isle, which incited the Sunspear Uprising.[1]

Trivia[edit]

The Guild Wars Wiki has an article on Kamadan, Jewel of Istan.
  • In Guild Wars Nightfall, the city was named Kamadan, Jewel of Istan. In the events preceding the Pact Commander arriving in the Domain of Istan, Palawa Joko renamed the city in his honor.

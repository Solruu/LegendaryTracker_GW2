Combine a Gift of Infused Gems, a Gift of the Sun, a Gift of the Beach, and 10 Purified Rift Essences in the Mystic Forge to create Gift of Rays.
Created by combining the following in the Mystic Forge:
• 250 Obsidian Shards
• 250 Beryl, Chrysocola, Emerald, Opal, Ruby, or Sapphire Orbs
• 1 Bloodstone Shard
• 100 Mystic Runestone
 — In-game description

Notes[edit]

  • A total of 17 Antique Summoning Stones can be gained per week.
  • It takes 20 weeks to buy the required amount for Gift of Jade Mastery or 6 weeks by also completing daily Dragon's End meta event and weekly Strike missions with challenge mode active, unless they are bought from the trading post.
  • Added with the Guild Wars 2: End of Dragons expansion.
  • 5 Antique Summoning Stone can be gained from completing the Canthan Reward Track on repeatable as often you want for 5 antique stones every time

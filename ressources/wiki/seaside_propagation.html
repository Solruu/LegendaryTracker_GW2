Acquisition

[edit]
  • From the  Ancient Hollow Waypoint — waypoint, walk south-east until you reach the edge. Drop down and glide to the north side of the small island directly below with the Fertile Soil.

Notes

[edit]
  • After planting the seed, a Primordial Orchid will be available for daily harvest on the fertile soil.

Achievement[edit]

Acquiring Agaleus Legendary Armor 5
Complete 24 steps to acquire the legendary precursor Agaleus.It's not just the "captain's" story that's fishy...
Reward: Agaleus Container
Objectives: 24 objectives in total
  • Talk to the castaway "captain" in Pub Canach.
  • Bring a drink to the captain.
  • Listen to the rest of Captain Lakes's tale.
  • Find the diver.
  • Complete the Master Diver achievement, then return to Dive Master Astora.
  • Find the treasure in Sharkmaw Caverns.
  • Find the sunken treasure in the Siren's Reef fractal or Underwater Ruins fractal.
  • Look for treasure using your skimmer's Aetherlocation skill in Castora.
  • Defeat Taidha Covington to retrieve a piece of the mask from her reward chest.
  • Find the piece of the mask in the 'Booter's Path in Castora.
  • Find the piece of the mask in the base of Wiley's Buccaneers.
  • Discover where Wiley hid his piece of the mask.
  • Find the piece of the mask in Castora.
  • Find the mask piece on one of the freebooters in Castora.
  • Find the piece of the mask in Southsun Cove.
  • Find the piece of the mask in Lion's Arch.

  • Return to Captain Lakes to figure out how to put all the pieces together.
  • Speak to Cecil the Strong Arm.

  • Complete one of the explorable paths in Honor of the Waves to help the Shark Spirit.
  • Return to the Shark Spirit.
  • Return to the shaman.

Objectives[edit]

Learn rumors about the legendary aquabreather[edit]

  1. Talk to "Captain" Ruymond Lakes NPC near  Pub Canach Waypoint —
  2. Using the Mixology Station, create a drink and take it to the captain
  3. Listen to captain's dialogue
  4. Complete (previous completions do count for this) Sunken Treasure Hunter: Master Diver — Find and open the sunken chests. (5)
  5. Speak with Dive Master Astora in Lion's Arch near  Moran Memorial —

Obtain pieces of the mask[edit]

There are eleven mask pieces. You'll first need to complete Weyandt's Revenge jumping puzzle in Sharkmaw Caverns (Lion's Arch). You'll then receive the Tattered Note, hinting at the locations of the other mask pieces, which may be obtained in any order.

These steps are completed in Castora:

  1. Use your skimmer's Activate Aetherlocation to open Aetherlocation Treasures in Castora. Must have Castoran Intuition mastery unlocked.
  2. Obtain the mask piece from a chest by following the  Greenroot Grotto Skimmerway — in Shipwreck Strand
  3. Defeat enemies for a random drop near  Grogslinger's — in Shipwreck Strand
  4. Complete jumping puzzle at  The 'Booter's Path —

These steps are completed in Central Tyria:

  1. Complete either Siren's Reef Fractal or Aquatic Ruins Fractal
    1. After completing the fractal, loot a Sunken Chest (requires a Ornate Rusted Key)
    2. Note: Bring a few keys in case you open the wrong chest
  2. Complete the world boss event [Group Event] Kill Admiral Taidha Covington (50) at  Covington Keep —
  3. Obtain Wiley's mask piece from Gendarran Fields
    1. Interact with a Torn Note inside of  Wiley's Cove —
    2. Read the note in your inventory
    3. Teleport to  Vigil Keep Waypoint —
    4. Find the cave along the cliff face south of the waypoint
    5. Enter the cave and loot the chest at the far end of the chest
    6. Note: There are two chests in the cave. The mask piece is contained in the far chest. It may require a relog to appear.
  4. Obtain the mask piece deep underwater near  Privateer's Moorage — in Southsun Cove
  5. Obtain the mask piece deep underwater west of  Phoenix Roost — in Lion's Arch. It may require a relog to appear.
  6. Obtain the mask piece deep underwater near  Death's Anthem — in Cursed Shore

Combining the mask pieces[edit]

  1. Return to "Captain" Ruymond Lakes at  Pub Canach Waypoint —
  2. Talk to Cecil, who is standing next to the captain
  3. Talk to the Castaway NPC, just west and down the cliff from  Leystone Promontory — (may require a relog to appear)
  4. Teleport to  Honor of the Waves Waypoint —
  5. Speak with the shark spirit swimming in the waters to the west of the waypoint
  6. Complete any explorable path in the Honor of the Waves dungeon
  7. Return to the shark
  8. Return to the Castaway NPC to obtain Agaleus Container

Lore[edit]

Meteorlogicus
Meteorlogicus was designed by a sylvari astrologer, Surya—a love letter to the sky. She once used the orrery to trace a thunderous cloud that lurked on the horizon. She knew then: a dragon had awoken.

Graphics[edit]

Meteorlogicus is a glowing orrery whose planets and clouds move. Sparkling stars emanate from the center glow.

Meteorlogicus has a day/night cycle.

Day
  • Fluffy white clouds emanate from the base of the orrey.
  • The sun glows yellow, and a blue glow surrounds the entire scepter.
  • A blue trail follows the weapon when wielded, but not when stowed.
Night
  • The clouds are dark, and lightning appears around them.
  • The sun glows violet, and a violet glow surrounds the entire scepter.
  • A violet trail follows the weapon when wielded and when stowed (after being drawn for the first time in a new area).
Footfalls
  • The user leaves behind lightning bolts followed by dark clouds.
Skill Effects
  • Default projectiles are replaced with a celestial stream of energy, which also leave a faint sparkling trail in its path. This effect replaces the following skills:
    • Guardian: Orb of Wrath.
      • Dragonhunter: Spear of Justice.
    • Necromancer: Dhuumfire, Dark Path and Life Blast.
    • Elementalist: Fiery Frost, Glacial Drift, Hurl, Ice Shards, Stone Shards, and Water Trident.
      • Tempest: Overload Fire's whirl finisher.
    • Mesmer: Ether Barrage, Ether Blast, Ether Bolt, and Counterspell.
    • Revenant:
      • Renegade: Citadel Bombardment
    • Thief:
      • Specter: Shadow Bolt, Double Bolt, Triple Bolt, Shadow Sap, Measured Shot

Trivia[edit]

  • This scepter is bigger than most of the others in the game.
  • It has a base damage type of Lightning.
  • The symbols on the pommel spell out "rain" in Ascalonian.
  • Meteorology is the study of the atmosphere and, in particular, weather forecasting.

Glimmering Resin Weapon Collector is a Rare Collections achievement that involves unlocking all 17 Glimmering Resin weapon skins.

Achievement[edit]

Glimmering Resin Weapon Collector Rare Collections 3
Unlock all 17 Glimmering Resin weapon skins by crafting them after unlocking their recipe or by purchasing them from completed renown heart vendors in Eternity's Garden.
Reward: Gift of Restoration
Collection
 Unlocked 1 Skins 1
Unlocked 8 Skins 1
Unlocked 17 Skins 1

Chasing Tales[edit]

These achievements each lead to a Heroic Dragonsblood weapon. The steps can be summarised as:

  • Complete the Dragonsblood story step once to unlock account wide access to Forge Master Hilina's wares.
  • Buy a Dragonsblood Weapon Recipe Book from Forge Master Hilina (500 Volatile Magic plus 5 Laurels).
  • Pick and consume the recipe for the weapon type of your choice.
  • Craft the Dragonsblood weapon (requires 60 Branded Mass and 3 Exquisite Serpentite Jewels, plus other non-account bound ingredients).
  • Buy the appropriate Chasing Tales item from Forge Master Hilina (requires 100 Branded Mass and 5 ). This item is instantly consumed and unlocks four hidden achievements, one of which tracks completion of the other three.
  • Complete the other three achievements (various tasks usually involving killing Branded)
  • Use a Vision Crystal at the Heroic Font in the Thunderhead Keep to receive the Vision item. (This step can only be completed after the prior three achievements have been completed.)
  • Craft the Heroic Dragonsblood weapon using the Vision item, Steelstar's Inscription (requires 50 Branded Mass) and other regular ascended weapon components.

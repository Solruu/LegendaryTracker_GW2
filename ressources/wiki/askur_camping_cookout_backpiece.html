Notes[edit]

  • After being destroyed or used in a recipe, Askur Camping Cookout Backpiece Remnant with the same Mystic Forge functionality can be bought from Ward Trader Sampaguita.
  • This item can be dyed. It has 4 dye channels.
  • This item's description was updated from "This back item is used for crafting Janthir Wilds legendaries. Further copies can be purchased from Ward Trader Sampaguita." with the June 24, 2025 game update.

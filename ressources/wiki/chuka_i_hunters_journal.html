Related achievements[edit]

Notes[edit]

  • The Completed Hunter's Journal has a 20th entry.
  • You have to do the collection in the numeric order. Otherwise things like Tiger Marks won't show up.
  • You need to craft the Hunter's Journal before you can make progress on any of the "Entries", however it does not need to be in inventory when completing them.
  • You can do this collection - and most of the Chuka & Champawat collections - on any character, doesn't have to be a Huntsman.

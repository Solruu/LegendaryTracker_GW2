Notes[edit]

  • If the player has already purchased a Catmander's Compendium, the Commander's Compendium will be discounted to 150  + 250 .
  • A Commander's Compendium or Catmander's Compendium is required to buy Commander's Wings of War.

Trivia[edit]

  • At release, this item cost 100  but only unlocked Commander privileges for the character who used it; as of the September 2014 Feature Pack update, the unlock is now account-wide.

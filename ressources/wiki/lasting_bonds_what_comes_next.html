Lasting Bonds: What Comes Next is an A Bug in the System hidden achievement and the third and final Lasting Bonds collection that involves collecting items for Efi, a young Olmakhan charr cub found in Atholma. It is unlocked after receiving the Pocketed Olmakhan Bandolier from her for completing the prior achievement Lasting Bonds: What We Do Here, and Lasting Bonds: Where We Come From before that. Completion awards a 32-slot Reinforced Olmakhan Bandolier bag, also required for Visions of Sandswept Isles in Vision I: Awakening.

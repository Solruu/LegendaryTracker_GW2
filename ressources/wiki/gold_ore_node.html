Gold Ores are resource nodes found in level 40-55 areas. They can be mined at least three times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • If a Steel or better mining pick is used
    • Gold Ore (guaranteed)
    • Amethyst Lump
    • Carnelian Lump
    • Lapis Lump
    • Peridot Lump
    • Spinel Lump
    • Sunstone Lump
    • Topaz Lump
  • If a Steel or better mining pick is not used
    • Ruined Ore Chunk (guaranteed)

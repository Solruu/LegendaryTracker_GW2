Double-click to coat your weapon with Itzel Spirit Poison. Used to sever the spirit strings from the Keep Construct.

Itzel Spirit Poison is a gizmo that applies the Itzel Spirit Poison effect for obtaining the Spirit Strings from defeating the Keep Construct boss in the Stronghold of the Faithful raid wing, required for the Envoy Armor I: Experimental Armor Legendary Armor collection.

Notes

[edit]
  • This item has unlimited uses and will not be consumed upon application of the effect. As such, after acquiring the Spirit Strings, it is safe to be sold or discarded.
  • Spirit Strings was moved from the environmental Keep Construct Chest to the rewarded coffer item in April 2019[1]. The  Itzel Spirit Poison effect must be active when opening the Keep Construct's Coffer in order to obtain the Spirit Strings. It is suggested that players still activate the poison effect before fighting the Keep Construct as a safety precaution.

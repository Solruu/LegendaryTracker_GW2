Lore[edit]

The HMS Divinity
As Zhaitan's influence grew, the Vigil raced to the site of Kryta's greatest maritime tragedy on an improbable mission. The HMS Divinity's long-dead crew—shackled to their ship as it sank—were all recovered.

Graphics[edit]

Aura
  • Sea foam or fog billows from the player's wrists and trails out behind them as they move.
  • Upon drawing the weapon, there is a splash effect around the player.
Draw/Stow effects
  • When drawing the weapon, the masts unfold, the sails go up, the ship's wheel spins, and three small cannons stick out of each side of the ship and fire in sequence.
  • Upon stowing the weapon, the ship's wheel spins, the cannons retract, the sails lower, and the masts fold down.
Footfalls
  • Small puddles of water with ripples, like a cannonball landed in them.
Skill effects
  • Default projectiles are replaced with flaming cannonballs that leave a fiery trail, accompanied by the greatly muted sound of a cannon firing. This effect changes the following skills:
    • Deadeye: Brutal Aim, Deadly Aim, Death's Judgment, Skirmisher's Shot, Spotter's Shot, Double Tap, Three Round Burst, Death's Retreat, and Trail of Knives (downed skill).
    • Engineer: Rifle Burst, Jump Shot, Throw Junk (downed skill), and Grenade Barrage.
      • Holosmith: Blade Burst, Particle Accelerator.
    • Warrior: Kill Shot, Fierce Shot, Volley, Explosive Shell, Brutal Shot, and Throw Rock (downed skill).
      • Berserker: Gun Flame.

Notes[edit]

  • It is recommended that you do not attempt to stat change your precursor item with the standard stat swap recipe in the Mystic Forge. While you will be provided with an item with the desired stats, it will have been replaced with a generic ascended item that cannot be used as a legendary precursor.

Trivia[edit]

  • Added to the game with the February 8th 2017 update (LW season 3, Episode 4: Head of the Snake).
  • HMS is a ship prefix used by British warships that stands for "Her/His Majesty's Ship."
  • A "gift-wrapped" version of this skin can be found as part of the Wrapped weapons set.

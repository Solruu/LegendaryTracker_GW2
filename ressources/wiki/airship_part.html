Rewarded by[edit]

  • Completing the group event Find and slay the treasure mushroom! (5).
  • Completing the map Verdant Brink (50).
  • Rewarded for completing events in Verdant Brink.

Vendors[edit]

  • Itzel Mastery Vendors in Verdant Brink, Auric Basin, and Tangled Depths provide a wide variety of goods.
  • Used to purchase Machetes from the Whispers Keeper at the Pact Base Camp in Dragon's Stand.
  • Can be used in the appropriately upgraded Ley-Energy Matter Converter to purchase 1 container daily.
  • 16,100 are required for all collections and unlocks.

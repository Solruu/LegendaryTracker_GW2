Frostfang I: The Experimental Axe is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Tooth of Frostfang, for the legendary axe, Frostfang. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Tooth of Frostfang Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Frostfang I: The Experimental Axe Legendary Weapons 3
The power of Jormag and the other Elder Dragons seeped into the lands of Tyria during the long epochs while they slept. Upwellings of this power may be found throughout the land,
for those who know where to look and how to capture it. Let finding this power be the first part of your journey to craft the legendary precursor Tooth of Frostfang.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Frostfang Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Dragons
Collection
 Collected 15 Items 3

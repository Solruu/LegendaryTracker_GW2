Aurora: Awakening is a Legendary Trinkets collection achievement to gain the Gift of Valor, a requirement for creating Aurora. This collection is the first step in obtaining this item. Each collection item after the Sentient Seed is obtained from its own achievement collection.

Notes[edit]

  • The Draconis Mons Master collection requires completion of the A Henge Away from Home collection, starting this collection from scratch requires 16 Druid Runestones that can only be collected once per day, it is therefore recommended to start this process early.
  • Continuing the topic of time-gated drops, Crystallized Suet required for Bitterfrost Frontier Master is a random drop from chests in The Bitter Cold. There are five chests, and each chest can only be opened once per day per character.
  • Ancient Cipher required for Siren's Landing Master is a random drop from chests in Abaddon's Reliquary, which can only be opened twice per day per character.
  • Roll for Greed is another time-gated achievement that can count towards Siren's Landing Master. It requires 10 Hidden Reliquary Chests total to be opened in Abaddon's Reliquary, and only two chests a day can be opened per character. However, this task is optional if you choose to obtain the greaves by completing any other 36/44 achievements under One Path Ends (achievements) or completing the related PvP Reward Tracks.
  • Finally, Bloodstone Chip, Petrified Burl, Plump Winterberry, Sparking Jade Shard, Albino Orchid Blossom and Orrian Nacre are random drops from the map-specific resource nodes, so obtaining them may also be time-gated.

Awakening the Druid Stone is a hidden achievement that is unlocked when the player completes the The Druid Stone. After completion, the corresponding achievement Sprouting the Druid Stone gets unlocked.

Achievement[edit]

Awakening the Druid Stone Flashpoint 3
Continue to cultivate your relationship with nature through tributes to the druid spirits of Draconis Mons.
Prerequisite: The Druid Stone
Reward: Living Druid Stone
Collection
 Collected 14 Items 3

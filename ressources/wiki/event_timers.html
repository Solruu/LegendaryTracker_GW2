These are some of the significant dynamic events that happen on a set timer. They include world bosses as well as meta events in various expansion and Living World zones.

You can reach this page from the game by entering /wiki et into the in-game chat box.

The event timer is customizable: event bars can be hidden using the [x] in the top-right corner of each bar, repositioned by dragging, and further adjusted using the settings panel below. For a complete list of available options, see: Feature documentation.

Related items

  • World Boss Portal Device — a Gem Store item that announces the occurrence of world bosses in the in-game chat and allows instant teleportation to their locations.
  • Ley-Infused Lodestone — a gizmo purchased from Magister Ela Makkay or Sarettokk that indicates the current or next map for the Ley-Line Anomaly.
  • Maguuma Pact Operation Portal Device — a Gem Store item that announces the occurrence of meta events within the Heart of Maguuma and instant teleportation to their locations.
  • Hero's Choice Chest — a type of a daily reward from several meta events in expansion zones.

Notes[edit]

  • The recipe is discovered upon completing Lasting Bonds: What We Do Here.
  • The recipe is unlocked account wide for the 3 professions (Tailor, Leatherworker, Armorsmith) it can be crafted with.
  • Can be given to Efi to acquire "Hope for Tomorrow", required for the Lasting Bonds: What Comes Next collection.
    • Efi will not take the Bandolier if it is occupying a bag slot.
    • Giving the Bandolier to Efi will permanently remove it from your inventory.

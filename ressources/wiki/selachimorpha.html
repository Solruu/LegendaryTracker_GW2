Lore[edit]

Selachimorpha
"Thought I was pullin' yer fins, didn't ya? I told ya. I tell... I tell no... I told ya the truth!" —"Captain" Lakes

Agaleus is an artifact of the Spirit of the Wild Shark and greatly improves its wearer's swimming capabilities, giving them some of the physical features of a shark. Captain Weyandt, known as a collector of valuable and powerful items, acquired the pieces of Agaleus with the help of one of his crew members who was a former Shaman of Shark. Weyandt, fearing mutiny, decided to hide the pieces of the mask where he alone would be able to retrieve them. He hid the pieces in the waters of Shipwreck Strand, Siren's Reef, Southsun Cove, Lion's Arch, Bloodtide Coast and Cursed Shore, but some of the pieces ended up in the possession of Captain Wiley and Admiral Taidha Covington.

Agaleus would eventually be reassembled by the same former Shaman of Shark who originally found the pieces, but only after the new recipient of the mask proved themselves to Shark by helping the Spirit in Honor of the Waves.

Graphics[edit]

Selachimorpha is a piece of Aquatic Headgear which when equipped underwater adds iridescent blue fins to the character's arms, legs and back. The character is also surrounded by swimming schools of blue fish.

  • Charr characters have an additional two fins on the tail.
  • The fins have a dynamic rippling light effect.
Effects
  • The character's body is surrounded by large air bubbles and schools of blue fish.
  • The character is trailed by fish, air bubbles and a white jet stream.
  • The neon lines on the front the mask are lights that fade and pulse.
  • Currently, both the fish and the fins disappear when fully dead underwater.
  • The visual effect will disappear during downstate.
  • If Selachimorpha is worn while on a skiff, the blue fins will be present, but not the school of fish.
Transmutation
  • Head Armor can be transmuted to the Selachimorpha item skin but has no effects on land.
  • The transmuted Selachimorpha Head Armor skin has no snorkel.
  • If the transmuted Selachimorpha Head Armor skin is worn underwater, it will be replaced by the character's currently equipped Aquatic Headpiece.
  • If the Head Armor skin transmuted to Selachimorpha is worn underwater and the character has a hidden Aquatic Headpiece (including the Legendary Selachimorpha) there are no underwater effects and it appears as it would on land.

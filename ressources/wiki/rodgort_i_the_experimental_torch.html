Rodgort I: The Experimental Torch is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Rodgort's Flame, for the legendary torch, Rodgort. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Rodgort's Flame Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Rodgort I: The Experimental Torch Legendary Weapons 3
Fire is the most volatile and energetic of the elements. Consign the parts of magical beasts to the flames, and meditate on the flames that result,
as you begin your journey to attain the legendary precursor known as Rodgort's Flame.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Rodgort Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of the Bonfire
Collection
 Collected 11 Items 3

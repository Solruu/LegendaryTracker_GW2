Palm Saplings are resource nodes found in the explorable zones of Castora, the Heart of Maguuma, and the Ring of Fire. Some can also be found in Skywatch Archipelago. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Elder Wood Log (1-2) (guaranteed)
  • Foxfire Cluster
  • Hidden Trove
  • Palm Lumber Core
  • Ruined Log - if a Mithril Logging Axe or better is not used

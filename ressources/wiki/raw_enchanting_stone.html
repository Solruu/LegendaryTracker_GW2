Raw Enchanting Stone is the map-specific collectible token from Starlit Weald. Raw Enchanting Stone and Antiquated Ducat and can be converted at a 1:5 ratio with no limit:

  • Consuming 1 Raw Enchanting Stone awards 5 Antiquated Ducats.
  • 5 Antiquated Ducats can be used to purchase 1 Raw Enchanting Stone at any Castoran renown vendor.

Acquisition[edit]

Gathered from[edit]

  • Small chance to be gathered from any resource node in Starlit Weald if Resourcefulness Mastery was unlocked.
  • Aetherlocation Treasure (1-2) (chance, in Starlit Weald, depending on Prosperity Unending mastery)
  • Large Obscured Chest (3) (guaranteed, from chests in Starlit Weald, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Long-Lost Keepsake Box (3) (guaranteed, from chests in Starlit Weald, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Medium Obscured Chest (2) (guaranteed, from chests in Starlit Weald, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Seer Artifact Chest (3) (guaranteed, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Seer Storage Chest (guaranteed, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Small Obscured Chest (guaranteed, from chests in Starlit Weald, requires Obscured Riches mastery)

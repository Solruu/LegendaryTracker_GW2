Rewarded by[edit]

  • Most events reward 1-2 Curious Mursaat Ruin Shards. How much of this is from the Mursaat Ruins Delving Improvements mastery is to be determined.

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Collect coalesced Mists energy from defeated enemies and throw it into the jade construct before it deactivates (1-2)
    • Collect lightning to keep the asura turrets charged (1-2)
    • Craft bows for the survey (1-2)
    • Defeat Yagon and the captured jade construct (1-2)
    • Eliminate the foe that your warclaw located (1-2)
    • Eliminate the titanspawn that your warclaw located (1-2)
    • Escort Priory Scholar Ciana to the southern ruins (1-2)
    • Escort the jade construct through the Valley of Agony by throwing coalesced Mists energy into it (2-4)
    • Escort the Kodan Wildstalker (1-2)
    • Find and defeat the source of the Mists runoff scent (1-2)
    • Grab a rifle from the pack dolyak and prevent the bees from destroying the scanners (1-2)
    • Have your warclaw sniff around to reveal bloodstone samples. Return the samples to Priory Scholar Ciana (1-2)
    • Help the southern survey team put the ghosts to rest (1-2)
    • Keep the areas clear while the golems gather samples (1-2)
    • Keep the jade construct alive by throwing coalesced Mists energy into it as it fights the titanspawn (1-2)
    • Overload the jade construct by throwing overcharged coalesced Mists energy into it while it's at full health (1-2)
    • Program the golems and bring them to the ruins entrance (1-2)
    • Protect Ciana while she investigates the construct (1-2)
    • Stop Manikaz from completing his ritual (1-2)
    • Weaken and tranquilize Mistburned creatures for allies to research (1-2)
  • Completing the Assist those surveying Mistburned Barrens in keeping their camp safe and running (1-2).

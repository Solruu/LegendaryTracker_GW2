Refine into Bolts.

— In-game description

Silk Scraps are tier five basic crafting materials. Three scraps can be refined into a Bolt of Silk by Armorsmiths, Leatherworkers, Scribes and Tailors. They can be salvaged from level 58-80 light armor.

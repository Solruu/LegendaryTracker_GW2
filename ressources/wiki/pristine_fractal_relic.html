Pristine Fractal Relics are a currency earned by participating in Fractals of the Mists. They are stored in the Account Wallet and can be spent at BUY-2046 PFR or BLING-9009 in Mistlock Observatory.

Acquisition[edit]

The main source of Pristine Fractal Relics is the reward chests from doing the Daily Fractals achievements, which give a total of 15 per day if all dailies and recommended are done (3x4 for the daily chests, plus 3x1 for the recommended; an additional 12 can be gained by doing the challenge modes). The Fractal Initiate, Fractal Adept, Fractal Expert and Fractal Master achievements also award some Pristine relics when completed.

Notes[edit]

  • You can convert Pristine Fractal Relics to Fractal Relics by purchasing Bag of Fractal Relics from BUY-2046 PFR.

Trivia[edit]

  • The consumable functionality was added in the December 10, 2013 update.
  • Since the June 23, 2015 update Pristine Fractal Relics are no longer consumables. They are now a currency and are stored in the Wallet.

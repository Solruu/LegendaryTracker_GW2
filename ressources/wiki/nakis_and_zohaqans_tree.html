Acquisition

[edit]
  • Interact with the Lovers' Tree found on the shore of The Ruined Paths. The etching is carved into the base of the tree near the beach.
    • Starting from the  Atholma Waypoint — , head east, following the shore. The Lovers' Tree is found just south of the entrance to the Cove of Zohaqan.

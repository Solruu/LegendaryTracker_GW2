Astralaria IV: The Cosmos is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the Gift of the Cosmos for the legendary axe, Astralaria. It is the fourth step to obtain the weapon.

Achievement[edit]

Astralaria IV: The Cosmos Legendary Weapons 3
The heavens have aligned to support you in your quest! Chart their places within the heavens to discern their wisdom, and defeat the Elder Dragons and their spawn that threaten the world.
Complete these tasks, and earn the Gift of the Cosmos, the penultimate step in attaining the legendary axe known as Astralaria.
Prerequisite: Scholar of Secrets
Unlock Item: The Mechanism
Reward: Gift of the Cosmos

Notes[edit]

  • The Magnification Lens also drops from other events with an Eye of Zhaitan - see the item page for a full list of events. The gathering of the lens can be handily combined with obtaining the Ancient Planetarium Slide during the assault on the gates of Arah.
  • The Hollow Saurian Bone can also drop in Dragon's Stand
  • The Celestial Cartographer's Gear must be assembled before the steps to create the Tyrian Constellation Almanac as the Celestial Cartographer's Gear is used to create the Star Charts.
    • Stars need to be charted during the night — see Day and night for details. Dusk and dawn do not count as night.
    • Star Chart: Cauldron can be done at any time as the Fractals of the Mists are not affected by the day/night cycle.
  • The steps to create Cosmic Power can be done at any time.
  • The location to use the Celestial Cartographer's Gear at the Hidden Garden is on the second platform (the grassy rock on the way up to the Canticle of Light), above the chest to the east, not near it. You do not need to complete the jumping puzzle to obtain the chart.
  • When interacting with the Hero Point to collect item #47 Ley-Line Font Energy, having Quartz Crystals in your inventory appears to prevent the option to acquire the item, offering instead to fuse 25 crystals for a Charged Quartz Crystal. Depositing any quartz crystals to the character's Bank appears to avoid this.

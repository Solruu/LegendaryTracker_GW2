Unknown Nightmares: Gift of the Feast is a Legendary Backpack: Orrax Manifested achievement.

Achievement[edit]

Unknown Nightmares: Gift of the Feast Legendary Backpack: Orrax Manifested 5
This collection can be a useful reference for what is needed for each gift to create Orrax Manifested.
Prerequisite: Salvation's Cost
Collection
 Completed 18 Steps 5

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Trading post (buy) Trading post (sell) Notes
1 Gift of the Feast Item Crafting material — — Combine a Gift of the Mistburned Isles, a Gift of Shadows, a Gift of The Feast, and Orrax Contained in the Mystic Forge to create the legendary backpack and glider Orrax Manifested.

Created in the Mystic Forge by combining:
• 1 Gift of the Appetizer
• 1 Gift of the Entrée
• 1 Gift of the Side Course
• 1 Gift of the Dessert
2 Gift of the Appetizer Item Crafting material — — Combine a Gift of the Appetizer, a Gift of the Entrée, a Gift of the Side Course, and a Gift of the Dessert in the Mystic Forge to create the Gift of the Feast.

Created in the Mystic Forge by combining:
• 200 Bowls of Poultry Satay
• 100 Red-Lentil Saobosas
• 50 Spicy Marinated Mushrooms
• 5 Fruits of the Shadow
3 Gift of the Entrée Item Crafting material — — Combine a Gift of the Appetizer, a Gift of the Entrée, a Gift of the Side Course, and a Gift of the Dessert in the Mystic Forge to create the Gift of the Feast.

Created in the Mystic Forge by combining:
• 200 Plates of Orrian Steak Frittes
• 100 Plates of Truffle Steak
• 50 Plates of Spicy Herbed Chicken
• 5 Fruits of the Shadow
4 Gift of the Side Course Item Crafting material — — Combine a Gift of the Appetizer, a Gift of the Entrée, a Gift of the Side Course, and a Gift of the Dessert in the Mystic Forge to create the Gift of the Feast.

Created in the Mystic Forge by combining:
• 200 Meaty Asparagus Skewers
• 100 Bowls of Black Pepper Cactus Salad
• 30 Mystic Clovers
• 5 Fruits of the Shadow
5 Gift of the Dessert Item Crafting material — — Combine a Gift of the Appetizer, a Gift of the Entrée, a Gift of the Side Course, and a Gift of the Dessert in the Mystic Forge to create the Gift of the Feast.

Created in the Mystic Forge by combining:
• 200 Bowls of Prickly Pear Sorbet
• 50 Bowls of Passion Fruit Tapioca Pudding
• 2 Amalgamated Rift Essences
• 5 Fruits of the Shadow

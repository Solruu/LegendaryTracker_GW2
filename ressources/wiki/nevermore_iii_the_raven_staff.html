Nevermore III: The Raven Staff is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Raven Staff, for the legendary staff, Nevermore. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Nevermore III: The Raven Staff Legendary Weapons 3
Tyria is both a physical and a spiritual place, and its hills and forests are alive with ghosts, spirits and other ethereal creatures.
Seek the spirit of the wild raven that haunts its hills and forests. Let it lead you on its path in your quest to attain The Raven Staff, the next step in your quest to attain Nevermore.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Ravenswood Staff
Reward: Chest of the Wild Spirit
Collection
 Collected 1 Item 1
Collected 34 Items 2

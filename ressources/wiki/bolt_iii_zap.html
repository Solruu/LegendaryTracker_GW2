Bolt III: Zap is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Zap, for the legendary sword, Bolt. This collection is the last step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Bolt III: Zap Legendary Weapons 3
Brilliance strikes like a bolt from the blue, and that electric shock of imagination applies to both wielding and crafting the mightiest of weapons.
Gather the energies of Tyria to infuse them into a superior weapon in your journey to craft the legendary precursor Zap.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Sword
Reward: Chest of Energy

Notes[edit]

  • Only one Capacitive Bottle is needed for the eighteen bottle-related collection items, but a fresh Charged Quartz Crystal is needed for each Overcharged Quartz collection item (for a total of 9).
  • If you don't receive the collected item, you can manually add the Capacitive Bottle to the collection by right clicking on it and selecting "Add to Collection".
Bug:
The bottle may disappear from your inventory during the second step of the collection (capturing energy from the cooling chamber in the Thaumanova Reactor Fractal), forcing you to buy a second bottle from Grandmaster Craftsman Hobbs or the TP.

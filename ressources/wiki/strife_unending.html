Lore[edit]

Strife Unending
Prey! Prey in herds, in waves! Running and bellowing, flinging stones and beating the gates. But we are many, and they are prey. Soon, the game! We are waiting well for the loudest voice. For chasing, scaring, pouncing, killing! The game!

Graphics[edit]

Search YouTube for videos related to Strife Unending.
Hands effect
  • A glowing blue mist-flame appears on both of the player’s hands, similar to a revenant’s effect.
  • Equipping two of these accessories enhances the visual intensity of the effect.
Killstreak draw effects
  • Upon drawing a weapon, a crossed-swords (representing the engaged map icon known as OJs) appears above the player's head.
  • The appearance changes based on the player’s killstreak (enemy players in WvW and enemies in PvE).
  • Starting at tier 3, if a guild is represented, its emblem also appears as part of the draw effect.
  • At the final two tiers, the swords turn blue and a roaring Warclaw Spirit also appears. These tiers require two of these accessories to be equipped.
  • Stacks are reset when defeated, changing maps, or switching templates.

  • Tier 1 effect (0 kills)

  • Tier 2 effect (10 kills)

  • Tier 3 effect (20 kills)

  • Tier 4 effect (30 kills)

  • Tier 5 effect (40 kills)

  • Tier 1 draw effect animation (Click to view)

  • Tier 5 draw effect animation displaying the players represented guild emblem (Click to view)

Siege weapon effects
  • Flame Ram and Shield Generator placed, and Siege Golem controlled by the accessory owner, are affected by the blue mist effect, overriding their default visuals.
  • Other siege weapons are only affected visually through their projectiles.
  • Structural siege weapons are not affected.

  • Flame Ram placed by the accessory's owner

  • Shield Generator placed by the accessory's owner

  • Active Siege Golem controlled by the accessory's owner.

Legendary Armor is a category found in the Achievements category Collections.

For the Envoy Armor I: Experimental Armor achievement, this collection is unlocked by defeating any boss in the Forsaken Thicket, and contains the precursors for the raid based legendary armor.

Notes[edit]

  1. ^ Legendary Armorer: Please note that the Legendary Armorer achievement is only advanced by crafting Perfected Envoy armor from raids, not by upgrading PvP or WvW ascended armor to legendary.
  • Though the Astral collections specify Heavy armor, unlocking any armor weight of the above skins will unlock light, medium, and heavy versions.

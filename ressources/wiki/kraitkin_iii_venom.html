Kraitkin III: Venom is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Venom, for the legendary trident, Kraitkin. This collection is the third and final step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Kraitkin III: Venom Legendary Weapons 3
The krait are the monsters of the deep—vile, violent, and totally evil. Their slave-raiding parties roam the beaches, and their towers rise from the depths of the sea.
Battle these malevolent masters of the ocean in your journey to craft the legendary precursor Venom.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Trident
Reward: Chest of the Krait

Collected 21 Items 2

Notes[edit]

  • Only one Krait Focusing Crystal needs to be crafted and in your inventory to acquire all the animus trophies. It will not be consumed after each use.
  • The animus trophies will not appear in inventory as items, they immediately unlock in the collection as you obtain them.
  • The Toxic Krait Historian page can help with some of the locations. For some locations (like Sipedon Deeps), the shard is near the location where it was found for the Toxic Krait Historian, but not in the exact same place.

The Juggernaut III: The Colossus is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Colossus, for the legendary hammer, The Juggernaut. This collection is the final step needed to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

The Juggernaut III: The Colossus Legendary Weapons 3
The power of the earth is not only in its strength, but in its fluid nature, moving slowly moving as stone should not move.
Collect and bond with the oozes that live in the heart of the world to solve their mysteries and create the legendary precursor, The Colossus.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Hammer
Reward: Chest of the Ooze
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 32 Items 2

Acquisition[edit]

Gathered from[edit]

  • Small chance to be gathered from any resource node in Mistburned Barrens if Bearkin Harvesting Techniques Mastery was unlocked.
  • Grand Chest (Mistburned Barrens) (4, 6, 8) (chance, depends on amount of lane participation stacks)
  • Mursaat Obsidian (guaranteed)
  • Mursaat Ruins Cache (1-2) (chance, with 4 stacks of Lowland Expertise)

Twilight III: Dusk is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon Dusk, for the legendary greatsword Twilight. This collection is the final step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Twilight III: Dusk Legendary Weapons 3
It is always darkest before the dawn, and within that gloom lies great potential and power. Use your crafted Gloominator to pull the gloom from the land
in your journey to create the legendary precursor Dusk.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Nightsword
Reward: Chest of Gloom
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 30 Items 2

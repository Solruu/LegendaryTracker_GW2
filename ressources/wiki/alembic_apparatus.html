Notes

[edit]
  • You must first defeat at least one boss in Mythwright Gambit in order to purchase this item.
  • Using this item before finding and filling all bottles gives the error message, "You do not have enough filled jars to use this alembic."
  • Once all bottles have been found and filled, use this item to obtain the Alchemical Alembic Complete.

Trivia

[edit]
  • An alembic is an alchemical vessel used for distillation.

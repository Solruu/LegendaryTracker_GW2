Unknown Nightmares: Gift of Shadows is a Legendary Backpack: Orrax Manifested achievement.

Achievement[edit]

Unknown Nightmares: Gift of Shadows Legendary Backpack: Orrax Manifested 5
This collection can be a useful reference for what is needed for each gift to create Orrax Manifested.
Prerequisite: Salvation's Cost
Collection
 Completed 7 Steps 5

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Notes
1 Gift of Shadows Item Crafting material Combine a Gift of the Mistburned Isles, a Gift of Shadows, a Gift of The Feast, and Orrax Contained in the Mystic Forge to create the legendary backpack and glider Orrax Manifested.

Created in the Mystic Forge by combining:
• 1 Gift of Darkness
• 1 Gift of Scales
• 1 Gift of Titan Understanding
• 1 Bloodstone Shard
2 Gift of Darkness Item Trophy A gift of darkness used to create Twilight.

Purchase the recipe for this item from any Mystic Forge vendor and reach an armorsmith rating of 400 to craft.
3 Gift of Scales Item Crafting material Hint: Refined from 50 Scales, 50 Smooth Scales, 250 Large Scales, and 100 Armored Scales.
4 Gift of Titan Understanding Item Crafting material Combine a Gift of Darkness, a Gift of Scales, a gift of Titan Understanding, and a Bloodstone Shard in the Mystic Forge to create the Gift of Shadows.

A Gift of Titan Understanding can be purchased from the renown heart vendor in Mistburned Barrens or Ward Trader Sampaguita.
5 Bloodstone Shard Item Trophy Hint: Purchased from a Mystic Forge vendor for Spirit Shards.
6 Curious Mursaat Ruin Shard Item Consumable Hint: Earned by completing events in Mistburned Barrens.
7 Curious Mursaat Remnants Item Consumable Hint: Earned by completing events in Bava Nisos.

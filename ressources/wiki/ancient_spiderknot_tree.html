Ancient Spiderknot Trees are resource nodes found in Nayos.

  • Ebony Orb

Notes[edit]

  • With the original launch of Inner Nayos, all logging nodes on the map were called Ancient Spiderknot Trees with some giving Ancient Wood Logs while most gave Elder Wood Logs. With the November 28, 2023 Update, the nodes that gave Elder Wood Logs were renamed to Spiderknot Trees.

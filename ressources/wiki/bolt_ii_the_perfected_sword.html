Bolt II: The Perfected Sword is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Zap, for the legendary sword, Bolt. This collection is the second step to obtain the second tier of the precursor, Perfected Sword.

Achievement[edit]

Bolt II: The Perfected Sword Legendary Weapons 3
Your first attempt at crafting the legendary precursor, Zap, has been modestly successful, but not yet yielded the weapon you ultimately desire.
This journey has only just begun. Find a teacher, research your procedures and hone your skills before you try a second time.
Prerequisite: Magister of Legends
Unlock Item: Zap Experiment
Reward: Tricks and Tips for Advanced Sword Crafting

Blazeridge Defender is a trophy required for the Sunrise I: The Experimental Daysword legendary weapon collection.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Successfully complete any of the events related to The Steeleye Span meta event in Steeleye Span, around the  Steeleye Waypoint — .
  • The event Protect Tribune Steelgrip (43) does not award the trophy, however it is quickly followed up by a repair event which does on a success.

Endless Summer is a non-unique legendary trinket obtainable through Visions of Eternity. Up to two rings can be stored in the Legendary Armory, allowing for equipping two rings at once, one in each slot.

Lore[edit]

Endless Summer
Xuorri was cornered. At last, the centaur hunters had finally caught up with him. As his enemies lifted their swords, he simply closed his eyes and prayed, "Zintl, light my path and guide me toward the Endless Summer." In that moment, Xuorri was engulfed by a radiant light. The hylek's skin warmed under the beams, and when Xuorri opened his eyes, his pursuers writhed on the ground. They had been blinded by the Lifegiver's light, allowing Xuorri a swift escape.

Graphics[edit]

On land[edit]

Weapon stowed

Upon idle stance, a ray of sunlight illuminates the character. Additionally, a sand basin forms under the feet while crabs, sandcastles and seashells appear and disappear at random.

Anomaly:
The ray of sunlight does not spawn in some areas, such as certain caves.
Weapons drawn

Upon weapon draw, the character is engulfed in a brief splash of water. A pool of water forms under the feet and small fluorescent fish leap out of the water.

Underwater[edit]

Weapon stowed

A ray of sunlight illuminates the character. Large air bubbles form and briefly float towards the surface before disappearing.

Anomaly:
The underwater animation appears when the character is on land in underwater areas such as the Castoran grottos.
Weapons drawn

Upon weapon draw, the character is engulfed in a brief splash of mist and large air bubbles. The same fluorescent fish in the land animation can be seen swimming around the character.

Bug:
While underwater and with weapons drawn, the pool of water at the character's feet is still visible on the floor's surface.

Azabe Qabar Gravestone Rubbing is a trophy required for the Astralaria I: The Device legendary weapon collection.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Interacting with the Inscribed Casket Hero Challenge at Reliquary Vault in Cursed Shore.

Getting there

[edit]
  • Go to  Meddler's Waypoint — and head east into the cave that leads to the hero challenge. If the event is closed, a bunny hop to the upper path left of the gate will allow entry to the back (safe from the miasma bubble).

Notes

[edit]
  • This item can be safely sold or destroyed after acquisition.

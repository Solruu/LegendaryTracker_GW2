Brandstone Research is an achievement related to collecting Brandstone and helping Yasna with her research.

  • It takes 7 days to complete, as you can finish only one task per day.
  • Completing the achievement will unlock Yasna as a merchant, allowing you to purchase the recipes for Astral weapons.

Achievement[edit]

Brandstone Research Daybreak 3
Istan is being bombarded by Brandstones, which may have unique and useful properties.
Reward: Kralkatite Ore (50)
Collection
 Collected 7 Items 3

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Notes
1 Kralkatite Research Item Trophy Hint: Assist Yasna with her research in the Astralarium.
Collect 10 Kralkatite Ores and bring them to Yasna.
2 Branded Residue Research Item Trophy Hint: Assist Yasna with her research in the Astralarium.
Collect 10 Branded Residues and bring them to Yasna. They drop from any Branded creatures in PoF maps.
3 Miasma Research Item Trophy Hint: Assist Yasna with her research in the Astralarium.
Use the Particle Collector from Yasna on a Brandstone Impact Site and then activate it. You obtain Particle Collector (Filled) which you return to her.
4 Powdered Gemstone Research Item Trophy Hint: Assist Yasna with her research in the Astralarium.
Collect 10 Powdered Rose Quartz from the Brandstone Chunks from the Brandstone impact site and bring them to Yasna. Can be bought on TP.
5 Treatise on Astral Alignments Item Trophy Hint: Assist Yasna with her research in the Astralarium.
Speak to the renown heart NPC Archivist Salwa to enter the restricted archives. Take the Treatise on Astral Alignments and bring it to Yasna.
6 Mirror Calibration Readings Item Trophy Hint: Assist Yasna with her research in the Astralarium.
Complete the Defend Akili while he recalibrates mirrors around the Astralarium event at the Astralarium and return to Yasna. If your inventory is full, you will not receive the item and might have to repeat the event.
7 Blazing Kralkatite Item Trophy Bring this to Yasna in the Astralarium.
Combine 10 Kralkatite Ore and 5 Powdered Rose Quartz at the Beam of Light at the top of The Astralarium.

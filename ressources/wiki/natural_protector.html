Natural Protector is a trophy required for the Nevermore I: Ravenswood Branch legendary weapon collection.

Acquisition

[edit]

Rewarded upon completion of the Stop Mordrem from spreading corrupted tendrils in the Iron Marches event.

Waypoint
 Village of Scalecatch Waypoint —

Notes

[edit]
  • South along the road from Village of Scalecatch Waypoint.
  • The event can be forced if not currently active by doing / re-doing The Dragon's Reach: Part 1 chapter of the Living Story, Season 2, if available to the player.

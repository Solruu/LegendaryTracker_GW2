Acquisition[edit]

  • Collect 10 Powdered Rose Quartzs from the Brandstone Chunks from the Brandstone Impact Site and bring them back to Yasna.

Notes[edit]

  • If you have at least 10 Powdered Rose Quartzs in your inventory, you can talk to Yasna again right away and turn them in for achievement completion.

Lava-Boiled Water Sample is a trophy required for the Frostfang III: Tooth of Frostfang Legendary Weapons collection.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Use the Glacial Imbued Jar in the boiling froth where the lava meets the water below  Sloven Pitch Vista — , south of  Simurgh Waypoint — at the border of Sloven Pitch and Cozen Desolation in Fireheart Rise.

Notes

[edit]
  • Another collection item can be collected while in Fireheart Rise, Fireheart Living Ice from Ice Elementals. Many can be found south of  Snow Ridge Camp Waypoint — in Icespear's Shelf.

Incinerator III: Spark is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Spark, for the Legendary dagger, Incinerator. It is the final collection needed to be able to craft the precursor.

Collection flow chart. Click to enlarge.

Achievement[edit]

Incinerator III: Spark Legendary Weapons 3
Natural materials are the foundations of understanding, but creative alchemy requires creations unlike those found in the natural world.
Gather these unnatural materials in your efforts to forge the legendary precursor Spark.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Dagger
Reward: Chest of Chemistry
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 24 Items 2

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Nearest waypoint Notes
1 Perfected Dagger Item Dagger  Trader's Forum Waypoint — Crafted from a recipe received by completing the Incinerator II: The Perfected Dagger collection.
2 Incinerator Vol. 3 Item Trophy  Trader's Forum Waypoint — Purchased from Grandmaster Craftsman Hobbs after training the Historian of the Armaments ability in the Legendary Crafting Mastery Track.
3 Ever-Burning Fuel Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Use the laboratory equipment found in the Thaumanova Reactor Fractal to mix a blend of Destructive Substance and your Searing Concoction to form Ever-Burning Fuel.
4 Destructive Substance Item Trophy  Versoconjouring Waypoint — Use power from the generators in the Bauxite Alchemicals Lab in Malchor's Leap to combine Acrid Jelly and Corrosive Paste to form a Destructive Substance.
5 Acrid Jelly Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Use Cryo Dust and the Scalding Fumes from the Molten Boss Fractal to stabilize Unstable Miasma and form an Acrid Jelly.
6 Cryo Dust Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Use the Boiling-Hot Magma in the Volcanic Fractal to blend 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust with a Corrupted Ice Shard to form Cryo Dust.
7 Corrupted Ice Shard Item Trophy  Slough of Despond Waypoint — Acquire a corrupted ice shard from the chest after killing the Claw of Jormag in Frostgorge Sound.
8 Unstable Miasma Item Trophy  Slough of Despond Waypoint — Use the pressure in the depths of the ocean in the Iron Horse Mines of Frostgorge Sound to form an Unstable Miasma from a Mordrem Thrasher Gas Bladder and 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust. Note that you will need two Pieces of Mordrem Thrasher Gas Bladder to create one bladder.
9 Corrosive Paste Item Trophy  Breaktooth's Waypoint — Use the materials from the shattered waypoint in Vexa's Lab in Fireheart Rise along with Gelid Paste to congeal the Explosive Fumes into a Corrosive Paste.
10 Gelid Paste Item Trophy  Maelstrom's Waypoint — Use the heat from the Heart of the Destroyer in Mount Maelstrom to fuse 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust with an Icy Core to form Gelid Paste.
11 Icy Core Item Trophy  Krennak's Homestead Waypoint — Acquire an icy core from the chest after defeating the Svanir Shaman Chief at the Frozen Maw in Wayfarer Foothills.
12 Explosive Fumes Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Use the pressure found in the depths of the Aquatic Ruins Fractal to combine 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust with Swamp Gas to form Explosive Fumes.
13 Swamp Gas Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Acquire swamp gas from the Mossman in the Swampland Fractal.
14 Searing Concoction Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Use the equipment from Frizz's Lab in the Aetherblade Fractal to combine the Smouldering Concoction with the Combustive Substance to form a Searing Concoction.
15 Smouldering Concoction Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Use the cold environment found in the Underground Facility Fractal to safely react Flame Jelly with Caustic Paste at the ice pillar to form a Smouldering Concoction.
16 Flame Jelly Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Use the frigid energy of the Elemental Source in the Snowblind Fractal to form Flame Jelly out of a Fire Core and 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust.
17 Fire Core Item Trophy  Muridian Waypoint — Gather the fiery core of the elemental formed by the cleanup efforts in the ruins of the Thaumanova Reactor in Metrica Province.
18 Caustic Paste Item Trophy  Fort Marriner Waypoint — Form caustic paste by combining 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust with the elements from an Inquest Power Core in the low-pressure environment found at the top of the Uncategorized Fractal.
19 Inquest Power Core Item Trophy  Old Sledge Site Waypoint — Obtain an Inquest Power Core from the chest after defeating the Inquest Golem Mark II in Mount Maelstrom.
20 Combustive Substance Item Trophy  Gleaner's Cove Waypoint — Use the lasers via the console in Spekk's Lab in Caledon Forest to meld Blasting Powder with the Volatile Compound into a Combustive Substance.
21 Blasting Powder Item Trophy  Drakkar Waypoint — Use the cold environment near the Frost Portal at Drakkar Spurs in Frostgorge Sound to prevent an explosion while mixing an Ever-Burning Ember with 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust to form Blasting Powder.
22 Ever-Burning Ember Item Trophy  Gladefall Waypoint — Acquire an ever-burning ember from Drakin Cinderspire's chest in the Iron Marches.
23 Volatile Compound Item Trophy  Cuatl Waypoint — Use the low-pressure environment of Goemm's Lab high above Metrica Province to safely combine 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust with Putrid Ichor at a Console to form a Volatile Compound.
24 Putrid Ichor Item Trophy  Splintered Coast Waypoint — Acquire putrid ichor from the chest after defeating Tequatl the Sunless in Sparkfly Fen.

Rewarded by[edit]

  • Some events reward one Curious Mursaat Currency, while some more difficult events reward two. This increases to two and four, respectively, after training the Scavenger of the Wilds mastery.

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Assist Ekto's paranormal research (2)
    • Clear out the elementals (2)
    • Clear Rising Bloom's garden of Mistburned flowers (2)
    • Collect affected flora samples for Curious Remedy's research
    • Collect and turn in debris from the shore and enemies (2)
    • Collect jars, fill them with lightning, and deposit them in the drop-off locations (2)
    • Defend the captive kodan sacrifice (2)
    • Defend the kodan base from the Mistburned creatures (2)
    • Defend the lightning rods and repair them with scrap from enemies (2)
    • Follow Guiding Star and their team on the expedition (2)
    • Help Gleaning Copse clean up rotting plants and clear out rotting enemies (1-2)
    • Help the kodan gather foraged plants and animal meat (2)
    • Help the kodan prepare for their expedition by distributing supplies (2)
    • Investigate Forager's Hunt for hidden enemies (2)
    • Kill the giant crab terrorizing the shore (2)
    • Kill the leviathan (2)
    • Of Mists and Monsters (1-2)
    • Paint titan footprints and bring them to Running Colors (2)
    • Rescue the storm chaser (2)
    • Security Activated: Defeat the Champion Jade Construct (2)
  • Completing any of the following renown hearts:
    • Assist the kodan in researching the titan blight (2)
    • Help the kodan study and settle in the Janthir Syntri region (2)
    • Support the kodan as they maintain their camp (2)

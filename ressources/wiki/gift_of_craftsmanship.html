Notes[edit]

  • One Gift of Craftsmanship is required to craft each piece of PvE legendary armor. It is not used to craft WvW and PvP legendary armors.
  • Although the in-game description only says "A gift used to create legendary armor", it's also used to create legendary runes, sigils, and relics.
  • One equipped legendary set requires up to 22 gifts: 6 armor pieces, 7 runes (for armor and the breathing apparatus), 8 sigils (for all land and aquatic weapon sets), and 1 relic. This totals 1100 Provisioner Tokens.
  • For a complete Legendary Armory it would take 54 gifts: 8 sigils, 7 runes, 1 relic, and 38 armor pieces (2 weights of Eikasia gloves, 18 Envoy armor pieces & 18 Obsidian armor pieces). This is equivalent to 2700 Provisioner Tokens.

Trivia[edit]

  • The Gift of Craftsmanship used to use the same icon as the Gift of Battle but was given a unique icon with the March 19, 2024 game update.

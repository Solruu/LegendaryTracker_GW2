Terebinth Protector is a trophy required for the Nevermore I: Ravenswood Branch legendary weapon collection.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Purchase from the Veteran Heart of the Terebinth after completing Recover the Revered Terebinth from the Nightmare Court (17) at  Revered Terebinth — in Nemeton Grove, Brisban Wildlands, north of  Watchful Source Waypoint — .

Notes[edit]

  • This item becomes soulbound upon equipping it, however if equipped on a character that is not a part of the Leatherworker, Armorsmith, or Tailor disciplines it can effectively become unusable for crafting its ascended counterpart as soulbound items are not considered available for crafting purposes, even if placed in a shared space like a bank slot.

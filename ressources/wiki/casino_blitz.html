Casino Blitz is a meta event that takes place around the Free City of Amnoon in Crystal Oasis. It starts at 00.05, and every two hours after that.

Rewards[edit]

All rewards below are with successful completion of the meta only.

Guaranteed rewards
  • Crystal Oasis: Hero's Choice Chest (once per day, per account)
  • Elegy Mosaic (6-10, 16-20)
Possible rewards

Common chance:

  • Book of the Twins' Armor Recipes (0-1)
  • Book of the Twins' Weapon Recipes (0-1)
  • Recipe: The Twins' Grieving Insignia (0-1)
  • Recipe: The Twins' Grieving Inscription (0-1)
  • Chest of the Desert Specialist (0-1)
  • Intact Mosaic (0-2)

Very rare:

  • Festive Confetti Infusion
  • Tinari's Music Box (Tradable)
  • Unopened Endless Choya Piñata Tonic

Notes[edit]

  • Each round lasts 5 minutes, and progression to the next round is only awarded on successful completion of a round.
  • In each round, there is a limited amount of coins one player can find through the city: it is 40 for the 1st, 38 for the 2nd and 36 for the 3rd. Additional coins may be earned by completing the Clear out the gang of troublemakers to recover stolen coins bonus event.
    • One of the coins for the first round is located outside of the orange indicated area on the southwest edge of the Altar of the Six Gods, on the platform next to the boat and shelter.
  • This event does not scale up. You will always have to collect the same amount of coins.
  • The Choya Piñata event only spawns on successful completion of round 3.
  • It is possible to fail one round and still achieve the Choya Piñata event.
  • Both players on a Siege Turtle mount get coins

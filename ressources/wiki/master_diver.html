Master Diver

Master Diver is an achievement, requiring the player to find Sunken Chests in various parts of Tyria that are opened with an Ornate Rusted Key.

Walkthrough[edit]

Objective Map Area Nearest Point of Interest Nearest Waypoint Notes
1 Gendarran Fields Sunken Chest Looted Provern Shore  Jackdaw's Reef —  Brigantine Waypoint — North-West from Waypoint.
2 Harathi Hinterlands Sunken Chest Looted Shieldbluff Point  Koofooloo —  Shieldbluff Waypoint — North-East from Waypoint.
3 Bloodtide Coast Sunken Chest Looted Merchantman's Strait  Sullivan's Wake —  Stormbluff Waypoint — South east from Waypoint. Located in a wrecked ship NE of Merchantman's Strait midway between Stormbluff Isle waypoint and Remanda Waypoint. You can see the ship's crow's nest sticking out from the water.

Note: there are two chests in this ship; the Sunken Chest is on the seabed under the front of the wreck, while Penzan's Lost Treasure is in the middle of the wreck on a deck along with two other non-interactive chests.
4 Kessex Hills Sunken Chest Looted Viathan's Arm  Krait's Larder —  Overlake Haven Waypoint — West from Waypoint.
5 Iron Marches Sunken Chest Looted Lake Desolann  Spentlung Depth —  Brandwatch Encampment Waypoint — North-Northeast from Waypoint. Above the cave near Spentlung Depth, not inside of it.
6 Snowden Drifts Sunken Chest Looted Valslake  Moldary Point —  Valslake Waypoint — East from Waypoint in rock cave.
7 Frostgorge Sound Sunken Chest Looted Grimstone Mol  Iron Horse Mines —  Slough of Despond Waypoint — Southwest from Waypoint. Above the Iron Horse Mines point of interest, not below in the tunnels.
8 Lornar's Pass Sunken Chest Looted False Lake  Dwarves' Gloaming —  Guutra's Homestead Waypoint — West-Southwest from Waypoint.
9 Timberline Falls Sunken Chest Looted Scale Strand  Sipedon Deeps —  Coil Waypoint — West from Waypoint.
10 Mount Maelstrom Sunken Chest Looted The Mire Sea  Restless Deeps —  Irwin Isle Waypoint — North-northeast of the Waypoint.

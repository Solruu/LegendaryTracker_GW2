Ley Line Crystals are a currency earned by participating in events in Tangled Depths. They are stored in the Account Wallet and can be spent at merchants within the Tangled Depths.

Rewarded by[edit]

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Find and slay the treasure mushroom! (5)
    • Kill the chak crown! (12)
    • Kill the minor wyvern setting up shop near the SCAR team (5)
    • Rescue Zildi's Assist-o-Matic by slaying the chak lobber (12)
  • Completing the map Tangled Depths (50).
  • Rewarded for completing events in Tangled Depths.

Vendors[edit]

  • Nuhoch Mastery Vendors in Verdant Brink, Auric Basin, and Tangled Depths provide a wide variety of goods.
  • The Nuhoch Stomper/Flopper [Ley-Line Crystal Collector] that appears in the Ley-Line Confluence after a successful King of the Jungle map meta, and is identical to a Nuhoch Mastery Vendor.
  • Used to purchase Machetes, Plated weapons and Leystone armor boxes from the Whispers Keeper at the Pact Base Camp in Dragon's Stand.
  • Can be used in the appropriately upgraded Ley-Energy Matter Converter to purchase 1 container daily.
  • Trading 25 Crystals for various gizmos once per day with the Zinn's Print-o-Matic.

Contributing to the meta achievement[edit]

Incursive Investigation: Working Together 6
Work with the Mist Stranger to address a disturbance in the Fractals of the Mists and earn a legendary reward.This achievement will unlock additional Incursive Investigation achievements when certain steps are reached.
During the Fractal Incursion event, some steps of this achievement will be unlocked week by week. Afterward, there will be no limit on it.
Rewards:
  • Tier
 1:   Mists-Grasper Gloves Box
Objectives: 10 objectives in total
  • 1. Speak with the Mist Stranger to learn about disturbances in the fractals.
  • 2. Speak with gathered allies to hear the plan.
  • 3. Complete a Fractal of the Mists in quickplay mode and loot the final treasure chest.
  • 4. Bring the Agony-Torn Gloves to the Mist Stranger.
  • 5. Complete the Incursive Investigation: First Recursion achievement and wait until Fractal Incursion week one ends.
  • 6. Check in with the Mist Stranger and your allies.
  • 7. Complete the Incursive Investigation: Second Recursion achievement and wait until Fractal Incursion week two ends.
  • 8. Check in with the Mist Stranger and your allies.
  • 9. Complete the Incursive Investigation: Third Recursion achievement.
...
  • 10. Confer with the Mist Stranger about the end of the crisis.

Notes[edit]

  • Incursive Investigation: Working Together - If you find yourself stuck on step 3, be sure that when looking for a group do NOT join a group. Instead, click the button "Find a Fractal Quickplay Group (Beta)". Clicking the "Join" button next to a group is not the same thing.
  1. ^ Incursive Investigation: Working Together: The Mist Stranger can be found in the Fractal Incursion Conference instance, accessed via a gold star instance marker just to the west of the Fractal entrance in Lion's Arch.
  2. ^ Incursive Investigation: Infinite Recursion: If the amount of fractalline dust you receive (e.g. from other achievements) is higher than what you still need to complete the Infinite Recursion achievement, any remaining dust is lost instead of being carried over to the next repetition.
  3. ^ Incursive Investigation: Threat Preparation 1: Does not require partaking in any of the meta events. Just travel near to the specified waypoint. Using the actual waypoints is not required; progress is given upon moving within 750 units of the suggested locations.
  4. ^ Six Iterations of Fractalization: The title of this achievement is a reference to "Six degrees of separation", a theory by which every person in the world is connected to everyone else (or, in this case, to an ArenaNet developer) by at most 6 social connections ("friend of a friend..").

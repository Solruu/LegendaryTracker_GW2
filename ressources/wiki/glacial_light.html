Acquisition

[edit]
  • Bring the Glacial Mirror to the western edge of the plateau high above Modri Caverns. To get here, head east from the  Astralarium Waypoint — across the water, then use a springer to climb the cliffs above the pirate shacks.
    • The interaction will only appear if the player is standing on the small ledge next to the larger one, as the spot is on a cliff face.

Notes

[edit]
  • This item does not enter the player's inventory.
  • The interaction spot can be a bit buggy, try logging out to character select and back if the interaction point does not appear.

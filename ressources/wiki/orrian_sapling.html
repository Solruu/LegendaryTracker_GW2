Orrian Saplings are resource nodes found mainly in Malchor's Leap, Cursed Shore, and Siren's Landing. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Ancient Wood Log
  • Ebony Orb
  • Hidden Treasure
  • Foxfire Cluster
  • Ruined Log - if anything less than Orichalcum Logging Axe is used

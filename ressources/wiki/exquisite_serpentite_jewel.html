Acquisition[edit]

Gathered from[edit]

Three drops per day are guaranteed from the Resplendent Hidden Chest in The Hammer's Hoard.

  • Chest (Thunderhead Peaks) (1-2) (chance)
  • Chest (Thunderhead Peaks) (chance)
  • Dragon Crystal Node (chance)
  • Dragon Crystal Node (1-2) (chance, home instances)
  • Hidden Chest (Thunderhead Peaks) (chance)
  • Hidden Chest (Thunderhead Peaks) (3) (guaranteed, resplendent Hidden Chest)

Events[edit]

  • 0-3 can drop after completing the following meta events, once per day per character:
    • The Oil Floes
    • Thunderhead Keep

Dropped by[edit]

  • Rare chance to drop from foes slain in Thunderhead Peaks.

Notes[edit]

  • This item was account bound prior to January 19, 2021 patch. Any dropped jewels from before the patch remain account bound and will not stack with the tradable jewels.
  • It is possible to convert an account bound jewel to a tradeable jewel by adding it to a piece of equipment, then salvaging it off.

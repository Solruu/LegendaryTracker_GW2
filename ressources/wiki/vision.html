Vision is a legendary trinket obtainable through Living World Season 4. All six episodes must be unlocked in order to craft this item. Progress can be started by buying the Trance Stone from any Volatile Magic vendor in any LWS4 map.

Achievements[edit]

  • The following achievements are required to acquire the necessary materials for crafting:
    • Vision I: Awakening — unlocked by purchasing the Trance Stone from any Volatile Magic Collector.
    • Vision II: Farsight — unlocked by completing the previous achievement

Graphics[edit]

Primary article: Legendary trinket#Visual effects

Aurora, Coalescence and Vision — share the same visual effect, which depends on the number of these trinkets worn and displayed at the same time.

  • With weapons stowed, an ethereal aura will appear around the player.
  • With weapons drawn, four orbs or bubbles will orbit the player's head.

Walkthrough[edit]

Destroy the Inquest Excavators[edit]

When the meta starts, three Inquest Excavators will appear in the North, East, and South of Cloister of Stars.

  • All three excavators must be destroyed at the same time to progress the meta event.
  • In order to damage the excavators, players must be standing in the rotating blue field, indicated by arrow markers because the excavators are  Phase-Shifted.
  • Periodically, the excavator will become invulnerable:
    • At 75%, the excavator becomes  Shielded, several Mark IX Centurion Golems and SERVs will protect the Excavator. When they are defeated, the excavator becomes vulnerable again.
    • At 50%, the excavator becomes  Shielded, a defiance bar must be broken with crowd control effects. When the bar is broken, the excavator becomes vulnerable again, but will also gain additional abilities, including a pulsing shockwave, a tornado effect, and Magneton Destabilizers.
    • At 25%, the excavator becomes  Shielded again, Champion Mark IX Centurion Golem will spawn to protect the excavator. When the golem is defeated, the excavator becomes vulnerable again and can finally be destroyed.
  • When an excavator is destroyed, a 30 second timer will begin. The other two excavators must be destroyed within this time limit. Otherwise, it will repair itself.

Gwyllian, Seneschal of the Forest[edit]

Primary article: Gwyllian, Seneschal of the Forest

After destroying all three excavators, you will have 20 minutes to defeat Gwyllian, Seneschal of the Forest at the center of  Consecrated Piazza — .

Gwyllian has two key mechanics:

  • Bunny tranformation and orb collection — Occurs at 82/57/32/12% health
    • Random players will be inflicted with  Something Feels Funny....
    • Upon expiry, those players will get the  You've been turned into a bunny! effect and transform into a bunny.
    • Bunnies must jump to collect floating orbs in the air.
    • Once enough orbs are collected, the boss will receive a defiance bar that must be broken to continue the fight.
  • Dispel illusions with crowd control — Occurs at 75/25% health
    • Gwyllian raises the boss arena and summons mini Gwyldell illusions on it and the surrounding floating islands.
    • Most of these illusions are distractions and killing them will have no effect.
    • The only illusions that need to be dealt with are those with defiance bars. However, they should be CC'd and NOT killed.
    • Progress toward the fight is only awarded for successfully breaking a defiance bar. None is awarded for killing them.
    • One reliable way to handle this mechanic is using the Warclaw mount:
      •   Chain Pull — 150 defiance
      •   Lance — 125 defiance

50% Inquest Phase[edit]

When Gwyllian reaches 50% health, three champion Inquest will spawn at the site of each destroyed excavator from Phase 1:

  • North: Champion Biomancer Plezzi
  • East: Champion Golemancer Graxx
  • South: Champion Electromancer Dribb

These champion enemies must be defeated at the same time. Similarly to before, defeating one champion enemy will start a 30 second timer. The other two bosses must be defeated in this window, or else they will revive.

Final Phase and Loot[edit]

After defeating the Inquest, the fight resumes with no new mechanics.

At 25% health, Gwyllian raises the platform once more and the illusion event repeats.

After defeating Gwyllian, a Magnificent Chest (auto-loot) will appear under the boss arena as it lowers to the ground. Once it reaches the bottom, an additional six smaller Seneschal's Chests will spawn.

Afterward, the door to  Sylvan Cradle — will open for access to:

  • Achievement — Starlit Weald: Tread Lightly — Visit the slumbering Gwyllian after completing the Secrets of the Weald meta-event. (0)
  • Mastery insight — Starlit Weald Insight: Cloister of Stars

Speak to PTM to be teleported back to  Priory Expedition Waypoint — .

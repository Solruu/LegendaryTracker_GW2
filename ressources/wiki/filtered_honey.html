Filtered Honey can be made from Raw Honey and is an ingredient of the Vial of Healing Breath, required for the HOPE I: Research Legendary Weapons collection.

Acquisition

[edit]

Recipe

[edit]

The required Recipe: Filtered Honey can only be acquired after purchasing Raw Honey.

  • Raw Honey is purchased from Fiona Hastings, located at her apiary south of  Vale Waypoint — , on the northwest bank of the lake in Clayent Falls, Queensdale.
  • Recipe: Filtered Honey can then be purchased from Atlalli, Master Alchemist, outside the gate east of  Eztlitl Grounds Waypoint — in Timberline Falls.

This article is about the Aetheric Anchor component. For the Gift of Maguuma Mastery component, see Gift of Insights.
Warning: This item is only acquired from a vendor, NOT the mystic forge.

Combine Gift of the Survivors, a Gift of the People, a Gift of Insight, and a Gift of the Elders in the Mystic Forge to create the Aetheric Anchor.

Purchased from Magister Sirkk in Crumbling Precipice in Starlit Weald in exchange for:
• 100 Mystic Clovers
• 55 Amalgamated Draconic Lodestones
• 4 Gifts of Condensed Might
• 4 Gifts of Condensed Magic
 — In-game description

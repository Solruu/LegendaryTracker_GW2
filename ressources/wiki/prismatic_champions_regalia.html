Prismatic Champion's Regalia is a legendary trinket obtainable through the  Seasons of the Dragons meta achievement in Living World Season 2, Season 3, Season 4 and The Icebrood Saga. All episodes of the aforementioned Living World seasons must be unlocked to be able to complete the achievement that rewards this item.

Graphics[edit]

Search YouTube for videos related to Prismatic Champion's Regalia.
  • The wearer is surrounded by a shimmering faint rainbow-colored glow. They also emit star-like sparks that rapidly fade.
  • The wearer leaves behind a trail of sparks of different colors as they walk.
  • When drawing weapons, a burst of light explodes from the wearer's chest.

Notes[edit]

  • The visual effects can be disabled.
  • The fourth PvE legendary trinket, Prismatic Champion's Regalia, was released on November 30, 2021 with the final update of Living World Return.

Trivia[edit]

  • Previously there was a container shown as the achievement reward named Prismatic Champion's Inheritance which was replaced, with this amulet, before it could be acquired.

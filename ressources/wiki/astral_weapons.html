Astral weapons are crafted level 80 FExotic weapons introduced in Daybreak.

Acquisition[edit]

  • After finishing the Brandstone Research achievement, you are able to buy the recipes for 10 Kralkatite Ore each from Yasna.
    • They can be crafted by Artificers, Huntsmen, and Weaponsmiths.
    • Each weapon requires an Inscription of the Spearmarshal, 50 Kralkatite Ingots, and two T6 weapon components to craft (such as Orichalcum Greatsword Blade and Orichalcum Greatsword Hilt).

Related achievements[edit]

  • Visions of Istan: Vision of Equipment: Astral Weapons — Craft six Astral or Stellar weapons. Scholar Yasna seeks knowledge and mastery.

Notes[edit]

  • Crafting your first Astral weapon triggers the start of the Astral Purification collection.
  • A total of 8190 Kralkatite Ore and 8015 Powdered Rose Quartz is needed to craft the full weapon set.
    • 20 Kralkatite Ore and 15 Powdered Rose Quartz to complete Brandstone Research achievement.
    • 10 Kralkatite Ore per weapon recipe.
    • 10 Kralkatite Ore for Recipe: Kralkatite Ingot.
    • 500 Kralkatite Ore and 500 Powdered Rose Quartz per weapon.

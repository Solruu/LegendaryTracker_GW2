Petrified Echovald Saplings are resource nodes found in the Echovald Wilds. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • One of the following:
    • Elder Wood Log
    • Ancient Wood Log
    • Chunk of Petrified Echovald Resin
  • Chance for another Chunk of Petrified Echovald Resin independent of the above drop
  • Hidden Treasure
  • Stained Glass Shard (guaranteed) (Only if the collection To Greater Heights is active)
  • Elite specialization equipment specific item (may not drop on the first hit):
    • Intertwined Wood Band - as a Ranger, if the collection Crushing Weight is active
    • Sturdy Petrified Bark Piece - as an Engineer, if the collection Percussive Maintenance is active
    • Cold-Welded Ore Chunk - as a Thief, if the collection Possession is active

Acquisition[edit]

  • Low chance from Chests opened by characters from level 21 to 35. (Slightly above 7% per upgrade component/gemstone drop.)
  • Mystic Forge, rare reward from throwing in 4 random Pebbles, Nuggets, Marks, Lumps, or four Copper Doubloons.

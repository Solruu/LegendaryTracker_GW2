Rodgort III: Rodgort's Flame is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Rodgort's Flame, for the legendary torch, Rodgort. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Rodgort III: Rodgort's Flame Legendary Weapons 3
Fire provides both heat and light, inflicting burns against the foe and calling attention to itself through its bright, flickering nature.
Use the flames for both weal and woe as you attain the legendary precursor Rodgort's Flame.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Torch
Reward: Chest of Burning
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 31 Items 2

Notes[edit]

  • For the parts that require you to pick up a torch from a campfire, you can mount up and waypoint with the torch, but you will drop it if you enter the water or use a transformation.
    • The turtle mount is an exception: neither the pilot nor the passenger will keep the torch upon mounting up.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Trade 250 Basic Elder Wood Warhorn to a Master Huntsman after unlocking the Howler II: The Perfected Warhorn collection.

Notes

[edit]
  • Speak to a Master Huntsman to unlock the recipe to craft Basic Elder Wood Warhorn.
  • Completion of this step grants access to fulfilling the requirements of The Art of Woodworking: Horn Edition and The Art of Forging: Warhorn Mouthpiece Edition.

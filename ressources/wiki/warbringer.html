Lore[edit]

Warbringer
Warbringer is made from the salvaged remnants of enemies fallen, emblazoned with swords and spears pulled from still-warm corpses. It is a reminder of battles won and lost—whispers of victory.

Notes[edit]

  • Upon un-sheathing weapons, the two turret-like parts extend, causing the backpack to burn more intensely. Additionally, when weapons are drawn, jumping causes the effect to happen again.
  • A total of 2800 WvW Skirmish Claim Tickets are required. At the maximum rate of 365+90=455 WvW Skirmish Claim Tickets per week this will take a minimum of 7 weeks to obtain.
  • Released with the Competitive Feature Pack on June 6th, 2017.

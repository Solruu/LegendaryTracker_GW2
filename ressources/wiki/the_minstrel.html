Lore[edit]

The Minstrel
The Minstrel is a bard's dearest companion, a vessel of their spirit and artistry. Delicate notes fade in the wielder's wake, leaving passersby with a gentle whisper of their vestige.

Graphics[edit]

The Minstrel is a golden rooster harp. While stowed, faint music notes float away from the focus. While drawn, a purple aura appears and large musical notes float away from the focus.

Aura
  • White swirls can be seen around the user's arm
  • A trail of musical notes emits from the weapon
Draw/Stow effects
  • When drawn, a harp strumming sound is heard
  • Musical swirls erupts around the user
Footfalls
  • The user leaves behind a small paint like splotches and bursts of faint purple, pink and white musical notes.

Notes[edit]

  • Crafting The Minstrel in the Mystic Forge or unlocking its skin also unlocks the Musical Minstrel novelty.

Trivia[edit]

  • Additional effects were added to The Minstrel on the 20th September 2016 update.
  • With the 24th July 2018 update, players who have unlocked the Minstrel's skin are able to purchase the Musical Minstrel novelty from Sarettokk.
  • The Minstrel was a joke profession announced on the Guild Wars 2 main site, before shattering into a picture of the truly announced Mesmer.
  • Inside the breast of The Minstrel is a feather.

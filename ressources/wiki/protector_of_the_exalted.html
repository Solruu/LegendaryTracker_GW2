Protector of the Exalted is a trophy required for the Nevermore I: Ravenswood Branch legendary weapon collection.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Defeat the Legendary Vinetooth Prime in Auric Basin.
Waypoint
 Eastwatch Waypoint —

Notes

[edit]
  • Northeast of Eastwatch Waypoint at Vinetooth Den point of interest.
  • It only appears after activating the three Eastwatch pylons during the Ruins of Eastwatch event. There is a ten minute time limit to defeat it.

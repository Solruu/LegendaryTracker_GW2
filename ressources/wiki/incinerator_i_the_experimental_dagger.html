Incinerator I: The Experimental Dagger is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Spark, for the legendary dagger, Incinerator. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Spark Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Incinerator I: The Experimental Dagger Legendary Weapons 3
From the heart of the furnace, the elements dance and caper at your command! Many rare and unusual elements are needed for your journey,
and you must capture and comprehend them all in order to take your first steps in creating the legendary precursor Spark.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Incinerator Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Industry
Collection
 Collected 18 Items 3

Fir Saplings are resource nodes found in level 30-60 areas, specifically in Dredgehaunt Cliffs, as well as specific sub-regions of Lornar's Pass and Timberline Falls. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Seasoned Wood Log (1-2) (guaranteed)
  • Hidden Cache
  • Walnut (1-2)
  • Ruined Log (guaranteed) - if a Steel Logging Axe or better is not used

Notes[edit]

  • A good farming run of Fir Saplings is run a circuit in Dredgehaunt Cliffs from Steelbrachen south through Heimdahl's Last Stand, east to Frostland Melt, follow the road north to Graupel Kohn and then south to Toran Hollow, then cut north past Wyrmblood Lake to Leopard's Snarl Shrine.

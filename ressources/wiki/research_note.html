Research Notes are a currency that also can be used as a crafting material, earned by salvaging crafted items with a Research Kit, or by toggling the option to automatically research crafted items in the crafting interface. They are automatically stored in the wallet upon acquisition.

Acquisition[edit]

Automatically salvage option.

Salvaged from[edit]

Using a Research Kit to salvage any item with the Research salvage type (shown as "Salvage: Research"). The amount of research notes depends on the level and rarity of the items. Salvage research can be found here, or see the external links for an overview of the most efficient items to craft.

Note: Stat changed items can no longer be salvaged for research notes.

Crafting[edit]

  • Toggle the option to automatically salvage the crafted item. Despite the wording, this does not require a research kit.
  • Acquired directly from crafting Peer Reviewed Research.

Contained in[edit]

Currency for[edit]

  • Currency for the following vendors:
    • Astral Ward Mage (relics)
    • Deft Lahar for Polychromatic armor boxes
    • Guest Elder
    • Keycard Exchange Service
    • Leivas
    • Myung-Hee
    • Repurposed Jade Servitor
    • Requisitions Specialist
    • Trader
    • Xunlai Jade Sales Associate
    • Zazzl and Archaeologist Vorri
  • Commonly used to buy Hydrocatalytic Reagents (at 50  per 10) for Gift of Research.

Notes[edit]

  • Since the July 7, 2022 game update, Research Notes go directly into the wallet. The previously earned notes can be double-clicked to add to the wallet.
  • Since the October 4, 2022 game update, Research Notes can be directly crafted via a checkbox in the crafting interface. This method does not require or consume Research Kits.
  • The February 24, 2026 game update updated stat changing to produce alternative item variants that couldn't be salvaged for notes.

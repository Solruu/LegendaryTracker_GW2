Acquisition

[edit]
  • Give 250 Basic Mithril Swords (1500 Mithril Ingots) to a Master Weaponsmith after unlocking the Bolt II: The Perfected Sword collection.

Notes

[edit]
  • Speak to a Master Weaponsmith to unlock the recipe to craft Basic Mithril Sword.
  • Completion of this step grants access to fulfilling the requirements of The Art of Forging: Sword Blade Edition and The Art of Forging: Sword Hilt Edition.

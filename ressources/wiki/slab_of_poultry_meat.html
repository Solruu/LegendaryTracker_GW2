Notes[edit]

  • Ascended Coq Au Vin grants the same stats as Plate of Truffle Steak (+100 Power, +70 Precision).
  • Ascended Poultry Aspic grants the same stats as Bowl of Nopalitos Sauté (+100 Concentration, +70 Toughness).
  • Item drops for all characters on the account, not just trained chefs.
    • However, it only counts towards the Gourmet Training achievement when acquired as loot on Diessa Plateau by a trained chef character.
    • The drop rate is dependent on magic find as well.
    • Easiest way to acquire Slabs of Poultry Meat for Gourmet Training is to hunt Crimson Moa just north-east of the Village of Butcher's Block around  Bloodcliff Waypoint — , there are packs that re-spawn quickly there.
  • Can be fed to a cat in Queensdale and two cats in The Grove as a part of the Hungry cat scavenger hunt.

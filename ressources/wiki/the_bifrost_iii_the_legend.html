The Bifrost III: The Legend is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Legend, for the legendary staff, The Bifrost. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

The Bifrost III: The Legend Legendary Weapons 3
Light shines upon the world, creating the colors around us. Study and create the color palette of the world using artistry and light.
Channel this rainbow into your efforts to craft the legendary precursor The Legend.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Staff
Reward: Chest of Rainbows

 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 32 Items 2

Notes[edit]

  • Players can see the scenic vantage points before getting to this tier.
  • Players can't have any Quartz Crystals in your inventory at the Metal Forest hero point. Otherwise it won't offer you the options hold the crystals up.
    • If you have more than 25 Quartz Crystals in your inventory, making a Charged Quartz Crystal will then allow you to hold crystals up.
  • Crafting the 6 different Jars of Paint requires:
    • 6 Jar of Paint Bases
    • 2350 Copper Ore
    • 2100 Iron Ore
    • 210 Lump of Coals
    • 210 Thermocatalytic Reagents
    • 100 Crystal Cores
    • 5 Carnelian Lumps
    • 30 Pouch of Red Pigments
    • 30 Pouch of Orange Pigments
    • 30 Pouch of Yellow Pigments
    • 30 Pouch of Green Pigments
    • 30 Pouch of Blue Pigments
    • 30 Pouch of Purple Pigments
    • Account-bound drops from different colored Oozes
    • Account-bound drops from gathering (non-Rich) Iron nodes and Coral nodes

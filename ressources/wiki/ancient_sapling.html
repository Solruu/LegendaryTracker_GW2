Ancient Saplings are resource nodes found in level 75-80 areas. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Ancient Wood Log (guaranteed)
  • Foxfire Cluster
  • Ebony Orb
  • Hidden Treasure
  • Krytan Lumber Core (In Kryta)
  • Palm Lumber Core (In the Heart of Maguuma)
  • Ruined Log (guaranteed) - if an Orichalcum Logging Axe is not used
  • Tamarisk Reed (In Desert Highlands)
  • Chunk of Petrified Echovald Resin (Rare chance from maps in Cantha region)

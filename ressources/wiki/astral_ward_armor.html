Astral Ward armor refers to a group of armor sets that can be acquired in the Secrets of the Obscure expansion, thematically tied to its story[1]. Completing the Astral Ward armor set is a required part of the path towards the legendary Obsidian armor. There is a collection achievement for gathering the entire set.

Acquisition[edit]

  • Purchased from Astral Ward Mage (Outfitter) or Lyhr in The Wizard's Tower
    • The Astral Craft mastery is required before the items can be purchased.
    • Unlocking the skin of one weight will also unlock it on the remaining two, therefore it is only necessary to get 6 armor pieces to unlock the full set of 18 skins.
    • Total cost for one full set to unlock all the skins and collection achievement steps:
      • 6 Purified Rift Essences
        • Crafted or purchased using the following materials in total:
          • 6 Mystic Clovers
          • 300 Globs of Ectoplasm
          • 1,500 Fine Rift Essence
          • 600 Masterwork Rift Essence
          • 300 Rare Rift Essence
        • If purchased, an additional 60-120 Globs of Ectoplasm are needed
      • 6 Pouches of Stardust
      • 6 Cases of Captured Lightning
      • 3,000 Research Notes
      • 1 Gift of Bones
      • 1 Gift of Claws
      • 1 Gift of Dust
      • 1 Gift of Fangs
      • 1 Gift of Scales
      • 1 Gift of Venom

Related achievements[edit]

  • Horn of Maguuma: Astral Ward Armor — Purchase the Astral Ward Armor set from the Wizard's Tower Outfitter. (10)
  • Legendary Armor: Astral Bearing — Gather the necessary armor skins and defeat Galene the Seething to unlock the ability to purchase the Arcanum of Astral Bearing from the Lyhr, the wizard smith. (3)
  • Legendary Armor: Astral Footprints — Gather the necessary armor skins and defeat Myros the Spiteful to unlock the ability to purchase the Arcanum of Astral Footprints from the Wizard's Tower. (3)
  • Legendary Armor: Astral Grasp — Gather the necessary armor skins and defeat Pherus the Subjugator to unlock the ability to purchase the Arcanum of Astral Grasp from the Wizard's Tower. (3)
  • Legendary Armor: Astral Heartbeat — Gather the necessary armor skins and defeat Nourys, the Eyes of the Abyss to unlock the ability to purchase Arcanum of Astral Heartbeat from the Wizard's Tower. (3)
  • Legendary Armor: Astral Stride — Gather the necessary armor skins and defeat Knaebelag the Terror to unlock the ability to purchase the Arcanum of Astral Stride from Lyhr, the wizard smith. (3)
  • Legendary Armor: Astral Thought — Gather the necessary armor skins and defeat Ignaxious to unlock the ability to purchase the Arcanum of Astral Thought from the Wizard's Tower. (3)

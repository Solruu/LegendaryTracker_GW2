Contained in[edit]

  • Some of the loot boxes awarded by PvP reward tracks contain high amounts of Dragonite Ore:
    • The Balthazar Back Item Reward Track has 4 champion boxes giving 25 Dragonite Ore each. The final Balthazar Backpack Box gives 50 ore.
    • The Glorious Armor Box from the Glorious Reward track gives 50.
    • Each champion box in the region tracks give progressively more Dragonite Ore, starting at 10, 15, then 25. The final weapon skin box gives 50.

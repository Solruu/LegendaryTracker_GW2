Ice Elemental Residue is a trophy required for the Frostfang III: Tooth of Frostfang Legendary Weapons collection.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Use the Glacial Imbued Jar at the Glorious Chest after defeating the Champion Svanir Shaman Chief world boss in Frusenfell Creek, Wayfarer Foothills, near  Vendrake's Homestead Waypoint — .
    • Requires [Group Event] Kill the Svanir shaman chief to break his control over the ice elemental (10) at the end of The Frozen Maw meta event chain, which occurs every 2 hours.

Notes

[edit]
  • Another collection item can be collected while in Wayfarer Foothills, Wayfarer's Living Ice from Ice Elementals. Note that the Icebrood Elementals summoned during the meta event are considered a different type.

Visions of Dragonfall is a Legendary Trinkets achievement that is a part of the Vision I: Awakening collection.

Notes[edit]

  • This collection used to require the acquisition of the Corsair Jerkin. This was fixed with the patch on June 03, 2019, players who have completed the previous requirement for this achievement will still retain their progress.

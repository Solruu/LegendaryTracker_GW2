“

An amalgamation of rift essences used for crafting that have been purified by magic...and more than a little luck.

Hint: Refined from an Amalgamated Essence and a Mystic Clover.

— In-game description

Purified Rift Essence is an Gascended crafting material obtained by crafting. It is used as crafting material for the legendary ring Endless Summer as well as a currency to purchase pieces of the Astral Ward armor and the Oneiros-Spun armor.

It can be reverted back to an amalgamated rift essence and a mystic clover by purchasing a Box of Purified Rift Essence Components from Rift Researcher Adam in Lowland Shore (requires Guild Wars 2: Janthir Wilds).

Banyan Saplings are resource nodes found in level 45-70 areas. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Hard Wood Log (1-2) (guaranteed)
  • Hidden Hoard
  • Coastal Lumber Core
  • Ruined Log (guaranteed) (If a Darksteel Logging Axe or better is not used)

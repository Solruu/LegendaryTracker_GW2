Refined Envoy armor is an ascended armor set which serves as the precursor for crafting the Legendary Perfected Envoy armor sets.

One weight set can be selected from the Chest of Refined Envoy Armor obtained after finishing the Envoy Armor II: Refined Armor achievement.

Acquisition[edit]

  • Chest of Heavy Refined Envoy Armor
  • Chest of Medium Refined Envoy Armor
  • Chest of Light Refined Envoy Armor
  • Further pieces of any weight can be acquired by crafting from a recipe bought at the corresponding Master craftsman after Envoy Armor II: Refined Armor has been completed.

Notes[edit]

  • It is recommended that you do not attempt to stat change your precursor item with the standard stat swap recipe in the Mystic Forge. While you will be provided with an item with the desired stats, it will have been replaced with a generic ascended item that cannot be used as a legendary precursor.

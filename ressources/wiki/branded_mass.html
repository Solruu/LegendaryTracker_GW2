Notes[edit]

  • Each account is able to harvest 25 nodes per day.
  • Each Dragonsblood weapon costs 60 Branded Mass to craft. (50 per inscription and 10 additional as part of the recipe.)
    • A minimum of 960 Branded Mass is required to complete Journeyman of the Forge.
  • Each Heroic Dragonsblood weapon costs 150 Branded Mass to craft. (100 to unlock the collection and 50 per inscription.)
    • A minimum of 2400 Branded Mass is required to obtain all Heroic Dragonsblood weapons, after crafting the normal Dragonsblood weapons.

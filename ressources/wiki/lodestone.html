Lodestones are tier 5 rare crafting materials.

  • Pure Lodestones
    • Charged Lodestone - Drops from non-corrupted air creatures
    • Evergreen Lodestone - Drops from non-corrupted plant creatures
    • Glacial Lodestone - Drops from non-corrupted ice creatures
    • Molten Lodestone - Drops from non-corrupted fire creatures
    • Onyx Lodestone - Drops from non-corrupted earth creatures
  • Corrupted Lodestones
    • Corrupted Lodestone - Drops from some icebrood corrupted creatures
    • Crystal Lodestone - Drops from some branded corrupted creatures
    • Destroyer Lodestone - Drops from some destroyer corrupted creatures
    • Mordrem Lodestone - Drops from some mordrem corrupted creatures
  • Resonating Lodestone
  • Amalgamated Draconic Lodestone
  • Unidentified Lodestone is related to the Conservation of Magic achievement.

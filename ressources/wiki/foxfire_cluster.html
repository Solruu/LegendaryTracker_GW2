Notes[edit]

  • Mysterious Vine, Cultivated Vine, and Mawdrey each require 48 Foxfire Clusters in their creation: 20 for the infused pot and 28 for 7 Plates of Piquant Plant Food. Overall, 144 Foxfire Clusters are required to create Mawdrey.

Trivia[edit]

  • Foxfire is the bioluminescence created by certain kinds of fungi on decaying wood.

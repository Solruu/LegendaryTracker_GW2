Notes[edit]

  • The flame blast cannot critically hit.
  • The flame blast has a 0.85 damage coefficient and uses the unequipped (690.5) weapon strength value in damage calculations.
  • The effect originates at the target with a range of 240 and affects up to 5 foes.
  • The flame blast does not inflict burning
  • Each foe hit by the flame blast will add adrenaline to the Warrior’s resource pool.

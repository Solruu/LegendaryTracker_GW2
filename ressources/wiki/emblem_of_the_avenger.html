Notes[edit]

  • The original purpose of this item was to upgrade the Slumbering Conflux ring (no legendary visuals) into Conflux (with legendary visuals). The introduction of the toggle checkbox to switch off the effects on Conflux with the July 13, 2021 (Legendary Armory) release removed the acquisition method for Slumbering Conflux, and used the Historical Conflux recipe.
  • As originally written in the Spring 2023 Studio newsletter, a new use for this item was introduced later in 2023 with the June 6, 2023 game update, specifically the addition of stat-swap recipes for the WvW infusion, and as a vendor currency.

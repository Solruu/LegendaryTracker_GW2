Legendary Relic is a type of legendary equipment that, once equipped, has the power to take on the properties of any other relic. It was introduced in The Realm of Dreams release of the Guild Wars 2: Secrets of the Obscure expansion cycle on February 27th, 2024.

Lore

Legendary Relic
"Mmm... This relic is compact. Extremely versatile as well. Should these find their way into too many hands, I will be looking for a new line of business."—Archivist Ikur

Mechanics

The Legendary Relic differs from other legendary equipment as it won't automatically include the functionality of newer relics added in future expansions. Instead, players will have to unlock each relic before being able to select it as an option in the legendary item.[1] The Legendary Relic has no visual effects.

With the exception of the core relics (which are all automatically unlocked for use) and Secrets of the Obscure relics (automatically unlocked with the expansion obtained), all other relics have to be obtained through normal means prior to them becoming available through the selection window of the Legendary Relic. Practically, this means any relic which has been unlocked as part of a relic collection will be available to the player, even if it has subsequently been deleted.

Unlock requirements

Automatically unlocked for use within the legendary relic:

  • Rare Collections: Relics—Core Set 1 — Bind Core Set 1 relics by crafting them or purchasing them from the Trading Post. Note: Relics from the Level-80 Boost will not count toward progress. (10)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Core Set 2 — Bind 12 Core Set 2 relics to your account by crafting them or purchasing them from the Trading Post. (5)

Automatically unlocked with Guild Wars 2: Secrets of the Obscure:

  • Rare Collections: Relics—Secrets of the Obscure Set 1 — Collect 12 relics from Secrets of the Obscure Relics Set 1. (10)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Secrets of the Obscure Set 2 — Collect 6 relics from Secrets of the Obscure Relics Set 2. (5)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Secrets of the Obscure Set 3 — Collect 6 relics from Secrets of the Obscure Relics Set 3. (5)

Requires credit from the associated relic collection:

  • Rare Collections: Relics—Janthir Wilds Set 1 — Collect 6 relics from Janthir Wilds Relics Set 1 from achievements or from the Janthir Wilds Reward Track. (5)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Janthir Wilds Set 2 — Collect 6 Janthir Wilds Set 2 relics by completing achievements or from the Janthir Wilds reward track. (5)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Janthir Wilds Set 3 — Collect 6 Janthir Wilds Set 3 relics by completing achievements or from the Janthir Wilds reward track. (5)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Visions of Eternity Set 1 — Collect 6 relics from Visions of Eternity Relics Set 1 from achievements or from the Visions of Eternity Reward Track. (5)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Visions of Eternity Set 2 — Collect 6 relics from Visions of Eternity Relics Set 2 from achievements, relic vendors, or from the Visions of Eternity Reward Track. (5)
  • Rare Collections: Relics—Visions of Eternity Set 3 — Collect 6 relics from Visions of Eternity Relics Set 3 from achievements or relic vendors or from the Visions of Eternity reward track. (5)
Anomalies:
  • Although they also can be acquired from Guild Wars 2: Janthir Wilds and used as their FExotic variant, some relics such as Relic of the Fractal require Guild Wars 2: Secrets of the Obscure to be used in the Legendary Relic.
  • Relics—Visions of Eternity Set 2 can be acquired and used as their FExotic variants regardless of expansion ownership, but require Guild Wars 2: Visions of Eternity to be used in the Legendary Relic.

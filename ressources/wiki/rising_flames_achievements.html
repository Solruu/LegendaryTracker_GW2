Notes[edit]

  1. ^ "Rising Flames" Mastery: The Patron used to be in the Rising Flames Story Journal achievement category, but still contributes towards the meta achievement.
  2. ^ Collecting Coalescence: Progress is only obtained from the corresponding ley-line pre-event Defeat the dragon minions before they absorb too much ley-line magic (80), and not from the succeeding coalescence event itself.
  3. ^ Hungry Hal: Feed Hal the Hermit 50 Ember Apples. There are at least 14 Ember Apple trees around Osprey Pillars: 7 within  Osprey Aeries — , and 7 on the island north of  Barrier Spires — . Each tree resets after 24 hours per character, so it is possible to use multiple characters to complete the achievement in a single day. For easy access to Hal, complete the renown heart Win the favor of the circus (80) at Osprey Pillars then speak with Ringmaster Ivy near  Castaway Circus Waypoint — for a quick teleport up to the side of the volcano, and glide northwest down to Hal. The bundle is not removed upon waypointing.
  4. ^ Skrilla's Benefactor: Only the dialogue option A gold for a good cause. gives progress.
  5. ^ Walking on Fire: Eligibility can be tracked with the  Qualify for the Floor Is Lava? effect. The tunnel begins southwest of  Crumbling Trail Waypoint — in the pool of lava where Vermignus spawns. You can touch the lava as long as you don't die. The Skyscale trivializes this achievement.

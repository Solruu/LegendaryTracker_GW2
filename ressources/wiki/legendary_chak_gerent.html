Legendary Chak Gerents are powerful chak which spawn during the King of the Jungle meta-event in each of the four lanes around Ley Line Confluence in Tangled Depths. Killing all four of the Chak Gerents during King of the Jungle is required for the successful completion of the meta-event.

The Gerents are called the Chak's 'defensive measure' against the four cannons which are siphoning ley line energy for power.

Skills
  • Lunge — The creature charges towards a target, dealing a large amount of damage and will  knock targets down for 3-5 seconds.
  • Pheromone Clouds — The creature leaves a blue, donut-shaped AoE. While placed, the range expands and deals a high amount of damage to targets within.
  • Swipe — The creature swipes with its left claw, dealing a large amount of damage and will  knock targets down for 3-5 seconds.
  • Tail Slam — The creature alternates slamming its tail behind it left and right,  knocking down enemies caught by the impact for 3-5 seconds. [verification requested]
    • Cave In — Rubble falls from the ceiling as multiple AoEs, inflicting massive damage and  knocking down enemies for 3-5 seconds.
  • Toxic Gas — Spreads poisonous gas in an area as it retreats underground, forcing players to move on to a clearer section.
  • The creature moves around its target in an evasive maneuver, evading attacks for 1-2 seconds.1
1 Note: This is not an offensive skill, but rather a defensive one. As a result, the name of the skill is unknown.

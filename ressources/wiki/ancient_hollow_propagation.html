Acquisition

[edit]

Plant a Fire Orchid Seed in the fertile soil southwest of the Ancient Hollow Waypoint.

Location

[edit]
  • From  Ancient Hollow Waypoint — walk south-west until you reach a root with a Bouncing Mushroom. Take a series of 4 Bouncing Mushrooms into an Updraft. Out of the Updraft, head west into a cave with Speed Boost Mushrooms and occasionally a Treasure Mushroom. On the western edge of the cave you will find the fertile soil.
  • Alternatively you can utilize the Ley Line to the south up to the mushroom cave and Skyscale or Oakheart's Reach/glide back around, following the second image to the right.

Notes

[edit]
  • After planting the seed, a Primordial Orchid will be available for daily harvest on the fertile soil.
  • From the patch of fertile soil, you have easy Gliding access to the Wind Rider Ledge Propagation.

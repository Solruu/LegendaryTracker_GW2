Mebahya Saplings are resource nodes found across the Crystal Desert. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Elder Wood Log (1) (guaranteed)
  • Foxfire Cluster
  • Ruined Log (guaranteed) (If a Mithril Logging Axe or better is not used)
  • Tamarisk Reed (In Desert Highlands)

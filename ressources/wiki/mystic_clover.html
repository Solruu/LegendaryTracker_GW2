Acquisition[edit]

Overview[edit]

Mystic Clovers can be acquired through multiple methods:

  • Mystic Forge — Forged directly using Mystic Coins, Globs of Ectoplasm, Obsidian Shards, and Spirit Shards (for Philosopher's Stone or Mystic Crystal).
    • Also available for 3  + 5  + 3  + 3  from Miyani/Mystic Forge Attendants (combined weekly limit of 10), and Lyhr/Ward Crafter Lucirae (unlimited).
  • Reward tracks — Earned from PvP Reward Tracks, WvW Reward Tracks, and Skirmish reward track (through Mist-Warped Bundle).
  • Vendors — Purchased weekly for 2 Mystic Coins, 2 Globs of Ectoplasm, and 2 Spirit Shards per Mystic Clover, plus an additional currency depending on the vendor:
    • Fractals of the Mists: 150  from BUY-4373 (limit 10 per week).
    • Raids: 30  from Manfred Njallson (limit 15 per week).
    • PvP: Emblem of Tournament Victory or 5  from League Vendor (combined limit 5 per week).
    • WvW: 10  + 20  from Dugan (limit 5 per week; does not require Spirit Shard).
    • Lunar New Year: 10 Exotic Essence of Luck from New Year Rewards (limit 1 per week; does not require other items).
  • Wizard's Vault — Purchased for 60  each (limit 20 per season).
  • Unbound Magic — Very low chance from Magic-Warped Bundle.
  • Achievements
    • Glory to the Legions — Similar to repeatable Reward Tracks, the final chest of each achievement (reward tier 20) contains 2 Mystic Clovers.
    • Chest of Legendary Crafting Materials rewarded from five "Strike Back" achievements, containing a total of 35 Mystic Clovers (non-repeatable).

The recipes only have a chance to produce Mystic Clovers, estimated through drop research to be about 31%.[1] The rest of the time, a weighted[2] random item of the following is given instead (quantities listed are for the 1 clover recipe; the 10 clover recipe will yield ten times as many):

  • 1-3 of a tier 6 refined basic crafting material:
    • Ancient Wood Plank, Bolt of Gossamer, Cured Hardened Leather Square, or Orichalcum Ingot
  • 2-4 of a tier 6 fine crafting material:
    • Ancient Bone, Armored Scale, Elaborate Totem, Pile of Crystalline Dust, Powerful Venom Sac, Vial of Powerful Blood, Vicious Claw, or Vicious Fang
  • 1 of a select tier 5 rare crafting material:
    • Corrupted Lodestone, Crystal Lodestone, Destroyer Lodestone, or Pile of Putrid Essence
  • 2 Globs of Ectoplasm
  • 5 Mystic Coins
  • 2 Mystic Crystals
  • 2 Obsidian Shards
  • 1 Hidden Treasure

See Mystic Clover#Notes for more information about averages and probabilities.

Sold by[edit]

Note that vendors with identical costs share the weekly limit. An exception is Lyhr and Ward Crafter Lucirae, who do not have a weekly limit, and buying from them will not count against the weekly limits of Miyani and the Mystic Forge Attendants even though their costs are the same. Additionally, League Vendor's two offers have a combined limit despite using different currencies.

Notes[edit]

  • Drop research for clover recipes can be found here.
  • Assuming that both recipes have the same chance to produce clover, the average cost to obtain a single clover:
    • 3.2 Mystic Coins
    • 3.2 Obsidian Shards
    • 3.2 Globs of Ectoplasm
    • 1.94 spirit shards
  • The above calculation does not take into account any drops of these items you might receive from 'failed' attempts.
  • A Gift of Fortune or Mystic Tribute will require 154  + 154  + 154  if the 77 Mystic Clovers are exclusively purchased from weekly vendors.
    • Additionally depending on the vendor, it will cost 11,550 , 2,310 , or 2,310 .

Probability notes[edit]

Both Mystic Forge recipes have technically identical costs. Drop rate research suggests that both recipes have around 30-32% of yielding clovers, and drop the same materials. Keep in mind though that the 1-clover recipe is more likely to produce average odds on the way to crafting any particular piece of legendary gear. (See the talk page for details and discussion.) This does not mean the 1-clover recipe will give you more clovers, just that you are more likely to end up with an amount close to what would statistically be expected due to lower variance.

Roughly speaking, the 10x recipe is faster and less tedious, while the 1x recipe is more reliable, when working on 70-80 clovers. Remember though that when using the 10x recipe, it is always statistically cheaper to produce amounts below 10 (e.g. from 70 to 77 clovers) with the 1x recipe.

Tukawa Saplings are resource nodes found in level 30-60 areas. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Seasoned Wood Log (1-3) (guaranteed)
  • Hidden Cache
  • Walnut (1-2)
  • Ruined Log (guaranteed) - if a Steel Logging Axe or better is not used

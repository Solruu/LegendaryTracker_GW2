Twilight is a legendary greatsword crafted in the Mystic Forge. It can be combined with its counterpart Sunrise to create Eternity.

Unlocking the weapon in the Legendary Armory will grant the player a Memory of Twilight.

Lore[edit]

Twilight
While the other gods slept, Grenth took up his throne—beckoning his companions to dance and howl beneath the stars. It was said that his powers were most potent when the moon was highest in the sky.

Graphics[edit]

Twilight is a demonic greatsword whose blade can best be described as a window into space. The blade's flats are a painterly design whose texture shifts and rotates. Moving or swinging the blade leaves a painterly afterimage that also acts as a window into space. While drawn, the blade is surrounded with a soft aura that reflects its color; and white sparkles pop off the blade.

Aura
  • The user's body is surrounded by darkness.
  • Small dark orbs rise from the user's body.
Draw/Stow effects
  • When drawn, a burst of darkness emanates from the user's body.
Footfalls
  • The user leaves behind painterly footprints that act as a window into space.
Skill effects
  • Ranged skills will throw Twilight instead of the default projectile. This effect changes the following skills:
    • Guardian:   Whirling Wrath and   Binding Blade.
      • Dragonhunter:   Spear of Justice.
    • Warrior:   Bladetrail and   Throw Rock (downed skill).
    • Ranger:   Throw Dirt (downed skill) .
      • Soulbeast: any projectile from Soulbeast pet skills such as   Narcotic Spores.
    • Mesmer:   Mirror Blade.
    • Revenant:   Essence Sap (downed skill).
      • Renegade:   Citadel Bombardment.
    • Necromancer:
      • Harbinger Shroud:   Tainted Bolts and   Dark Barrage.
      • Ritualist:   Essence Blast

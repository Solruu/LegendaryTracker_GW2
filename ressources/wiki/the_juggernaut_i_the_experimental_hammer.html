The Juggernaut I: The Experimental Hammer is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Colossus, for the legendary hammer, The Juggernaut. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, The Colossus Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

The Juggernaut I: The Experimental Hammer Legendary Weapons 3
The wonders of the stone and soil, carved by a nature and by sentient races, exude both power and stability.
Meditate on these great places in order to take your first steps in created the legendary precursor known as The Colossus.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: The Juggernaut Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of the Colossal
Collection
 Collected 32 Items 3

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Plant 3 Fire Orchid Seeds in the Fertile Soil in the Temple of Melandru at the Cathedral of Verdance in Cursed Shore.
    • From the  Verdance Waypoint — , walk up the ramps to the Altar of the Withered Heart. The fertile soil can be found next to the point of interest.

Notes

[edit]
  • After planting the seeds, 3 Primordial Orchids will be available for daily harvest at the fertile soil patch.

Stella Radians is a non-unique legendary trinket obtainable through Visions of Eternity. Up to two accessories can be stored in the Legendary Armory, allowing for equipping two accessories at once, one in each slot.

Lore[edit]

Stella Radians
"Congratulations, candidate. Of the many, there are but few worthy enough to undergo the Divitium Nava. Who else could be at the forefront but you? It is with great honor and excitement we confer unto you this star. Let those who behold your countenance marvel at how brightly you shine!"

Graphics[edit]

  • Shares the same visual effect used by Divitium Nava Candidate during the Divitium Nava story.
  • A circular crown-shaped effect appears behind the player's head.
  • Drawing a weapon triggers a radiant flash of rainbow-colored light.
  • Equipping two of these accessories does not alter or enhance the effect.

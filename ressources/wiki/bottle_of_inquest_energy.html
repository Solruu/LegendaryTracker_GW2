Acquisition

[edit]
  • Use the Capacitive Bottle, applying the Primed Bottle effect. Approach an Inquest Technician that uses air skills and get hit by their Shocking Stab skill.
    • Technicians that use air skills have "Stuns" listed under their abilities.

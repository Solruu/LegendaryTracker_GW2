Acquisition

[edit]
  • Starting from the  Atholma Waypoint — , go southeast and use your Springer to get atop a large rock; once there, you will see a Sand Portal, enter it and it will teleport you onto the main Inquest platform. Make your way through the structure, until you reach a hexagon shaped building. Use your Springer once again to scale it; a Sand Portal will be located inside. Go through the portal and you will end up in a room guarded by either an Assault Cube or a Detonation Cube. The Olmakhan Songbook will be on the desk, next to an Inquest Console.
  • Alternatively, you can collect the songbook from outside the chamber without going through any portals. The room is located just up and south of  Tunnel Access Delta — .

Notes

[edit]
  • Grants 25 Volatile Magic when acquired.
  • To use the Sand Portals, a Jackal with the Shifting Sands mastery is required. The final room is accessible only through the Sand Portal.

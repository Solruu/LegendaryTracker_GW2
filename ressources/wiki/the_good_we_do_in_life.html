Acquisition

[edit]
  • Starting from the  Atholma Waypoint — , head north with your Springer, following the arrows on the wooden platforms. Once at Ewyn's Retreat, find Ewyn and talk to him.
    • If you haven't yet completed the Tales of Elona achievement, do that first, and then come back and talk to Ewyn about Efi.
    • If you have completed the mentioned achievement, you may talk to him directly.

Notes

[edit]

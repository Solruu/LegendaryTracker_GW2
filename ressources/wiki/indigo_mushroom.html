Notes

[edit]
  • Drops only for players who have the appropriate legendary collections unlocked: The Moot III: The Energizer or The Minstrel I: The Experimental Focus.
    • They will continue to drop indefinitely after one of these collections has been unlocked.
  • Using an Indigo Mushroom while having at least 25 in the current character's inventory will consume them to create a Drop of Indigo Mushroom Milk. You will need 251 Indigo Mushrooms for The Moot III: The Energizer collection.
  • Drops in various quantities:0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 13, 14, 15

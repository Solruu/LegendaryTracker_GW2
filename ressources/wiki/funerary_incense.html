Acquisition[edit]

  • Each Incense from Renown Heart vendors can only be purchased 1 time per account per day.
  • You can buy an unlimited number using Trade Contracts or Elegy Mosaics from Primeval Steward at the Tomb of the Primeval Kings in Desert Highlands.
  • Exchanging Crystalline Ingots is limited to 5 per day.

Notes[edit]

  • A fast way to acquire many Funerary Incense is to join a legendary bounty train and collect Elegy Mosaics to exchange for Funerary Incense at the Primeval Steward.
  • Another way is to leave characters at the Sanctum of Nabkha end chests and the chests inside Tomb of the Primeval Kings, as these all reward Trade Contracts which can be earned slowly, but easily by logging in to the characters each day.

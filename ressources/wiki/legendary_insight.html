Legendary Insights, commonly abbreviated as Li or LI, are a currency obtained from raids. They are stored in the wallet and are used to craft the HLegendary Perfected Envoy armor and Coalescence.

Acquisition

[edit]

Overview

[edit]

Up to 77 Legendary Insights can be acquired per week by completing all raids, every daily bounty, and 10 quickplay raids. These are broken down as follows:

  • 43 are awarded from weekly completions; 1 from each encounter:
    • 15 from Heart of Thorns (Wings 1-4)
    • 10 from Path of Fire (Wings 5-7)
    • 3 from Janthir Wilds (Wing 8)
    • 15 from raid encounters:
      • 1 from Core Game — Old Lion's Court
      • 7 from Icebrood Saga
      • 4 from End of Dragons
      • 2 from Secrets of the Obscure
      • 1 from Visions of Eternity
  • 28 are awarded from daily achievements in  Daily Raid Bounties, if all four are completed every day.
  • 6 are awarded from the weekly achievement  Weekly Quickplay Raid Encounters.
  • Quickplay mode will not count for a daily bounty, nor does it award coffers.
  • Challenge Modes no longer award additional Legendary Insights.[1]

Notes

[edit]
  • The original description, prior to the July 19 2022 game update, was "Used in the creation of legendary armor at the Mystic Forge."
    • Since the same game update, Legendary Insights replaced Legendary Divinations in every Path of Fire raid container and boss loot. Any remaining LD might be converted to LI either via a double-click or through a raid vendor.

Trivia

[edit]
  • While they can be acquired by accounts without expansions, legendary equipment crafting is locked behind owning them.

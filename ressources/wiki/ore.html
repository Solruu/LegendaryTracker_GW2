  • Ore is a raw unrefined material that can be obtained through mining nodes, however it can also be obtained via salvaging items.
  • Ores can be refined into ingots.
  • Ore will appear on the minimap as the icon. Hovering your cursor over the icon will show the type of ore and kind of node.

Ore vein[edit]

Ore veins are scattered throughout all Tyria. Mining a node requires the respective gathering tool. Once a player interacts with the ore, they will enter a mining animation and receive ore every time the node is hit.

Rich Ore Veins are a special type of node, which can be mined ten times as opposed to three.

Trivia[edit]

  • Mithril is a fictional metal first appearing in J.R.R. Tolkien's fantasy writing The Lord of the Rings.
  • Orichalcum is a mythological metal associated with the city of Atlantis.

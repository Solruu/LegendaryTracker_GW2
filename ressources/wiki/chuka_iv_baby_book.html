Walkthrough[edit]

Events, hearts and enemies to obtain specialist ingredients:

  • Collect griffon feathers for Kailani's flying machine (36) - talk to the NPC for Singed Griffon Feather
  • [Group Event] Defeat the champion jungle skelk lurker before it escapes (64) - do this twice for two Superior Skelk Liver
  • Help Jenrys protect nature from the Inquest (69) - interact with a barrel of inquest waste and kill magical tuna until you have two Infused Oily Fish Meat
  • Kill any skelk for two Skelk Liver.
  • Kill any tuna for two Oily Fish Meat.
  • Purchase six Brick of Clay from the Zephyrite vendors in Dry Top.

Crafting food:

  • Craft 3 Tiger Food.
  • Craft 1 Tiger Supplements.
  • Craft 1 Nutritional Tiger Food.
  • Craft 1 Infused Tiger Supplements
  • Craft 1 Infused Tiger Food.

Crafting milk:

  • Craft 3 Artificial Tiger Milk.
  • Craft 1 Tiger Supplements.
  • Craft 1 Nutritional Tiger Milk.
  • Craft 1 Infused Tiger Supplements.
  • Craft 1 Infused Tiger Milk.

Everything else:

  • Craft 1 Tiger Nesting Materials.
  • Craft 1 Heat Stone.
  • Craft 1 Tough Leather Ball.
  • Craft 1 Tiger Scratching Post.
  • Craft 1 Tiger Pole.
  • Craft 1 Tiger Gym.

Use the Baby Book to teleport to the Den, deposit each item.

Travel to Heart of the Snow Leopard, speak with Shaman Sigarr to remind you of the locations Chuka and Champawat died at - bring a Lesser Vision Crystal to each location:

  • End of the Tribulation Caverns jumping puzzle in Dredgehaunt Cliffs.
  • East and above Rosko's Campsite Waypoint in Fields of Ruin.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Trade 250 Basic Mithril Axe to a Master Weaponsmith after unlocking the Frostfang II: The Perfected Axe collection.

Notes

[edit]
  • Speak to a Master Weaponsmith to unlock the recipe to craft Basic Mithril Axe.
  • Completion of this step grants access to fulfilling the requirements of The Art of Forging: Axe Blade Edition and The Art of Woodworking: Axe Haft Edition.

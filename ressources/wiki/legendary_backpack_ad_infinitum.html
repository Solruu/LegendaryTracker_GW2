Legendary Backpack: Ad Infinitum is a Legendary Backpacks achievement that requires items from the Fractals of the Mists in combination with some specific tasks. This collection is the final step to obtain the Gift of Infinity, required to complete the legendary backpack, Ad Infinitum.

Notes[edit]

  • [1] You will need 1 Ball of Dark Energy in addition to those gained from salvaging Upper Bound in order to purchase the Aetherblade Diploma. This can be obtained by salvaging the Fractal Capacitor (Infused) created in the previous collection or salvaging another (non-ring) Ascended item.
  • Before unlocking, the achievement has the following instruction text: "To Unlock, Complete Ad Infinitum III, then talk to Kelvei in the Fractals of the Mists." Actually, you need to craft Unbound (which requires completing Ad Infinitum III) and then talk to Kelvei to unlock the collection.
  • The fractal levels provided under "Acquisition" are the minimum levels that must be completed.

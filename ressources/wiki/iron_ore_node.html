Iron Ore resource nodes are found in level 15-50 areas. They can be mined at least 3 times with a 50% chance to give 2 ore per hit.

Gathering results[edit]

All areas:

  • Iron Ore (guaranteed)
  • Ruined Ore Chunk (guaranteed) - if an Iron Mining Pick or better is not used
  • Vial of Cobalt Salts (Only if the collection The Bifrost III: The Legend is active)
  • Bauxite Ore (Only if the collection Incinerator I: The Experimental Dagger is active)
  • Nickel Ore (Only if the collection Incinerator I: The Experimental Dagger is active)

If in a lower-level area:[verification requested]

  • Amethyst Nugget
  • Carnelian Nugget
  • Lapis Nugget
  • Peridot Nugget
  • Spinel Nugget
  • Sunstone Nugget
  • Topaz Nugget

If in a higher-level area:[verification requested]

  • Amethyst Lump
  • Carnelian Lump
  • Lapis Lump
  • Peridot Lump
  • Spinel Lump
  • Sunstone Lump
  • Topaz Lump

In higher-level zones such as Lornar's Pass, Dredgehaunt Cliffs, and Bloodtide Coast you may receive Lumps instead of Nuggets when harvesting Iron Ore nodes.

Notes[edit]

  • Each component Gift requires map completion of the respective zone, that the associated heart has been completed that day (except for Gift of the Highlands), and costs 23,331 , for a total of 93,324 .
  • Each component Gift can be purchased only once per character, so Gift of the Desert can be crafted only once per character.
    • Oddly, Gift of the Rider is unlimited, even though it can only be used together with Gift of the Desert.

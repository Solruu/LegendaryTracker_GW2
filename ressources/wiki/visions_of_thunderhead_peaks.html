Visions of Thunderhead Peaks is a Legendary Trinkets achievement that is a part of the Vision I: Awakening collection.

Achievement[edit]

Visions of Thunderhead Peaks Legendary Trinkets 10
Explore Thunderhead Peaks for greater insight.Vision and visions, ended together in time...

Note: Completion of this achievement requires completion of the Riding Skyscales achievements.
Unlock Item: Trance Stone
Collection
 7 Steps Completed 10

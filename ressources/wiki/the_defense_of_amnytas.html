Rewards[edit]

  • Individual events do not reward any bonus chests, but will still give map bonus rewards.
  • Leaving the zone with enough participation will reward a Forgotten Nourys Reward Box (guaranteed) (sent through mail)
Daily
  • One bonus event chest with
    • Amnytas: Hero's Choice Chest (guaranteed) (once per day per account)
Repeatable
  • One Magnificent Chest (auto-looted)
  • Six small chests (up to 30 can be opened each day per account)
Bug:
The small chests will also spawn upon failure and are possible to open before the player is killed and teleported away.

Participation[edit]

  • Participate in events:
    • Enemies give triple the value while at low participation..
    • Defeat normal, veteran, and elite enemies — 1% participation (3% while below 25%)
    • Defeat champions — 5% participation (15% while below 25%)
      • A low amount of contribution will only give 1% participation
    • Hand in energy — No participation
    • Revive summoner — No participation
    • Destroy tentacles — No participation
    • Event completion:
      • Gold — 5% participation
      • Silver — ~3.5% participation
      • Bronze — ~3% participation
  • [Group Event] Defeat Nourys, the Eyes of the Abyss (80):
    • Success — 25% participation
    • Failure — ~13% participation
  • After five minutes of not gaining participation, your accumulated percentage will decay by 1.67% every 10 seconds.
Anomaly:
As nothing gives participation atop the spire, decay will often happen despite actively contributing in defeating Nourys and performing Dagda's ritual.

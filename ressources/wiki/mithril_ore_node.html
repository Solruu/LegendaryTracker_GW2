A fully-mined Mithril Ore node.

Mithril Ores are resource nodes found in level 70-80 areas. They can be mined at least three times, except for rich veins, which can be mined ten times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Mithril Ore (guaranteed)
  • Beryl Crystal
  • Chrysocola Crystal
  • Emerald Crystal
  • Opal Crystal
  • Ruby Crystal
  • Sapphire Crystal
  • Azurite Crystal (chance in Molten Furnace Fractal and Molten Furnace story mission)
  • Chunk of Pure Jade (only rarely in Cantha)
  • Chunk of Granite (in the Crystal Desert)
  • Ruined Ore Chunk (guaranteed) - if a Mithril Mining Pick or better is not used

Visions of Jahai is a Legendary Trinkets achievement that is a part of the Vision I: Awakening collection.

Achievement[edit]

Visions of Jahai Legendary Trinkets 10
Explore Jahai Bluffs for greater insight.What is time to one who transcends it?

Note: Completion of this achievement requires completion of the Riding Skyscales achievements.
Unlock Item: Trance Stone
Collection
 7 Steps Completed 10

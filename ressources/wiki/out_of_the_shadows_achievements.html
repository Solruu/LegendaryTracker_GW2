Notes[edit]

  1. ^ Dive Master: Just Dropping By: The simplest way to reach the island with the goggles is to begin from  Zealot's Overlook Waypoint — and glide into the nearby ley-line. Once you reach the top, take the updraft to your right.
    There is no pool at the bottom of this dive; instead, a portal will spawn once you approach the ground, which will teleport you back up to the goggles.
  2. ^ Jade Carving: Defeat any jade constructs in Bloodstone Fen. Constructs which spawn during Out of the Shadows missions also count.
  3. ^ Magic Unbound: The Gathering: Jumping off the ledge with the diving goggles (see above) is an easy way to complete this achievement.
  4. ^ Mantle Pieces: Any White Mantle in Bloodstone Fen count towards this achievement.

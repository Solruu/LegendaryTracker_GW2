Ardent Glorious armor is a PvP-exclusive GAscended or HLegendary armor set that can be acquired from earning rewards in PvP Leagues.

Acquisition[edit]

Ascended variant[edit]

  • Purchased from the Ascended Armor League Vendor in the Heart of the Mists.

Legendary variant[edit]

Primary article: Legendary armor#PvP armor set

Crafting the HLegendary armor set requires the corresponding GAscended armor piece. Several items needed in the crafting process require the Heart of Thorns expansion unlocked.

Notes[edit]

  • Players can purchase either the FExotic armor skin or the pricier GAscended armor piece to unlock the wardrobe skin for the Mistforged Glorious Hero's armor set.

It is recommended that you do not attempt to stat change your precursor item with the standard stat swap recipe in the Mystic Forge. While you will be provided with an item with the desired stats, it will have been replaced with a generic ascended item that cannot be used as a legendary precursor.

See also[edit]

  • Glorious armor — A simple FExotic version without particle effect or animations.
  • Glorious Hero's armor — A similar GAscended and HLegendary versions but with more vibrant colors. Purchased with PvP Tournament Voucher.
  • Mistforged Glorious Hero's armor — Requires PvP Rank: 100 and an equivalent Ardent Glorious armor wardrobe skin unlocked to purchase.

Trivia[edit]

  • This armor set was introduced in PvP League Season 5[1].

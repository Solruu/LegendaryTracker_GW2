Chuka and Champawat III: Tigris is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Tigris, for the legendary short bow, Chuka and Champawat. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Chuka and Champawat III: Tigris Legendary Weapons 3

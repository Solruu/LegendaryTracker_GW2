Coalescence is a legendary trinket crafted in the Mystic Forge. The three Path of Fire raids must be completed in order to craft this item.

Lore[edit]

Coalescence
They walked through the Underworld, dove straight into the Mystic Forge. They crossed through the long-forgotten halls of Ahdashim—such feats would be cast in gold and told to awe-struck children.

Achievements[edit]

  • The following achievements are required to acquire the necessary materials for crafting:
    • Coalescence I: Unbridled — unlocked by defeating any raid boss.
    • Coalescence II: The Gift — unlocked by completing the previous achievement
    • Coalescence III: Culmination — unlocked by completing the previous achievement

Graphics[edit]

Primary article: Legendary trinket#Visual effects

Aurora, Coalescence and Vision — share the same visual effect, which depends on the number of these trinkets worn and displayed at the same time.

  • With weapons stowed, an ethereal aura will appear around the player.
  • With weapons drawn, four orbs or bubbles will orbit the player's head.

Piece of Rare Unidentified Gear is a container that, upon opening it, provides a random equipment item of ERare rarity, with a small chance for an item of Exotic rarity. Rare Unidentified Gear is often provided as loot and rewards in all areas of the game for level 80 characters, and is also available in other rarities: Common Unidentified Gear (CFine) and Unidentified Gear (DMasterwork). See Unidentified Gear for the overview for all three rarities.

Unidentified Gear can be salvaged both directly and after identifying it into individual equipment items, with the latter providing additional higher rarity crafting materials. As a lot of rewards in the game are provided in the form of items, Unidentified Gear is used to reduce inventory clutter while preserving the chance for higher quality loot.

Notes[edit]

  • Precursor weapons of first generation can drop from Unidentified Gear of all types.

Trivia[edit]

  • This item was originally called "Sandworn Wreckage". It was renamed shortly before the launch of Path of Fire.
  • Originally, Unidentified Gear only dropped within the Path of Fire expansion and Living World Season 4 maps. As of the March 26, 2019 update, they now drop in most areas of the game.
    • This update also stopped magic find from affecting these items.

Notes[edit]

  • Prior to Feb 8th 2017, crafting this item only required a Legendary Inscription instead of a Visionary Inscription.
  • Stat changing this item will completely change it to a regular Ascended weapon and cannot be used to craft Shooshadoo.

Trivia[edit]

  • A baby quaggan can be seen inside the cracked egg from the correct angle.

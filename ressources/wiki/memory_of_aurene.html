Acquisition[edit]

Contained in[edit]

  •   End of Dragons Reward Coffer (25) (choice, only repeatable)

Rewarded by[edit]

Note: Events listed drop memories in their appropriate finale chest which is only lootable once per day per account. Story steps reward memories only when replaying them for the second time, and only to the instance owner.

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Defeat Soo-Won (15)
    • Engage Kralkatorrik (6)
  • Completing any of the following story steps:
    • Living World Season 3
      • Episode 2: Rising Flames
        • Dragon Vigil (2-3)
      • Episode 3: A Crack in the Ice
        • Precocious Aurene (3)
    • Living World Season 4
      • Episode 1: Daybreak
        • Eye of the Brandstorm (3)
        • The Hero of Istan (3)
      • Episode 2: A Bug in the System
        • A Kindness Repaid (3)
      • Episode 3: Long Live the Lich
        • Seized (3)
        • Be My Guest (3)
      • Episode 4: A Star to Guide Us
        • A Shattered Nation (3)
        • Legacy (3)
      • Episode 5: All or Nothing
        • Scion & Champion (3)
        • The Crystal Blooms (3)
        • The Crystal Dragon (3)
      • Episode 6: War Eternal
        • The End (3)
        • Descent (3)
  • Completing Dragonstorm (6).

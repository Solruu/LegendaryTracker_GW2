A common way to obtain Amalgamated Gemstones is from the daily final reward chests of various meta events from the expansions, the timers for them are listed here.

Events[edit]

The following events that do not follow a timer also award a Hero's Choice Chest with an Amalgamated Gemstone:

  • In Gyala Delve:
    • Destroy the ravenous wanderer
  • In Inner Nayos:
    • The Road to Heitor
    • The Fangs That Gnash
    • Defeat Eparch, the Consumed King

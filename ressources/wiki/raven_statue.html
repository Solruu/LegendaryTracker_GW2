Notes

[edit]
  • During the event, the statues will glow blue, indicating they can be interacted with. This glow will disappear upon interaction, regardless of whether you answer the riddle, and it will take several seconds before the statue becomes interactable again.
  • Each statue has its own unique pair of riddles (see above), but which riddle it presents is randomly selected.

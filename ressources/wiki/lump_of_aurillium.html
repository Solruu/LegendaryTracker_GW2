Lump of Aurillium is a currency earned by participating in events in Auric Basin. They are stored in the Account Wallet and can be spent at merchants within the Auric Basin.

Vendors[edit]

  • The Aurillium Collector at the Pact Camp in Tarnished Treetop sells Exalted Keys and recipes for crafting Viper's prefix gear.
  • The Exalted Mastery Vendor in Tarir, the Forgotten City provides a wide variety of goods.
  • Used to purchase Machetes from the Whispers Keeper at the Pact Base Camp in Dragon's Stand.
  • Can be used in the appropriately upgraded Ley-Energy Matter Converter to purchase 1 container daily.

Notes[edit]

  • Any weight class selected from the Bearkin War Helm Choice Box may be used in the mystic forge recipe.

Trivia[edit]

  • The name Nyr Hrammr is most likely derived from Old Norse nýr and hrammr, which mean "new" and "bear paw", respectively.

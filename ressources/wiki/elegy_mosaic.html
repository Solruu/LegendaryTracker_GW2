Notes[edit]

  • Unlocking the full set of Funerary weapons and all weights of Funerary armor costs a total of 936  and 87  36 .
  • Generation 2 legendary weapons crafted using Gift of Desert Mastery, and Coalescence, require a total of 300  for the components of Gift of the Rider.
    • 750  can also be spent to speed up acquisition of the 250 Funerary Incenses.
  • The achievement Last Rites must be completed to obtain elegy mosaics.

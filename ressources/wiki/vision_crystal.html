  • Leatherworker recipes
  • Tailor recipes

Achievements[edit]

A Vision Crystal is also used to accomplish certain achievements:

  • Knight of the Thorn
  • A Henge Away from Home
  • Lost Pilgrims: Can be brought in your inventory instead of finding the open world Vision Crystal bundle, will not be consumed.
  • Chasing Tales (used at the Heroic Font to complete the achievement, is consumed)

Salvage results[edit]

Can only be salvaged by using an Ascended Salvage Tool or Ascended Salvage Kit.

  • Bloodstone Brick (3-5)
  • Empyreal Star (3-5)
  • Dragonite Ingot (3-5)
  • Augur's Stone (0-1)

Trivia[edit]

  • In the original Guild Wars, the Vision Crystal was an artifact used in a series of missions that had to be completed in order to ascend.

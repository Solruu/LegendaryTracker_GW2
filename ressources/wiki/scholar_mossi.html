Scholar Mossi is an asura found standing next to Grandmaster Craftsman Hobbs, wielding Astralaria and HOPE, that offers certain collectibles to complete the second tier of precursor crafting.

Notes[edit]

  • Each collection must be completed before the next is available, and are only offered in the following sequence:
    • Desert Magics
    • Jungle Magics
    • Ancient Water Magics (if needed)
    • Herbal Magics (if needed)
    • Old World Magics
    • Stabilizing Magics (if needed)
  • Every Legendary weapon requires at least three of these collections. Frenzy and Kamohoali'i Kotaki are the only two weapons that require only Desert Magics, Jungle Magics, and Old World Magics. Bolt, Frostfang, Howler, Incinerator, Kraitkin, Meteorlogicus, and The Minstrel also require Ancient Water Magics and Stabilizing Magics. The remaining fifteen weapons require all six collections.

Notes[edit]

  • This sigil replaced the Superior Sigil of Sanctuary with the April 15, 2014 game update.
  • This sigil provides a stronger benefit than Superior Sigil of Life, however Superior Sigil of Benevolence only affects healing towards other allies.
  • This sigil does not provide a bonus to barrier output, while Superior Sigil of Life does.

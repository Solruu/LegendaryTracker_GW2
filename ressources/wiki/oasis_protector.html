Oasis Protector is a trophy required for the Nevermore I: Ravenswood Branch legendary weapon collection.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Offered for purchase by Cesseilia after helping her defend and research the oasis in the Whisperwill Bogs of Bloodtide Coast. Found south of the  Bogside Camp Waypoint — at Rhiann's Respite point of interest.

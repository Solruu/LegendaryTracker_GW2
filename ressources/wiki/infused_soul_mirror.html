Acquisition

[edit]
  • Acquire a Soul Mirror from Burnisher Kengo, north of  Northwatch Waypoint — in Auric Basin (cannot be during certain meta events), then with it still in inventory, light (or interact with after someone lights) the Brazier past the gate at  Bandit's Rest — cemetery within the Spirit Woods in Spirit Vale, just before Gorseval's Perch.
    • To light the Brazier, a player in the squad must carry the Brazier bundle from  Abandoned Outpost — through the rest of the Traverse the Spirit Woods encounter without dropping it (i.e. cannot weapon swap, change form, or be downed) or the bundle will be lost.

Related achievements

[edit]
  • Spirit Vale: Keep the Lights On — Light the brazier at the far end of the Spirit Woods without dropping the spectral torch. (2)

Notes

[edit]
  • Can be safely sold after acquiring it.
  • If a player needs the Infused Soul Mirror, anyone on the team can carry the torch, it does not need to be the player who needs the collection. Simply activating the brazier at the end of the Spirit Woods will give the player the Infused Soul Mirror, so long as they have the Soul Mirror in their inventory.
  • Even if lit, the torch will not light up again in a new raid instance before the weekly reset.

Walkthrough[edit]

  1. You must finish the Precocious Aurene story step to gain a Token of Affection and an Exalted Portal Stone from Aurene.
  2. You may either purchase the following recipes from Slooshoo in Bitterfrost Frontier map, or purchase the crafted pieces from other players:
    • Recipe: Dragon Hatchling Doll Adornments - Jeweler 400
    • Recipe: Dragon Hatchling Doll Hide - Leatherworking 450
    • Recipe: Dragon Hatchling Doll Eyes - Artificing 450
    • Recipe: Dragon Hatchling Doll Frame - Huntsman 450
    • Recipe: Gossamer Stuffing - Tailoring 450
  3. Right-click each piece and select "Add to Collection" from the menu.
  4. After crafting all the pieces (or purchasing them) and placing them in your inventory, click on a piece to assemble them.
  5. Use the Exalted Portal Stone to teleport to the egg chamber in Tarir, then talk to Aurene to present the doll.

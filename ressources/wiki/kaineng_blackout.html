Kaineng Blackout is a level 80 meta event that occurs in New Kaineng City. The power grid in the city is malfunctioning and thieves are taking advantage of the situation. You must stabilize districts and labs throughout the city and help technicians repair any damage.

After stabilizing most of the city, it's discovered that there is a corruption spreading throughout the power grid. You must help Yao and Officer Mizuki find the source of the corruption, isolate it, and purge it.

Bug:
Enemies from hold breaker box events may fail to respawn after the meta-event ends and continue harassing nearby friendly NPCs. This can result in elite-ranked anomalies appearing around the breaker boxes during the day.

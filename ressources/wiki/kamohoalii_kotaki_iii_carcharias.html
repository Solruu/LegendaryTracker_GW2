Kamohoali'i Kotaki III: Carcharias is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Carcharias, for the legendary spear, Kamohoali'i Kotaki. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Kamohoali'i Kotaki III: Carcharias Legendary Weapons 3
The sea is wide and mighty, and conceals both great dangers and wondrous prizes. Dive into it, explore its depths,
and swim deep beneath its ever-shifting surface in your journey to create the legendary precursor known as the Carcharias
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Spear
Reward: Chest of Diving
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 28 Items 2

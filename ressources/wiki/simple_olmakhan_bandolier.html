Notes[edit]

  • The Box of Inquest Spoils which contains the Simple Olmakhan Bandolier is only acquired once per account from the achievement The Charge (found in The Charge story step).
  • The recipe is discovered upon opening the Box of Inquest Spoils obtained by completing The Charge story step.
  • The recipe is unlocked account wide for the 3 professions (Tailor, Leatherworker, Armorsmith) it can be crafted with.
  • Can be given to Efi to acquire "We Carry Her Loss Together", required for the Lasting Bonds: Where We Come From collection.
    • Efi will not take the Bandolier if it is occupying a bag slot.
    • Giving the Bandolier to Efi will permanently remove it from your inventory.
Bug:
Efi may not see the bandolier in your inventory, blocking progress on this achievement. Try holding the Bandolier with the character that went through the story and/or received it initially.

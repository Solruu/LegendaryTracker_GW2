Other[edit]

Rare chance from the following sources:

  • Drop from foes throughout the world.
  • Drop from any container that can drop random exotic weapons, such as the Chest of Exotic Equipment, or from containers that drop Unidentified Gear.
  • Mystic Forge: chance when combining four rare or four exotic pieces of equipment with an average level of 75 or higher, e.g. one L60 weapon + three L80 weapons or three L75 pieces of armor and a L75 weapon.
  • Found in various chests:
    • The event chests after killing an epic boss, such as the Shadow Behemoth or Claw of Jormag.
    • Event chests in dungeons.
    • Jumping puzzle chests.
    • Bonus Chests from world boss events.
    • Basic chests found though the world.
  • Reward from guild missions.
  • Reward from Raids - each major boss is guaranteed to drop one exotic piece of equipment.

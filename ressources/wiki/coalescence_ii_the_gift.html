Coalescence II: The Gift is a Legendary Trinkets collection achievement to obtain the Gift of Complex Emotions, an item used to create the Legendary ring Coalescence.

The Collection requires the player to use the Trophy item Alembic Apparatus to interact with Bottles hidden around Tyria and then fill each individual bottle with emotions.

Talk to the Sanctum Librarian in The Dark Library, which can be accessed through the rift at the end of the Tomb of Primeval Kings in Desert Highlands. The rift can only be opened once the player has completed the Open Skies: Sunspear Sanctuary part of the Griffon mount scavenger hunt.

11 Bottle of Anguish (Filled) Item Service You feel a tremendous weight holding this vessel—one of regret and uncertainty.
Use the /kneel emote at the Field of the Fallen point of interest in Lion's Arch.

Interact with the bottles found on the table just after the Splorg Metamystics Lab south entrance in Metrica Province.
13 Bottle of Excitement (Filled) Item Service Your mood is raised significantly thanks to your possession of this jar.
Jump into the endlessly looped portal fountain (which keeps teleporting you above it after falling in), in the Fractals of the Mists lobby.
14 Bottle of Joy (Empty) Item Service This jar seems to possess the faintest traces of positivity, but is otherwise devoid of contents.
Interact with the bottles next to one of the quaggans named Coddler on the ground level of the Coddler's Cove jumping puzzle in Timberline Falls.
15 Bottle of Joy (Filled) Item Service Nearly weightless and possessed of some sort of bubbly energy, this jar is very pleasing to hold.
Jump into the fountain with the playing Quaggan Hatchlings near the Mystic Plaza Point of Interest in Lion's Arch.
16 Bottle of Shame (Empty) Item Service You cannot help but hide this jar from others.
Interact with the bottles found under the table in front of Logan Thackeray inside the Seraph Headquarters instance in Divinity's Reach.
17 Bottle of Shame (Filled) Item Service You feel strangely guilty possessing this jar, as if a terrible secret weighs on you.
Approach the Glint's Lair point of interest in the Desert Highlands.
18 Alchemical Alembic Complete Item Service Filling the jars has lead to a startling conclusion.
Use the Alembic Apparatus item in the Inventory after filling all the bottles.

Notes[edit]

  • You need to acquire Alembic Apparatus before you can collect Empty bottles.
  • Since you can acquire Alembic Apparatus before completing the previous collection, keep in mind that you must have completed Coalescence I: Unbridled to successfully interact with Bottles hidden around Tyria.
  • You can only complete the Filled bottle collection steps after completing their respective Empty collection steps.
  • Bottle of Fear (Filled) can be acquired by having another player who has completed the Open Skies achievement in the party to open the rift.

Acquisition[edit]

  • Take a Gift of Wood to the Veteran Ascalonian Woodcutter found at the end of the Urban Battleground Fractal after the final boss is defeated.
    • The Veteran Ascalonian Woodcutter is located to the left of the "chapel" from which Captain Ashym spawns, on the ramparts accessed by a nearby staircase.

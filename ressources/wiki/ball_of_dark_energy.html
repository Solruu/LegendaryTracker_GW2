Acquisition[edit]

Salvaged by using Ascended Salvage Kits and Ascended Salvage Tools:

  • Ascended weapons (guaranteed)
  • Ascended armor (guaranteed)
  • Ascended amulets (very low chance)
  • Ascended accessories (very low chance)
  • Ascended back items (very low chance)
  • Ascended Aquatic Headgear (low chance)

Guaranteed amounts from salvaging the following items:

  • Finite Result (5)
  • Upper Bound (9)

Notes[edit]

  • Salvage rate research can be found here.
  • A cheap and straightforward way to get Balls of Dark Energy is to buy an ascended armor piece from Raid encounters vendor Smoxxi and then salvage it with an Ascended Salvage Tool. e.g. The Defender's Chest of Boots will cost 150 Magnetite Shards and 5 gold. Crafting an ascended armor piece for salvaging is another option, but it's several times more expensive, requires account bound materials and a respective crafting profession leveled to 500.
    • Another caveat to crafting is that some of the more recently added ascended items do not appear to have their salvage tables set up correctly, and do not salvage into Balls of Dark Energy. An example of this would be the Empowered Boneskinner's Totem.
  • While some precursor weapons are of GAscended rarity, salvaging them does not guarantee a Ball of Dark Energy.

A gift used to create legendary weapons.

Acquired from fully exploring all Central Tyrian maps.

— In-game description

Gift of Exploration is an item earned from World completion and used for crafting the Gift of Mastery (as a part of crafting a first generation legendary weapon) and the guild hall decoration Tyrian Globe.

Acquisition[edit]

  • 2 Gifts of Exploration are rewarded upon World completion – map completing all explorable zones in Central Tyria. Each character can achieve world completion only once and, therefore, can only earn two Gifts.

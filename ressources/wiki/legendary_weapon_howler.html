Legendary Weapon: Howler is a Legendary Weapons achievement.

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Notes
1 Howl Item Warhorn This weapon is used to craft the legendary warhorn Howler.

Combine this weapon in the Mystic Forge with:
• 1 Gift of Howler
• 1 Gift of Mastery
• 1 Gift of Fortune
2 Gift of Howler Item Trophy A gift used to create the legendary warhorn Howler.

Made by combining these items in the Mystic Forge:

  • 1 Gift of Wood
  • 1 Wolf Statue
  • 100 Icy Runestones
  • 1 Superior Sigil of Accuracy
3 Icy Runestone Item Trophy Used to create legendary weapon and trinket gift items.

Purchased from Rojan the Penitent in Frostgorge Sound after successfully defeating the Claw of Jormag.
4 Gift of Wood Item Trophy A gift of wood used to create generation one and two legendary weapons and the legendary back item Ad Infinitum.

Purchase the recipe for this item from any Mystic Forge vendor and reach a huntsman rating of 400 to craft.
5 Wolf Statue Item Trophy A wolf statue used to create Howler.

Purchase the recipe for this item from any Mystic Forge vendor and reach a leatherworker rating of 400 to craft.
6 Gift of Thorns Item Trophy A gift used to create legendary weapons.

Purchased from dungeon vendors for 500 Tales of Dungeon Delving.

7 Gift of Mastery Item Trophy A gift used to create legendary weapons.

Made by combining these items in the Mystic Forge:
  • 1 Bloodstone Shard
  • 250 Obsidian Shards
  • 1 Gift of Exploration
  • 1 Gift of Battle
8 Gift of Exploration Item Trophy A gift used to create legendary weapons.

Acquired from fully exploring all Central Tyrian maps.
9 Bloodstone Shard Item Trophy Hint: Purchased from a Mystic Forge vendor for Spirit Shards.
10 Obsidian Shard Item Trophy Hint: Usually purchased from laurel merchants, fractal vendors, Mastery vendors, and quartermasters across Tyria.
11 Gift of Fortune Item Trophy A gift of fortune used to create legendary weapons.

Made by combining these items in the Mystic Forge:

  • 77 Mystic Clovers
  • 250 Globs of Ectoplasm
  • 1 Gift of Might
  • 1 Gift of Magic
12 Glob of Ectoplasm Item Trophy Salvage Item
13 Mystic Clover Item Crafting material Hint: Sometimes acquired by combining Mystic Coins, Obsidian Shards, Globs of Ectoplasm, and either Philosopher's Stones or Mystic Crystals in the Mystic Forge. Can also be purchased from strike, raid, or fractal vendors.
14 Gift of Might Item Trophy A gift used to create legendary weapons.

Made by combining these items in the Mystic Forge:

  • 250 Vicious Fangs
  • 250 Armored Scales
  • 250 Vicious Claws
  • 250 Ancient Bones
15 Gift of Magic Item Trophy A gift used to create legendary weapons.

Made by combining these items in the Mystic Forge:

  • 250 Vials of Powerful Blood
  • 250 Powerful Venom Sacs
  • 250 Elaborate Totems
  • 250 Piles of Crystalline Dust
16 Gift of Battle Item Trophy A gift used in legendary crafting.

Acquired from the WvW Gift of Battle Item Reward Track.

To create the Gift of War Prosperity, combine this in the Mystic Forge with:

  • 15 Mystic Clovers
  • 1 Gift of Condensed Might
  • 1 Gift of Condensed Magic

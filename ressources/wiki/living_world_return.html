“

Living World is the story of the dragon cycle, and now is the perfect time to look at everything that's happened on the path that brought us here. Join the mission to catch up on Living World episodes you've missed—for free—and jump-start your legendary journey in Guild Wars 2: End of Dragons!

— Official description

Living World Return was a series of bonus events that put different Living World episodes on spotlight each week with new achievements and rewards, as well as making the episodes free to claim. After their respective bonus event had ended, the achievements, as well as the rewards, were moved to Side Stories, from where they remain permanently available.

Claiming[edit]

Where to claim episodes inside the Story Journal.
  • These episodes are currently not available for free, but can still be purchased through the gem store (or through Steam for Steam accounts).
  • When these achievements were introduced, players could log in to the game during the free period to receive the episodes, even if you did not own the expansions.
    • On logging in to a character you would receive the unlocks, as well as the event notification, in your ingame mail.
      • If you did not own the episode already and left it in your mail without unlocking, the mail would disappear with the next free release and the free unlock would have been lost.
      • Some players reported the need to be above level 10 and visit a city to receive the mail. Another option was to enter the PvP lobby with level 3+ character.
    • In a case you could not access the mail, you could also have claimed the episodes directly from the Story Journal for 0 if you owned the expansion required to play the episode. Claiming from the mail was not restricted by any ownership, however.

Rewards[edit]

  • Complete any 10 Return meta-achievements to earn a Fragment of Prismatic Light, allowing you to choose an account-bound Generation 3 Precursor weapon from Leivas in either Seitung Province or Arborstone. The Fragment itself is not needed for the precursor choice and can be sold safely once acquired.
  • After fully completing the main meta-achievement for all three playable Living World seasons and The Icebrood Saga, Seasons of the Dragons, you'll receive the legendary amulet Prismatic Champion's Regalia.

Mail[edit]

Special event mail

ArenaNet

Return to the Living World Story

A special event focusing on a previous Living World story chapter is active this week! Check it out to earn special bonus rewards for playing that chapter's story and map. Completing multiple sets of event achievements across the Living World story will progress the Seasons of the Dragons meta-achievement, with some legendary rewards available!

If you missed a week or need to wait to experience the story, don't worry— the achievements are permanent. Look for achievements from previous weeks in the Side Stories category in the Achievements tab in your Hero panel.

Ascended Shards of Glory are a currency obtained from completing PvP league reward tracks. They can be used together with Shards of Glory to purchase ascended equipment from the Ascended Armor League Vendor and Ascended Weapon League Vendor; a complete set of one ascended armor costs 800 Ascended Shards of Glory, 20 Grandmaster Marks, and 13 gold.

Notes[edit]

  • After being destroyed or used in a recipe, Salmon of Knowledge Backpiece Remnant with the same Mystic Forge functionality can be bought from Kodan Landspeaker.
  • This item's description was updated from "This back item is used for crafting Janthir Wilds legendaries. Further copies can be purchased in the near future." with the June 24, 2025 game update.

Trivia[edit]

  • The Salmon of Knowledge is a reference to the legend of Fionn mac Cumhaill.

Orrian Pearls are tokens acquired and spent in Siren's Landing. They are that map's specific currency.

Acquisition[edit]

Gathered from[edit]

  • Hidden Reliquary Chest (0, 2, 5, 7, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, 51) (chance)
  • Orrian Oyster (guaranteed)
  • Orrian Oyster (9) (guaranteed, home instances)
  • Waterlogged Chest (0-3) (chance)
  • Known gathering amounts from Waterlogged Chests are as follows:
    • Power the northwest shrine (1) (per event)
    • Defend the northeast shrine (1) (per event)
    • Defend the southwest shrine (1) (per event)
    • Defend the southeast shrine (1) (per event)
    • Capture the beast (2) (once per character, per day)
    • Kill the horrid illusion (2) (once per character, per day)
    • Kill the champion wurm (2) (once per character, per day)
    • Defeat the unchained wyvern (3) (once per character, per day)[verification requested]
    • Abaddon's Ascent (3) (once per character, per day)

Participation[edit]

At least 25% participation is required to earn rewards, with maximum rewards being rewarded at 100%.

  • Complete events — 25% participation
    • Only events that are part of the meta event counts.
Anomaly:
As opposed to other map meta events, participation is exclusively earned through events, no enemies give participation, and there is no decay.

Dialogue[edit]

One of the following every other minute during event start countdown and while waiting to confront Livia
Livia: Best to prepare now.
Livia: I fear if we wait any longer, the path to the Mists gate will become more untenable.
Livia: The alliance is dragging their heels. (scoffs) Typical...
Livia: We don't know when or if alliance support will arrive, and my patience is wearing thin.
Approaching Arina
Arina: Commander! They're marching on the titan. You should head that way.
Starting the event
Livia: Wayfinder, we must act, and soon. The longer we delay, the narrower our window becomes. The titan must be dealt with, quickly and with lethal force.
I hope the alliance will understand.
The Tyrian Alliance may still send support.
Arriving in the zone during [Group Event] Defeat Lextalion the Merciless (80)
Bengt Tovasson: H-hello? Wayfinder? Is this thing— Yes, good!
Bengt Tovasson: We've entered the maw, beset by an unnatural foe. Join us!

Bengt Tovasson: Bengt here, Wayfinder. Our forces are making progress through the titan. Pick a prong of the fork and pitch in!
Bengt Tovasson: Can't promise it won't hurt if you don't choose mine. (chuckles)

Bengt Tovasson: Did that get through? Ah, forget it; you'll figure it out.

On success[edit]

Livia: Good, you're both here. The Mists gate... Its connection is in flux.
Livia: The titan isn't dead—I can...feel it—and there's no telling how long it will take to recover, if it can.
Councillor Ludo: Even if it's temporary, we've proven that it's possible to take this chamber.
Councillor Ludo: That will help mollify the alliance in the short term; many of them had doubts.
Bengt Tovasson: My report to the countess will be similarly optimistic. She was worried, to say the least.
Livia: Despite what you may have heard, it wasn't my intention to create bad blood—as much as I love a little drama.
Bengt Tovasson: Something you and Mabon had in common, it seems.
Livia: For now, we'll hold the Mists gate as long as we're able.
Arriving in the zone after success
Bengt Tovasson: Ah, Wayfinder! You missed out on all the fun.
Bengt Tovasson: We're keeping watch over the Mists gate in the central organ cavity, for now.
Talking to Livia
Here we are, Wayfinder. None the worse for wear. It would seem the alliance's concerns regarding the scepter were exaggerated. Whatever your opinion on the matter, I appreciate your support.
You all right? The scepter...
Livia: All things considered, it could have been worse.
Livia: Isgarren will be cross with me for a bit, but he'll see the necessity in time.
Livia: Not that I'm not used to working with self-important genious types.
Livia: One of these days, I should tell you about my work with Gadd. He was...well, exhausting.
Are you and the countess okay?
Livia: Between us, Anise is...like family to me.
Livia: Her second great-grandmother, Evennia, was a friend and ally in the Shining Blade.
Livia: I respect Anise's station and her experience; the Six know she's earned that much.
Livia: But...there are times I still see a little girl, playing pranks in the royal halls with Princess Jennah.
Good working with you again, Livia.
Talking to Councillor Ludo
Livia's actions will have consequences, but our success here bought us intel, credibility, and time. If the alliance doesn't see that, I'll have to explain it to them very slowly.
How do you think this operation went?
Councillor Ludo: By asuran standards or alliance— You know what? A win is a win. Alchemy knows I was due.
Councillor Ludo: Between Livia's actions and the immediate fallout, this could've been an abject failure.
Councillor Ludo: Now? I get to brief the alliance and write a report for the Arcane Council.
Councillor Ludo: I hope they see the merits of our collaboration. Otherwise, I'll have to drag them to the table kicking and screaming.
Seems you've made an ally in Empress Ihn?
Councillor Ludo: It's early stages, but my correspondences with the empress and her sister have been rather advantageous so far.
Councillor Ludo: It helps to have someone there to convey the intricacies of the asuran...mmm, expertise.
Councillor Ludo: Your colleague, Taimi, has been indispensable in that regard. I should've rescued her from Phlunt's clutches years ago.
Councillor Ludo: Tangentially, I received a...hm, juvenescent illustration of the empress and myself from her niece. Auspicious? Perhaps.
Until we meet again, Councillor.
Talking to Bengt Tovasson
Good to work with you, Commander, or is it Wayfinder? I'm starting to see that my son's gossip about your exploits caries weight after all.
You knew Mabon, huh?
Bengt Tovasson: A few decades back, when I was a spry acolyte of Raven and had fostered a talent for mesmer magic...
Bengt Tovasson: I met a disguised Mabon, sealing some anomaly in the Shiverpeaks. I aided him and was invited into the collective.
Bengt Tovasson: When he revealed his true self to the collective some years later, I stood by him as a close friend and pupil.
Bengt Tovasson: It wasn't a hard decision, although I can understand why it would be for some.
So, the Mesmer Collective...
Bengt Tovasson: You have some familiarity, given your friendship with Lady Meade, no? (chuckles)
Bengt Tovasson: I tease. Unlike the Astral Ward, we are small and connected enough to avoid alliance oversight. For now, at least.
Bengt Tovasson: Our inner council maintains internal matters, while the outer manages our external connections.
Bengt Tovasson: The Shroud, well... They're our "field agents" and investigators.
Bengt Tovasson: That's all you'll pry from me. Perhaps Anise will invite you to a gala someday. They're quite the spectacle.
We'll both leave a legacy worth remembering.

Bengt Tovasson: We'll get another shot, Livia. There's no wisdom in dying here.

Notes[edit]

  • If the event is not started, Livia will wait indefinitely, meaning it's possible to do the event long after the initial timer, and even back-to-back on multiple maps.
  • If story chapter A Titanic Undertaking is active with Help Livia weaken the titan's inner organs being the current step, it is possible to immediately gain 100% progression towards its goal for participating in the destruction of any of the titan's organs. That includes events: Assault the North Lung, Assault the South Lung, and Assault the titanic heart.

Trivia[edit]

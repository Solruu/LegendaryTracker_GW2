Provisioner Tokens are a currency earned by trading specific items to Faction Provisioners in various maps. They are stored in the Account Wallet and can be spent at the Faction Provisioners to buy Bloodbound weapons as well as bags of armor or materials and items needed to make legendary armor, runes, and sigils.

Notes[edit]

  • Almost all trades have individual weekly limits, but a few account-bound materials with time-gated acquisition can be traded indefinitely: Charged Quartz Crystal, Glob of Elder Spirit Residue, Lump of Mithrillium, and Spool of Thick Elonian Cord.
    • Spool of Silk Weaving Thread cannot be traded, as opposed to the other GAscended materials.
    • Research Notes can also be traded indefinitely despite the acquisition not being time-gated, but the amount needed per trade means it's at a premium.
  • It takes 2,600 Provisioner tokens to purchase enough Gifts of Craftsmanship to craft all current legendary items that require one, and thus fill the Legendary Armory (52 gifts for 8 sigils, 7 runes, the relic, and 36 armor pieces)
    • Note that this total includes both Obsidian Armor and Envoy Armor, which provide redundant functionality. Crafting only one set of armor per weight class reduces the total required to 1700 tokens.
  • Prior to the February 3, 2026 game update, 2 tokens were awarded per unique challenge mode raid boss per week, up to a maximum of 26 tokens per week.

Lore[edit]

Kamohoali'i Kotaki
A historian once wrote of a great beast that lurked off the coast of Orr—possibly a living Champion of Zhaitan. Although she hasn't been seen for a century past, her kin still roam the dark waters.

Graphics[edit]

Kamohoali'i Kotaki is a shark spear that has a purple glow on it and leaves a trail of blood in the water as you swim.

Aura
  • The user's body is surrounded by bubbles and scales.
Footfalls
  • The user leaves behind splashes of water, from which a ghostly shark breaches and chomps.
Critical hit effects
  • Critical strikes while wielding Kamohoali'i Kotaki will cause a spirit shark to chomp the enemy. The size of the spirit shark varies depending on the model of the enemy. This animation only appears for the following abilities:
    • Aquatic
      • Guardian: Zealot's Flurry and Wrathful Grasp
      • Warrior: Whirling Strike, Stab/Jab/Impale, Mariner's Frenzy, Parry, and Tsunami Slash
        • Berserker: Wild Whirl
      • Ranger: Surging Maw and Man O' War
        • Untamed: Vicious Pike
      • Thief: Stab/Jab/Poison Tip Strike/Deadly Strike and Nine-Tailed Strike
        • Deadeye: Malicious Deadly Strike
      • Mesmer: Feigned Surge
        • Mirage: Ambush Assault
      • Necromancer: Cruel Strike/Wicked Strike, Wicked Spiral, Dark Spear, Plague Blast, and Wave of Fear
        • Harbinger Shroud: Tainted Bolts and Dark Barrage
      • Revenant: Spear of Anguish, Rapid Assault, Igniting Brand, and Devour Brand
    • Terrestrial
      • Guardian: Daybreaking Slash, Helio Rush, Gleaming Disc, and Symbol of Luminance
      • Warrior: Mighty Throw, Maiming Spear, Spearmarshal's Support
      • Ranger: Bee's Sting, Drake's Swipe/Wyvern's Lash/Cheetah's Strike, Mongoose's Frenzy/Wolf's Onslaught, and Falcon's Stoop/Owl's Flight
      • Thief: Barbed Spear/Ashen Assault,Mantis Sting/Entangling Asp/Falling Spider, Unsuspecting Strike/Vampiric Slash/Shattering Assault, and Distracting Throw
      • Engineer: Puncturing Jab/Rending Strike/Amplifying Slice, Conduit Surge, and Lightning Rod
      • Mesmer: Psycut/Psystrike/Mind Pierce, Mind the Gap, Imaginary Inversion, Phantasmal Lancer, and Mental Collapse
      • Elementalist: Flame Spear, Blazing Barrage, Restorative Spear, Undertow, Lightning Javelin, Fulgor, and Stone Strike
      • Necromancer: Dark Slash/Deadly Slice/Sinister Stab, Perforate, Addle Isolate, and Extirpate
        • Death Shroud: Life Blast/Dhuumfire, Dark Path,Doom, and Life Transfer
        • Harbinger Shroud: Tainted Bolts, Dark Barrage, Devouring Cut, and Vital Draw
      • Revenant: Abyssal Blot
Draw/Stow effects
  • When drawn, a burst of bubbles emanates from the user's body and a echo noise is heard. The shark's mouth at the end of the spear also opens and the spearhead comes out.
  • When stowed, the spearhead retracts and the shark's mouth closes
Skill effects
  • Default projectiles are replaced with spirit shark projectiles.

Trivia[edit]

  • The name Kamohoalii is a reference to a shark god in Hawaiian mythology. Kotaki is the last name of Kekai Kotaki, a former ArenaNet concept artist who conceptualized this weapon.
  • This item was named Maw of the Deep during beta testing.

Notes[edit]

Bugs:
  • In October of 2024, the draw and stow animations were fixed after years of being broken.
  • When equipping Kamohoali'i Kotaki with your weapon unsheathed outside of water, you will show part of the blue water-like aura; this won't fade away until you mount or change areas.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Give 3 Fire Orchid Blossoms to Rosewood.
    • Rosewood can be found by heading from the  Ancient Hollow Waypoint — and using the first Bouncing Mushroom, found northwest of the waypoint. Using another Bouncing Mushroom will lead you to the ledge, with Rosewood on the left.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Trade 250 Basic Mithril Daggers to a Master Weaponsmith after unlocking the Incinerator II: The Perfected Dagger collection.

Notes

[edit]
  • Speak to a Master Weaponsmith to unlock the recipe to craft Basic Mithril Dagger.
  • Completion of this step grants access to fulfilling the requirements of Mithril Dagger Hilt Requisition and Mithril Dagger Blade Requisition.

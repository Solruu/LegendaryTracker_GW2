Refined Homestead Fiber is a crafting material used for Homestead decorations. It is obtained by refining common ingredients at the Homestead Refinement—Farm.

Acquisition[edit]

Sold by[edit]

  • Without upgrades, limited to 200 purchase per account per week. This limit increases by 100 for each DMasterwork and ERare Homestead Upgrade: Fiber Trade Capacity and 150 for each FExotic Homestead Upgrade: Fiber Trade Capacity unlocked from the Homestead Refinement—Farm.
  • Fully upgraded, limited up to 800 purchase per account per week. You can get up to 1600 Refined Homestead Fiber each week.[note 1] With two efficiency upgrades, Black Peppercorn, Vanilla Bean, Head of Lettuce, Onion, Sage Leaf, Raspberry, Saffron Thread, Pile of Flax Seeds, Orrian Truffle, and Seaweed each offer twice as much Fiber as other types.
  1. ^ This count does not include the Black Market.

Notes[edit]

  1. ^ Close the Door: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Close the Door.
  2. ^ Echo Evader: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Echo Evader.
  3. ^ Echo Evader: Eligibility isn't removed upon dying, and it's possible to get the achievement even if the event fails.
  4. ^ Mirror Memory Master: There are only 3 ward members inside the network. To get the achievement, find 3, turn them in, then find one of the 3 members again. They will be in the same place for that event. There is a dialog box for finding Rhianwyn, Prenn and Surepaw as you turn them in: "You have turned in Prenn. You need to turn in Rhianwyn and Surepaw to turn in Prenn again."
  5. ^ Pure Soul: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Pure Soul.
  6. ^ Pure Soul: Standing in the red circle zone on the ground will be applied  Malefic Darkness. Note that the 10 seconds requirement is accumulated across the fight. You can stand in and out of Beneficent Light to obtain continuous  Malefic Darkness Immunity, thus avoid getting  Malefic Darkness.
  7. ^ Separation Anxiety: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Separation Anxiety.
  8. ^ Sweet Relief: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Sweet Relief.
  9. ^ This Is Fine: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: This Is Fine.
  10. ^ This Is Fine: Use the /sit emote while in a poison field.
  11. ^ Two Birds: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Two Birds.
Bugs:
  • Sweet Relief achievement eligibility is only granted if you are present at the event location and unmounted prior to the event spawning. In addition, getting downed removes the eligibility.
  • Two Birds achievement eligibility is removed upon getting downed.
  • Close the Door achievement eligibility may be bugged if you're in the vicinity of the event when it starts. Logging out and back in fixes it.
  • This Is Fine achievement eligibility is lost if you go downstate. Re-logging can restore the eligibility.
  • Spirit Nutritionist is awarded seemingly at random. Players present on the same map and having fulfilled the requirements may or may not get achievement awarded to them. [verification requested]

Trivia[edit]

  • "For Old Times' Sake" is a reference to the Starlight Lantern achievements from Secrets of the Obscure, where players have to light a total 155 lanterns in order to obtain one piece of Obsidian legendary armor.
  • "This Is Fine" is a reference to a comic strip meme by the same name.

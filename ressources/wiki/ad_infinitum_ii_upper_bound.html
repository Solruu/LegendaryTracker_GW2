Ad Infinitum II: Upper Bound is a Legendary Backpacks collection achievement that requires items from the Fractals of the Mists in combination with some specific tasks, and can be unlocked by Kelvei after Ad Infinitum I: Finite Result. Completion awards the Proof: Upper Bound needed for crafting Upper Bound, the second tier of the precursor to the legendary back item Ad Infinitum.

Notes[edit]

  • Salvaging Finite Result will always yield 5 Balls of Dark Energy, 2 of which are needed to buy the Aetherblade Handbook and 2 to craft Upper Bound.
  • Get the Ice Elemental Core before starting the Silverwastes meta. This way you only have to do it once. Do not forget to imbue the core during the meta.

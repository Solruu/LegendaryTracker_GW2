Acquisition

[edit]
  • Trade 250 Basic Elder Focus to a Master Artificer.

Notes

[edit]
  • Speak to a Master Artificer to unlock the recipe to craft Basic Elder Focus.
  • Completion of this step grants access to The Art of Woodworking: Focus Core Edition and The Art of Woodworking: Focus Casing Edition.

Bugs

[edit]
  • The Master Artificer may continue to say that "Basic Focus Requisition" is "left to work on", even after successful completion. This can be ignored.

Achievements[edit]

Nevermore IV: The Raven Spirit Legendary Weapons 3
Take upon yourself the responsibility of raising a raven from egg to chick to adult, while you work toward taming the wild raven spirit you have already caught.
With the blessings of the Great Spirit, Raven, these two are destined to join together, earning you the Gift of the Raven Spirit, the penultimate step to attaining the legendary staff, Nevermore.

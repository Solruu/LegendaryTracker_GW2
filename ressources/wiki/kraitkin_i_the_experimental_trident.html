Kraitkin I: The Experimental Trident is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Venom, for the legendary trident, Kraitkin. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Venom Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Kraitkin I: The Experimental Trident Legendary Weapons 3
Versatile, flexible, and strong; tentacles invoke a feeling of strangeness and otherworldliness.
Meditate on the virtues of such tentacled beasts as wind riders and jellyfish as you take your first steps in attaining the legendary precursor, Venom.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Kraitkin Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Tentacles
Collection
 Collected 7 Items 3

Vision II: Farsight is a Legendary Trinkets achievement that rewards a Glimpse for the HLegendary trinket Vision.

Achievement[edit]

Vision II: Farsight Legendary Trinkets 10
Kneel and contemplate your mastery of the veil at Insights across Tyria.Can you see past the void?

Note: Only Mastery Insights in Living World Season 4 are required for this achievement.
Unlock Item: Shattered Gift of Prescience
Reward: Glimpse
20 Steps Completed 10

Objectives[edit]

  • Emote /kneel at 20 different Living World Season 4 mastery insights.
  • Doing so applies  Meditative Rest for 15 seconds.
  • Characters must remain kneeling until the effect expires to advance the achievement.

Notes[edit]

  • There are four mastery insights that are not possible to kneel at:
    • Sandswept Isles Insight: Rata Primus — due to being underwater
    • Domain of Kourna Insight: Dunlon Springs — due to being underwater
    • Domain of Kourna Insight: Ntouka Pond — due to being underwater
    • Dragonfall Insight: Above the Umbral Battlegrounds — due to being in the air

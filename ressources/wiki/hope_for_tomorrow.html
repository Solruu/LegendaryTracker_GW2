Acquisition

[edit]
  • Give a Pocketed Olmakhan Bandolier to Efi, found near the Bounty Board, south of the  Atholma Waypoint — .

Notes

[edit]
  • Grants 100 Volatile Magic when acquired.
  • Efi will not take the Bandolier if it is occupying a bag slot.
  • Giving the Bandolier to Efi will permanently remove it from your inventory.

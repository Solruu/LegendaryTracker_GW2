PvP League Tickets are a currency awarded for completing PvP Reward Tracks during PvP Seasons and Mini-seasons.

Acquisition[edit]

Including PvP League reward track and a Mini Llama trade to the League Vendor (Envelope of 10 League Tickets), player can accumulate at most 110 PvP League Tickets during a ranked season.

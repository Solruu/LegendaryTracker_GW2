WvW Skirmish Claim Ticket is a currency in the World versus World game mode, primarily earned through the Skirmish reward track and the Weekly World vs. World achievements. It is used for purchasing various rewards from the vendors, including WvW-exclusive legendary equipment.

Rewarded by[edit]

  • Defending Tier 3 Fortified towers, keeps and Stonemist castle (1) (guaranteed, required event participation levels above gold)
  • Defending Tier 2 Reinforced towers, keeps and Stonemist castle (1) (guaranteed, required event participation levels above gold)
  • Assaulting towers, keeps and Stonemist castle (1) (guaranteed, required event participation levels above gold)
  • During War Score beta events:
    • Skirmish reward (15, 5, or 5) (determined by team placement at the end of the skirmish, requires reaching 100% participation)
    • WvW Weekly Matchup rewards (150, 125, or 100) (determined by team placement at the end of the match, requires reaching Platinum match rewards (595 pips earned) to earn qualification)

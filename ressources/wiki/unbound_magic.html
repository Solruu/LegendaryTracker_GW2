“

Earned by various ways at locations heavily affected by the explosive release of magic into Tyria. Spent at vendors at those locales to purchase unlocks and rewards.

— In-game description

Unbound Magic is a currency stored in the Wallet, introduced for Living World Season 3. It is primarily earned by participating in various activities in Bloodstone Fen, Ember Bay, Bitterfrost Frontier, Lake Doric, Draconis Mons and Siren's Landing, and can be spent at merchants within those zones.

Other[edit]

  • Completing the group event Kill the Bloodstone-Crazed Devourer (10-20).
  • Completing any of the following maps:
    • Bitterfrost Frontier (100)
    • Bloodstone Fen (100)
    • Draconis Mons (100)
    • Ember Bay (100)
    • Lake Doric (100)
    • Siren's Landing (100)
  • Completing dynamic events in the above zones—given as both a reward for individual events, and as a map bonus reward
  • Gathering while using an Unbound Harvesting Sickle, Unbound Logging Axe, Unbound Mining Pick or any gathering tool with a Glyph of the Unbound applied.
  • Using   Consume Bloodstone Fragment, from Unstable Bloodstone Fragment bundles
  • Bonus chests from slaying Bloodstone Crazed creatures around Tyria (limited to once per creature per day; see Bloodstone Sightings for more details)

Notes[edit]

  • In order to gather Unbound Magic from nodes or floating orbs in the air, it is required to have completed Reaper of Magic story chapter or to have purchased either Ancient Mursaat Relic or Unbound Magic Gatherer.
    • From outside Bloodstone Fen, it will display the message "You must obtain an ancient mursaat relic to harvest unbound magic." when you can't obtain the currency. From inside the map, it will display "Complete the "Reaper of Magic" story step to learn to harvest unbound magic".
  • If your inventory is full when gathering from a resource node that has unbound magic, it will act like any other gathered object and will not be obtained until you have inventory space available.

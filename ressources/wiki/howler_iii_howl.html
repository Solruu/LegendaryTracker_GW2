Howler III: Howl is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Howl, for the legendary horn, Howler. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Howler III: Howl Legendary Weapons 3
Ghosts. Shades. Spirits of the Wild. They exist within our world and somewhere else as well, insubstantial, yet powerful.
Meditate upon their nature in your journey to craft the legendary precursor known as Howl.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Warhorn
Reward: Chest of Spirit
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 35 Items 2

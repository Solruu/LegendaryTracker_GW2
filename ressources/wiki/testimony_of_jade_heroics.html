Testimonies of Jade Heroics are a currency primarily earned by ranking up in World versus World or purchased from Skirmish Chests. They can also be found in the final container of most reward tracks, which is the only method of obtaining testimonies in Player vs. Player. Players can convert consumable Testimonies of Heroics into wallet currency by double-clicking on them. Testimony of Jade Heroics were introduced with the End of Dragons expansion and may notably be exchanged for End of Dragons Hero points as well as End of Dragons items at Heroics Notary.

Glimmering Resin weapons are level 80 FExotic weapons with Berserker's stats. These weapons are the visually enhanced versions of the Chromatic Resin weapons, featuring a shiny finish, white sparkles, and a soft pink and orange trail.

Acquisition

[edit]

Note: The player must complete the Renown Heart for the day before weapons or recipes will be offered for sale.

Crafting

[edit]
Recipe
  • The recipes can be bought from Historian Meliza and Steward Brizio in Eternity's Garden for Shadowstone Fragment (5).
  • Purchasing all recipes costs a total of 85 Shadowstone Fragment.
Crafting

After learning the respective recipe, weapons can be crafted by Artificers, Huntsmen, and Weaponsmiths with at least 400 proficiency using:

  • Their respective Chromatic Resin weapons
  • Their respective oiled components (Tier 6)
  • 10 Globs of Ectoplasm
  • 250 Research Notes

Crafting all 17 weapons requires a total of 170 Glob of Ectoplasm, 4,250 Research Note, and the corresponding Chromatic Resin weapons and oiled components.

Sold by

[edit]
  • Historian Meliza
  • Steward Brizio

Each weapon is sold for:

  • Their respective Chromatic Resin weapons
  • 6 Shadowstone Fragments
  • 12 Globs of Ectoplasm
  • 300 Research Notes

Buying all 17 weapons requires a total of 204 Glob of Ectoplasm, 5,100 Research Note, 102 Shadowstone Fragment, and the corresponding Chromatic Resin weapons.

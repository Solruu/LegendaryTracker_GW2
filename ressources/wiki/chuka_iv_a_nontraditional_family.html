Chuka and Champawat IV: A Nontraditional Family is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the Gift of Chuka and Champawat for the legendary short bow, Chuka and Champawat. It is the fourth step to obtain the weapon.

Achievement[edit]

Chuka and Champawat IV: A Nontraditional Family Legendary Weapons 3
Family isn't defined by blood or a shared name. They're who we go to when everything's against us, and we know they'll be there for us for no reason other than we're us.
Prerequisite: Scholar of Secrets
Unlock Item: Tigris
Reward: Gift of Family

Lore[edit]

Bolt
Interwoven with an impression of a dragon whose name Tyria has yet to learn, Bolt is imbued with the strength of a storm–swelling inside like a thunderous inferno, eager to aid its wielder in combat.

Graphics[edit]

Bolt is a golden sword with four dragons twisting up the blade.

Aura
  • While drawn, the user is surrounded by white, blue, and purple lightning, and white lightning periodically strikes the ground around the user.
Draw/Stow sounds
  • A long lightning sound is heard when drawing Bolt. While drawn, the weapon is surrounded by lightning.
  • A short lightning sound is heard when stowing Bolt.
Footfalls
  • The user leaves behind charred ground footprints with electrical sparks.
Kill animations
  • Bolt's Damage type is lightning. Enemies killed while wielding Bolt will display electrocuted death animations.
Skill effects
  • Default class weapon trails are replaced with lightning trails.
  • Lightning sounds are heard while attacking.
  • Ranged skills will throw Bolt instead of the default projectile. This effect changes the following skills:
    • Guardian: Zealot's Defense.
      • Dragonhunter: Spear of Justice.
    • Engineer: Grenade Barrage.
      • Holosmith: Blade Burst, Particle Accelerator, Refraction Cutter.
    • Weaver: Natural Frenzy and Shearing Edge.
    • Mesmer: Counter Blade. Clones wielding Bolt in the main hand will inherit all visual and audio effects. Clones wielding Bolt in the off hand will inherit the drawing sounds.
    • Ranger: Throw Dirt (downed skill).
      • Soulbeast: any projectile from Soulbeast pet skills such as Narcotic Spores.
    • Revenant: Essence Sap (downed skill).
      • Renegade: Citadel Bombardment.
    • Thief: Trail of Knives (downed skill) and projectiles acquired through Steal.
    • Warrior: Impale and Throw Rock (downed skill).
      • Berserker: Flaming Flurry.

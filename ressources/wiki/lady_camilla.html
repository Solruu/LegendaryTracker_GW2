Lady Camilla Einshorn is the Pact Commander's personal herald who has been documenting their rise as well as their legend and exploits and informing them of events taking place in dungeons. Her identity is revealed once she appears for the first time in person in the Domain of Kourna. Talking to her for the first time unlocks Rude Awakening, The Tyrian Service Medal and The Gandaran Service Medal.

Biography[edit]

Personal story[edit]

Lady Camilla Einshorn was a collector of tales of great daring and heroism and learned of the deeds of the hero, who would become known as the Pact Commander, from stories that had been reported to her in 1325 AE. She spoke with the Commander's mentor from Destiny's Edge who suggested that she contact the hero to add their stories to her collection.

In her first letter, Camilla did not outright name herself, preferring to go by "Your Herald" while explaining some of her background and asking for a favor from the Commander. She was curious about the reasons why Destiny's Edge had fallen apart since 1320 AE and hoped that the Commander could help her uncover what had happened while making sure the Commander was kept up to date with what Destiny's Edge members were doing in Tyria.[1]

Throughout the year, the Commander used the provided information to help Destiny's Edge fight their way through various dangerous dungeons while Camilla often contacted Destiny's Edge members to investigate matters in various locations or call some members to help others as was the case with summoning Logan Thackeray and Rytlock Brimstone to assist Zojja in the Crucible of Eternity. After defeating initial threats in each dungeon and seeing Destiny's Edge members move on to other challenges, the Commander received more letters from Camilla who urged the Commander to continue exploring the dungeons while bringing attention to lingering issues such as rising threats and surviving enemies of whom she had been informed.[2]

Living World Season 4[edit]

Lady Camilla continued chronicling the Pact Commander's actions in the following years and eventually decided to meet them face to face after traveling to the Domain of Kourna alongside other allies in the war against Palawa Joko in 1331 AE. Upon settling in Zelbahn Garrison which the Commander had cleared of hostile Awakened forces, Camilla sent a letter to the Commander and asked for a meeting. Upon encountering her idol, she could hardly contain her excitement before finally introducing herself as the mysterious Herald from the Commander's past.[3]

With the proper introductions done, Camilla proposed to create a banner wrought from the very best material which the Commander could carry into battle as a personal item to inspire both valor and terror. In exchange for making the banner and a suitable ornament for it, she asked the Commander to hunt for rare materials in Kourna and to spread their fame by participating in heroic battles throughout Tyria. With the deeds done and crafting finished by both Camilla and the Commander, she presented her idol with her ultimate gift: the Ascended Banner of the Dauntless Commander.

In 1332 AE, Camilla joined the allied forces who traveled from Central Tyria and Elona to aid the Commander in the war against the Elder Dragon Kralkatorrik. After arriving at the Auditorium of Thunderhead Keep, she witnessed the Commander's inspirational speech on the eve of battle and participated in the fight against Kralkatorrik when the Elder Dragon was lured into the keep. Although the allied forces were able to wound Kralkatorrik, the Crystal Dragon successfully killed the Commander's dragon companion Aurene before departing from the keep and left the Commander and allies mourning Aurene's passing.

The Icebrood Saga[edit]

In 1333 AE, Camilla followed the Order of the Crystal Bloom to the Eye of the North to eventually join the Commander and Aurene there, helping with arranging additional services for the halls.[4] When the Commander's allies from various factions were gathering at the Eye of the North to offer their assistance in the war against the rival Elder Dragons Jormag and Primordus, Camilla suggested that the Commander help weaponsmiths and various soldiers by boosting their morale and that the Commander should lead by example and travel to zones suffering from dragon minion incursions.

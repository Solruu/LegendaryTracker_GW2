Notes[edit]

  • Boom!, Silence the Screaming and We Are the Champions share a mastery point.
  • Respectable Researcher and More Than a Little Bit share a mastery point.
  1. ^ A Job for Warclaws: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: A Job for Warclaws.
  2. ^ Be Free, Little Bot: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Be Free, Little Bot.
  3. ^ Be Free, Little Bot: During [Group Event] Program the golems and bring them to the ruins entrance (80), leave the event area after programming a bot, without turning it in.
  4. ^ Down in the Valley: The overcharged coalesced Mists energy spawned during [Group Event] Overload the jade construct by throwing overcharged coalesced Mists energy into it while it's at full health (80) does not progress this achievement. The two versions can be distinguished by their behaviour. The overcharged version stacks up to 10 times, while the normal version only allows one charge being carried at a time.
  5. ^ More Than a Little Bit: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: More Than a Little Bit.
  6. ^ Pro Scavenger: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Pro Scavenger.
  7. ^ We Are the Champions: During [Group Event] Keep the jade construct alive by throwing coalesced Mists energy into it as it fights the titanspawn (80), help destroy the titanspawn champions the Champion Jade Armor is fighting (Champion Ruinshard Shambler, Rot Strider and Sentient Conduit) in  Yagon's Gift — by using a long range ability like a Skyscale   Fireball.

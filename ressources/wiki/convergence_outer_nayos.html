    • Public instance opens every 3 hours at XX:30, starting from 01:30 for 10 minutes until XX:40.

Participation[edit]

Participation is earned by defeating enemies and completing events. Through normal activity, this is not a mechanic that needs to be monitored.

  • Kill Kryptis Blasters — 1% participation
  • Kill normal Kryptis — 1.5% participation
  • Kill Avatars of Spite — 2.2% participation
  • Destroy Siege Engines — 10% participation
  • Kill champions — 15% participation
  • Complete events — 25% participation
  • Kill lieutenant — 25%? participation
  • Kill boss / general — 25% participation
  • Pick up Kryptis essence — No participation
    • Hand in essence — 0.2% participation per stack, 10% at 50 stacks
  • After 2 minutes of not earning participation, 1% participation is lost every 10 seconds.

Walkthrough[edit]

Mechanics[edit]

Overview of mechanics and spawning locations
  • If Zojja dies, the instance will fail and all players will be returned to The Wizard's Tower.
  • When Kryptis are defeated, they drop  Kryptis Essence on the ground. This essence then can be collected by running over it. The more essence you pick up, the more damage you do, but the less total health you have. You can carry up to 25 stacks at a time without the Essence Attunement Mastery, or up to 50 stacks with the Mastery. Going into a Downed state will cause you to drop all stacks of essence that can be picked up by others or by yourself, once you're up again.
  • Turning in essence to Zojja heals her, and powers her Resurrection Spell, that resurrects your allies who died and were turned into wisps.
  • Once the final phase begins and the Legendary Kryptis spawns, Zojja becomes immune to all forms of standard healing, and can be healed with essence.
  • Kryptis siege engines will occasionally spawn in the four corners of the map. Destroy these to prevent them damaging Zojja.
  • If a player joins a Convergence instance that has already started, they will spawn as a wisp awaiting the completion of the current resurrection spell bar.
  • If a player dies, they will enter  Wisp Form with the special skills below. Some abilities are only available with certain masteries unlocked. These players are invulnerable and may heal, buff, or revive downed players. With the Wisp Pockets Mastery, they will be able to collect Kryptis Essence and bring it to Zojja, speeding up their own resurrection. They can hit their jump keybind to move around more easily.

Phase 1: Start[edit]

It is recommended to have Superior Sigil of the Night and Superior Sigil of Demon Slaying.

The group initially spawns in a central starting island where Zojja will ask to confirm if they are ready. Once the event starts, small groups of typical Kryptis enemies will spawn. Concurrently, a 2-minute timer will begin for Kryptis reinforcements to arrive.

After a brief introduction on  Kryptis Essence, groups of Avatar of Spite will begin to spawn on the central island in a counterclockwise direction from northwest, southeast, and northeast of the central island. These Avatars of Spite chase Zojja to apply their explosion mechanic (they turn invulnerable as they begin exploding). Breaking their defiance bar will stop them in their tracks temporarily for you to burst them down. In addition, siege engines will also spawn in a clockwise direction on the other 4 corner islands of the map starting from the southwest island and should be destroyed to protect Zojja. Their seige attacks can be seen as darker orange circles within Zojja's hitbox. The Kryptis enemies that spawn around the seige engines despawn together with them being taken down. Both counterclockwise and clockwise spawns continue throughout phase 1 and 2.

Once the 2-minute timer ends, the reinforcements are 3 Kryptis Champion Avatars to be slain in order to progress to phase 2.

Zojja is also able to be healed by most spells and abilities affecting friendly targets. In addition to the healing she receives from turning in essences, it is good to have dedicated healers remain close to Zojja to keep her alive during phases 1 and 2.

Phase 2: Clear Islands[edit]

The objectives to defend Zojja from Avatars of Spite and siege engines from phase 1 continue. The Avatars of Spite on the central island will now begin spawning in a counterclockwise direction while siege engines continue their occasional clockwise spawns. Like in the previous phase, Zojja can still be healed by standard means, so having healers prioritize her defense remains important.

All four corner islands of the map will now need to be cleared. 5 Champion Kryptis Lieutenants will spawn on each of the islands followed by a Champion Kryptis General. Defeating each Champion Kryptis General will clear each island individually. Both lieutenants and generals have random Unstable Magic Abilities. When a specific island is cleared of its Champion Kryptis General, the siege engine on that island will also stop its spawn. After all four islands are cleared, phases 1 and 2 will be fully complete.

Phase 3 then begins with a legendary creature spawning on one of the 4 corner islands. The party/players who were responsible for the central island defense and healing of Zojja should also remember to completely destroy the last batch of Avatars of Spite or they will continue chasing Zojja to where she teleported (Legendary Creature spawn) and explode on her.

Phase 3: Legendary Creature[edit]

A Legendary creature (Demon Knight, Sorrow, Dreadwing, Hell Sister, or Umbriel, Halberd of House Aurkus) will now spawn. The creature chosen is random per instance, though the available options alternate each week at Tuesday 00:00[verification requested]; only Sorrow and Demon Knight are available one week, and only Dreadwing and Hell Sister are available the following week. However, Umbriel remains a constant, non-rotating third possible boss each week[verification requested].

  • Phase 3

Shared Mechanics[edit]

The Legendary creature scatters  Kryptis Essence around itself every time it loses 10% health and just before it teleports to the next random island at every 25% health interval. In addition, Zojja now becomes immune to all forms of healing except turned-in  Kryptis Essence so it is especially important to continue doing so in order to heal her and charge up her resurrection spell. She also begins taking more damage.

At its final 25% health, the Legendary Creature always teleports to the central island (starting area).

Demon Knight[edit]

Demon Knight.

Demon Knight is the easier legendary to deal with. Its most dangerous attack has a charge up animation; at this point it needs to be stunned to stop this attack. While it charges the attack, it also deals a massive amount of damage to the surrounding area. His second dangerous attack is a meteor shower. Players will be marked with numerical icons and have a yellowish aura around their computer screens. These players should move away from the group. After a short period, a cluster of meteors will crash down on the area in numerical order, with another cluster shortly after. It is preferable to NOT move towards Zojja when marked.

Bug:
One of the attacks that pulls the player away can also pull the player inside a stone or behind a texture making them stuck for the rest of the fight. Teleports do not work and you cannot /gg either. Using /stuck may fix this issue, but may take several attempts.

Sorrow[edit]

Sorrow is the harder enemy as it moves around more often. Break its defiance bar to stop it from flying around and to deal increased damage. It will fly up to summon its Sorrow's Brood. The smaller summoned wyverns often focus Zojja. When it lands, it deals a large amount of damage to those standing below. Barrier can mitigate this, but players with lower health should avoid this attack. When Sorrow is grounded, it's generally safe to stand just at the back of Sorrow, but still on top. If you are knocked into the sky, you can glide to avoid fall damage or access glide-only skills.

Hell Sister[edit]

Hell Sister.
Section-stub. This key section of the article is incomplete. You can help the Guild Wars 2 Wiki by expanding it.

Hell Sister is of medium difficulty. Her defiance bar does not unlock at any point in the encounter. Watch for a large eye above her and look away until it resolves and disappears. She will summon large aoe lines of pulsing rocks that push you back and deal damage. If you need to cross those areas, dodge or use stability. Every time the Hell Sister screams, she is casting an ability, so paying attention to your audio can be helpful. She often selects a random player to teleport to, unleashing a cone attack towards the group. Because of her area attacks and frequent movement, Zojja may take more damage against this boss than in other encounters. While it isn't incredibly dangerous for her to stand on Zojja, groups should endeavor to have the boss between themselves and Zojja so she is less likely to use her cone attacks on Zojja.

Umbriel, Halberd of House Aurkus[edit]

Section-stub. This key section of the article is incomplete. You can help the Guild Wars 2 Wiki by expanding it.

Umbriel, Halberd of House Aurkus is one of the harder bosses. His fight shares multiple mechanics with Kanaxai in the Fractals of the Mists. This fight is considered to be very condition heavy, and all of his attacks apply conditions. Autoattacks apply multiple stacks of bleeding, and during the fight Umbriel will regularly throw out axes that leave damaging area of effect fields immobilizing players as well as applying Exposed for 10 seconds. At 75%, 50%, 25% and 1% health, Umbriel will become invulnerable and spawns multiple aspects. These aspects all need to be defeated to make Umbriel's defiance bar open up, which needs to be broken in order to strip his invulnerability and continue the encounter. After the 75% Aspect split Umbriel will start using Frightening Speed roughly every 45 seconds. Five numbered AOE circles will be placed on players' locations, indicating the order in which Umbriel will attack. Shortly after these markers appear, Umbriel will jump from location to location, downing any players caught within the AOE. After the 50% Aspect split Umbriel will also periodically start casting Dreadful Gaze, dealing damage and applying Fear and 25 stacks of Vulnerability to all players who are looking at him. This mechanic is marked by a large eye appearing above him and the edge of the screen turning yellow, requiring players to turn around and look away from Umbriel until the attack resolves. Finally, Umbriel will start combining Frightening Speed and Dreadful Gaze together after 25%.

Mechanics[edit]

Section-stub. This key section of the article is incomplete. You can help the Guild Wars 2 Wiki by expanding it.

A summary of mechanics may also be found in-game by speaking with the Convergences Portal

  • Enemies are stronger
  • Zojja requires more essence to revive allies [verification requested]
  • Players gain the  Unstable Attunement effect when they start a convergence with challenge mode activated.
    • When a player is revived from a wisp, they lose Unstable Attunement and can no longer be revived from wisp form.
    • Once the legendary Kryptis has been drawn out, all wisps will be revived regardless of their Unstable Attunement status [verification requested] and everyone will be granted the effect once more.
  • The Noblesse Oblige mastery is disabled. (Noblesse Oblige removes downed penalties to revive targets and increases resurrection speed towards lower level targets.)
  • The Convergence must be completed within the time limit (30:00:00) to log the time and obtain bonus rewards.

Version history[edit]

Patch Changes
March 11, 2025
  • (Undocumented) The Convergences encounter from Secrets of the Obscure has been renamed to Convergence: Outer Nayos.
November 19, 2024
  • Updates have been made to Guild Wars 2: Secrets of the Obscure Convergence challenge mode rewards.
  • Convergence: Outer Nayos (Challenge Mode)
    • A new weekly achievement has been added to successfully complete unique encounters in Convergences: Outer Nayos with challenge mode active. Rewards include a new container with gold and a chance at exceptionally rare items.
    • Introduced stacking rewards based on the legendary enemy reaching health thresholds. Includes experience, karma, coins, and loot bags.
    • Updated the Convergence Challenge Mode: Bronze, Silver, and Gold chests to each award fine, masterwork, and rare rift essences.
    • Convergence Challenge Mode: Gold chests give different amounts of rewards based on the legendary enemy.
September 10, 2024
  • Reduced the damage and bleeding stacks of Umbriel's Rending Storm.
  • Reduced Umbriel's maximum health.
June 04, 2024
  • (Late Note) Challenge mode Convergences clear-time scoring has been updated to better ensure server stability. Players will see a message displayed in the event window as well as at the top of their screen when clear-time logging has begun. When the message disappears from the event window, it is safe to leave the map.
May 21, 2024
  • A challenge mode difficulty tier for Convergences has been added, as well as a new Convergences boss.
  • The World Boss Portal Device available from the Black Lion Trading Company now includes notifications and teleporting options for Convergences. To access this option, players must first unlock the Wizard's Tower by completing chapter 6 of the Guild Wars 2: Secrets of the Obscure story.
April 16, 2024
  • Fixed a client crash in Convergences.
February 27, 2024
  • Two new final bosses, the Dreadwing and the Hell Sister, have been added to Convergences. These two will cycle on a weekly cadence with Sorrow and the Demon Knight as the possible final encounters of a Convergence.
December 12, 2023
  • Convergence Creature Crusher: Progress for this achievement no longer gives a fixed amount when at 200% player participation. Adjusted the amount of progress granted when crushing different kinds of Kryptis. The achievement text will be updated in a future release.
  • Siege Engine Stomper: Adjusted the amount of progress granted when stomping siege engines.
November 29, 2023
  • Fixed an issue that prevented wisp transformations from being properly removed in Convergences.
November 28, 2023
  • (Late Note) Kryptis essence in Convergences will no longer disappear after falling into water.
November 14, 2023
  • Fixed an issue that caused reward chests claimed from repeatable Convergence achievements to reset the respective achievement's progress.
November 07, 2023
  • Convergence: Outer Nayos has been added to the game.

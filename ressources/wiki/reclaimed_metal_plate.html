Notes[edit]

  • It takes 38 Reclaimed Metal Plate (in addition to the map currencies) to complete the Plated weapon Collection, 2 for each one-handed weapon, and 3 for each of the two-handed weapons.
  • 30 Reclaimed Metal Plates are required for the Gifts of Dedication for each set of Perfected Envoy armor; a total of 90 for all three sets.
  • Assuming you buy the Provisioner Tokens solely with Reclaimed Metal Plates, an additional 50 are needed per piece of Perfected Envoy armor; 300 per weight class, or 900 for all three sets.

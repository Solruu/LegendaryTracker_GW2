Obtained by refining basic log materials at the Homestead Wood Refiner.

— In-game description

Refined Homestead Wood is a crafting material used for Homestead decorations. It is obtained by refining common wood types at the Homestead Refinement—Lumber Mill. Up to 200 refined Homestead wood can be initially obtained by refining wood every week. This limit can be permanently increased by purchasing wood trade capacity upgrades at the Homestead Refinement—Lumber Mill.

Refined Homestead wood can also be bought with coins on the Black Market tab of the Homestead Refinement—Lumber Mill. The black market prices increase after each purchase but reset back to the original price after every weekly reset. Refined Homestead wood bought this way doesn't count towards the weekly cap.

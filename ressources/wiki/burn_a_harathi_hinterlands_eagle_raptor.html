Acquisition

[edit]
  • Interact with the campfire or pick up any of the torches on the ground outside of Blackhold Mine Camp. A Veteran Eagle Raptor can be found to the east of  Blackhold Mine Camp — on a forested hill between the two roads.
    • You can mount up or waypoint with the torch, but you will drop it if you enter the water or use a transformation.

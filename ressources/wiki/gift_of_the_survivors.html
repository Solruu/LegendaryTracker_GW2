In the Mystic Forge, combine a Gift of the Survivors and a Gift of the People with two other legendary gifts to create a legendary item:

• Aetheric Anchor—Gift of Insight and Gift of the Elders
• Selachimorpha—Gift of Castoran Mastery and the Precursor Agaleus
• Endless Summer—Gift of the Hylek and Gift of Rays

Purchased from the Castaway Agnes in Hullgarden in Shipwreck Strand. — In-game description

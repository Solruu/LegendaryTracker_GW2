Frenzy III: Rage is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Rage, for the legendary harpoon gun, Frenzy. This collection is the final step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Frenzy III: Rage Legendary Weapons 3
Quaggan are a peaceful people, but when provoked, quaggans become engines of anger and fury. Usually, though, quaggans like friends. Quaggans want give them friendship bracelets.
Will you be quaggans' friend? Consider your reply carefully as you seek to attain the legendary precursor Rage.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Harpoon Gun
Reward: Chest of Quaggan Friendship
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 18 Items 2

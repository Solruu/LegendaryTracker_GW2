Aurora is a legendary trinket obtainable through Living World Season 3. All six episodes must be unlocked in order to craft this item. Progress can be started by obtaining the Sentient Seed from the Gleam of Sentience.

Lore[edit]

Aurora
When Mordremoth's power seeped into the earth, some sought to harness it, control it - bringing forth new dangers. Heroes faced those enemies head on with a spark of bravery and a warrior's might.

Achievements[edit]

The following achievements are required to acquire the necessary materials for crafting:

  •  Legendary Trinkets: Aurora: Awakening — for Gift of Valor, required to craft a Gift of Sentience.
    Unlocked by purchasing the Sentient Seed for 1,000  from the Gleam of Sentience converter, obtained by combining the following 4 converters in the Mystic Forge, each rewarded for completing their respective achievement:
  • Sentient Anomaly from Out of the Shadows: Conspiracy of Dunces — Find the journals chronicling the White Mantle's plans. (5)
  • Sentient Aberration from Rising Flames: Token Collector — Collect all the mursaat tokens that were scattered around Ember Bay. (40)
  • Sentient Oddity from The Head of the Snake: Cin Business — Find goods that might interest Cin—some that he lost, some that he wants to sell. (5)
  • Sentient Singularity from Flashpoint: Lessons Learned — Find all 14 of the golemancer's lost recordings in Draconis Mons. (6)
  •  Legendary Trinkets: Aurora II: Empowering — for Spark of Sentience. Unlocked after completing Aurora: Awakening.

Graphics[edit]

Primary article: Legendary trinket#Visual effects

Aurora, Coalescence and Vision — share the same visual effect, which depends on the number of these trinkets worn and displayed at the same time.

  • With weapons stowed, an ethereal aura will appear around the player.
  • With weapons drawn, four orbs or bubbles will orbit the player's head.

Spark of Sentience

Legendary Trinkets: Aurora II: Empowering — Collect all 21 items to create a Spark of Sentience. (10)

  • Requires 21 Xunlai Electrum Ingots
  • Unlocked after completing  Aurora: Awakening

Crafting Gift of Draconic Mastery requires 250 of each map-specific currency from the Living World Season 3 maps. If not already completed, the following  Side Stories (Living World Return) achievements can help obtain the required currencies:

  •  Return to Out of the Shadows: Return to Bloodstone Fen Tier 3 (7 achievements) — 5 Blood Ruby Strongboxes (250 )
  •  Return to Rising Flames: Return to Ember Bay Tier 3 (6 achievements) — 5 Sacks of Petrified Wood (250 )
  •  Return to A Crack in the Ice: Return to Bitterfrost Frontier Tier 3 (6 achievements) — 5 Winterberry Strongboxes (500 )
  •  Return to The Head of the Snake: Return to Lake Doric Tier 3 (7 achievements) — 5 Jade Shard Strongboxes (500 )
  •  Return to Flashpoint: Return to Draconis Mons Tier 3 (7 achievements) — 5 Fire Orchid Strongboxes (250 )
  •  Return to One Path Ends: Return to Siren's Landing Tier 3 (7 achievements) — 5 Orrian Pearl Strongboxes (250 )

Completing the  Aurora: Awakening achievement for Gift of Valor and to unlock  Aurora II: Empowering requires an additional:

  • 50  to purchase Pristine Blood Ruby
  • 100  to purchase Seraph Seraph Protector and Seraph Bloodstone Savant's Staff
  • 76  for the collections involved in obtaining The Wayfarer's Henge
  • 200  to purchase the back item for Supporter of the Gods

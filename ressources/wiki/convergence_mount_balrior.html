Getting there[edit]

  • The portal to this instance can be found in the Harvest Den in Lowland Shore, near the  Bonfire Lodge — point of interest. Look for the on the map to clarify the location even further.
    • Private instance can be created with a squad at any time. It requires Unstable Rift Motivation to open.
    • Public instance opens every 3 hours at XX:00, starting from 00:00 for 10 minutes until XX:10.

Participation[edit]

The amount of rift essences and Janthiri Gear Boxes in the map meta event chest depends on the level of personal participation. The minimum reward is given at 30%, and scales linearly up to the maximum at 100%. Participation can go up to 200% (to give leeway for decay), but no further rewards are given.

  • Kill titanspawn:
    • Normal — 0.11%
    • Veteran — 0.3%
    • Elite — 1%
    • Champion — 2%
  • Pick up  Calibrated Essence — 0.2%
    • Return essence to Distant Memory — 0.3% per stack, 15% at the max of 50 stacks
    • Destroyed walls do not give participation, but drop 6 essences providing a total of 3%
  • Deliver supplies to a thumper — 5%
  • Participate in any event — 10%
    • Including escorting the dolyak, defending base camp, building thumpers, defending thumpers, defeating the sapper, and defeating the group leader.
  • Tracking Titan Stench — 0.25%
  • Defeat the legendary titan — 30%
    • Adds killed during the fight do not give additional participation
  • After 3 minutes of not gaining participation, your accumulated percentage will go down by 1.5% every 10 seconds.

Phase 1: Escort[edit]

  • While escorting Distant Memory and Pack Dolyak up the mountain, titanspawn enemies will be encountered on the path up. There will be two waves of Titanspawn Bombers that will rush to the Pack Dolyak to explode and kill it. The Pack Dolyak can be healed back up, sped up with  Swiftness or  Superspeed, but will always stop when one of the waves spawns. Reaching Base Camp will finish this phase. The NPCs will talk and build for a while, preparing for the next phase.
    • Tip: After reaching the Base Camp, during the preparation phase, players can move ahead along the mountain path and break the Unstable Wall to pre-stack the  Calibrated Essence effect.

Phase 2: Thumpers[edit]

  • After a base is set, Supply Crates can be taken from Calibration Thumper Supplies from where the Pack Dolyak once was. These supplies need to be taken to the other 5 locations on the map. While dragging these supplies, players will receive the  Attached Supplies effect, showing them that they are pulling supplies. If they move too far away from their supplies, they will receive the  Rope Tension effect, which causes the rope to break at four stacks. Once at the other marked locations, the Sniff skill will become available. This skill starts the construction of the thumpers when used. A total of 5 supplies need to be pulled to the construction sites to create a thumper. Not all 6 of the thumpers need to be built, but it is better and quicker to build and protect them all. After a thumper is built, it needs to be protected. Standard titanspawn or Titanspawn Bombers will spawn around these thumpers, trying to destroy them. The event to protect a thumper lasts for three minutes, after which a Titanspawn Disruptor will spawn. Killing this Titanspawn Disruptor will lock in this thumper as completed, and no more enemies will spawn there. Thumpers can also be repaired by bringing more supplies to them. While there are 5 other locations with thumpers, the original thumper in the base also needs to be protected. The thumpers will generate  Calibrated Essence that can be gathered and given to Distant Memory to progress further in the event.

Phase 3: Legendary Titan[edit]

A legendary titan will now spawn. In public instances, the titan rotates each week at Tuesday 00:00. [verification requested]

Shared Mechanics[edit]

  • At every 10% of its health, the titan will drop up to 15  Calibrated Essences for players to collect.
  • At 75%, 50%, and 25%, the boss will flee from the current area. In these sub-phases, players have to track the Titan Stench along the mountain path. The route is random in each instance. (100%Ura's Throne > 75% and 50% altered between Decima's Stormfield or Greer's Lab > 25%Ura's Throne.) Once the progress bar has been filled by tracking the stench, the   Aetheric Jump can be used to teleport players to the target location. The player will have the special action key skill,   Cut Parachute, which disappears upon touching the ground.
    • Tip: While the recommended mount for tracking the stench is the Warclaw, players can use any kind of mount, such as the Roller Beetle.

Decima, the Stormsinger[edit]

Decima, the Stormsinger, in this instance, shares multiple mechanics with herself in the Mount Balrior raid. Many important mechanics, like red arrows, orange arrows, and green circles, may only become visible when Character Model Limit is increased. Several conduits spawn around the arena, and the laser fences will shift in different patterns during the fight.

Arrow mechanics can add one charge to the conduits (up to a maximum charge of three), indicated by an electrified appearance and an AoE around them. The AoE size increases per charge. Players will be punished for each maximally charged conduit during each defiance bar phase. Additionally, each maximally charged conduit that gains charge applies 1 Exposed to all players.

Each charged conduit releases sparks that must be stopped by a minimum number of players, indicated by the indicator above green circles. The minimum number increases for each charge on the conduit. Decima gains 1 Charge for each spark that reaches her.

List of notable skills

Primary article: Decima, the Stormsinger#Convergence: Mount Balrior for full skill lists.

  • The red arrow is a deadly attack that will instantly kill any players in its path. Targets the furthest player, the targeted player must position themselves behind a conduit to avoid this attack. (It's possible to use the Warclaw or Skyscale's Combat Launch to avoid being downed.)
  • Fluxlance (orange arrow) is similar to the red arrow attack, but less dangerous. Targets the furthest player, the targeted player must position themselves to avoid the conduits, as arrows striking a conduit will cause the AoEs to expand significantly and the minimum number for green circle is increases.
    • In later phases (after 75%), The number of arrows is increased from 1 to 5 and alternates between the orange arrow and the red arrow: 5 Orange → 1 Red + 4 Orange → 5 Orange, etc.
  • Sparking Aura (green circle), Conduits with charge periodically release dancing sparks that move toward Decima. Sparks lose some health and stop moving temporarily when enough players are standing in their aura. If a dancing spark reaches Decima, it is absorbed and grants her some 1 Charge.
    • The minimum number of players for green circle is increased if the arrows (Both red and orange arrows) hit the conduits.
  • Seismic Crash (knockback), When Decima leaps and slams the ground, any players inside her hitbox will be instantly killed. After landing, the immediate vicinity erupts with jagged rocks and pyrite, knocking back any player in the area. Players that impact a conduit will take extra damage and ricochet in another direction. Using defensive options like dodging, blocking, or Invulnerability to avoid both the initial impact and the knockback is recommended.

Greer, the Blightbringer, in this instance, similar to himself in the Mount Balrior raid combined with the Of Mists and Monsters meta event. Most of his attacks are in front of him so it is advised to stay behind him. While fighting, multiple green circle will spawn on the players. People need to be standing in these circles or a damaging AoE will spawn on the ground. An AoE will spawn around the enemy multiple times from different attacks. The Greer's AoE is the boon corrupt skills and the vine walls cannot be pass through. Most of his projectile can be block or reflect.

After each of chasing phases (at 75%, 50%, and 25%), Greer will be spawn with his greerlings, Gree, the Bringer and Reeg, the Blighter that have to dealt with before fighting with Greer. They will disappear after loss 25% of their health.

Version history[edit]

Patch Changes
June 03, 2025
  • Update 2:
    • Fixed an issue that could cause Ura to become stuck in Convergence: Mount Balrior.
  • Ura joins the Convergence: Mount Balrior boss rotation
  • The marker over Distant Memory's head has been updated.
  • Decima should no longer be able to damage wisps.
March 25, 2025
  • Thunder strikes on Mount Balrior will no longer reference Decima when she is not on the map.
March 12, 2025
  • Greer, the Blightbringer, will now properly appear as the final boss of Convergence: Mount Balrior on alternating weeks.
March 11, 2025
  • Update 2:
    • Greer's Blob of Blight will no longer target wisps in Convergence: Mount Balrior.
  • A new version of Greer, the Blightbringer, will now automatically replace Decima on alternating weeks.
  • Increased the interaction range for the Convergence: Mount Balrior brazier in Harvest Den.
  •  Titan Stench will now display matching icons on the compass and in the game.
  • Turning in a single calibrated essence will now give 7 seconds of  Wizard's Blessing instead of 6.
  • Red circles on the map will now follow the bosses in the Convergence.
  • Titanspawn in the "Defend the Maw of Balrior thumper" event will now spawn on the correct side of the breakable wall.
  • Dig site markers will now appear after the base camp has been set up.
  • Decima's dancing sparks will no longer display a raid marker on the map.
  • Ropes will now break at 4 stacks of  Rope Tension instead of 3.
November 25, 2024
  • Fixed an issue in which the  Lava effect would not be removed when leaving lava areas.
  • Fixed an issue in which some of Decima's attack indicators would be culled, regardless of settings.
  • Fixed an issue in which wisps could be damaged by Decima's Fulgent Aura.
  • (Late Note) Removed an in-development item from the Convergence.
November 19, 2024
  • Convergence: Mount Balrior has been added to the game.

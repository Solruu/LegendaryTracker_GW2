Acquisition[edit]

  • Dropped by any level 80 Branded creature while this section of the Brandstone Research achievement is active.

Notes[edit]

  • You will need 10 Branded Residue in order for Yasna to accept them.
  • You must actually speak to Yasna first, before these will start dropping.
  • This will drop from any Branded creatures in Path of Fire maps, not just the ones in the Domain of Istan.
  • Branded creatures in Thunderhead Peaks do not drop any Residue.

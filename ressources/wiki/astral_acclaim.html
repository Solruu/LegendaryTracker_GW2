Astral Acclaim is an account-wide currency acquired through the Wizard's Vault, a streamlined rewards system. All accounts with at least one level 80 character have access to the Wizard's Vault and the currency, though some tasks are exclusive to the Guild Wars 2: Secrets of the Obscure expansion.[1] Up to 1300 Astral Acclaim can be stored, though it's possible to exceed the cap if a player claims rewards when they have fewer than 1300 Astral Acclaim.[2] Stored Astral Acclaim persists between Wizard's Vault refreshes.[3]

Notes[edit]

  • Astral Acclaim and Wizard's Vault progress cannot be purchased with gems or real money.[1][4]
  • When beyond the limit of 1300 of Astral Acclaim and trying to claim an objective that would increase the amount further displays the following message: "You have too much Astral Acclaim. Spend some to allow this objective to be claimed."
    • Note that unclaimed Astral Acclaim chests disappear over their respective reset (daily/weekly/seasonally), even if after the reset the same objective is available again (e.g. weekly "Complete 10 events").
    • If including points carried over from the previous week, it is possible to have to a max of 1749 AA at once.
  • Added with the August 22, 2023 game update.
  • Whatever remaining Astral Acclaim you have accrued at the end of a season will roll over to the new season and can be used in those new rewards.

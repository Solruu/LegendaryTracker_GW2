Silver Ores are resource nodes found in level 15-40 areas. They can be mined at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Silver Ore (guaranteed)
  • Amethyst Nugget
  • Carnelian Nugget
  • Lapis Nugget
  • Peridot Nugget
  • Spinel Nugget
  • Sunstone Nugget
  • Topaz Nugget
  • Ruined Ore Chunk (guaranteed) — if an Iron Mining Pick or better is not used

Rewarded by[edit]

  • Completing most events in Bava Nisos. Dynamic events reward 1-2, while select group events reward 2-4.

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • A Titanic Voyage (32) (Total, full event with 100% participation)
    • Defeat Lextalion the Merciless (3-4)
    • Defeat the Champion Titanspawn (2-4)
    • Defeat the swarm of titan minions (2-4)
    • Destroy jade constructs, titanspawn, and other mursaat defenses (1-2)
    • Help Kyung complete her ritual by consoling spirits, finding lost mursaat mementos, and driving away titanspawn (1-2)
    • Help Rysta preserve and sort the mursaat texts before they degrade completely (1-2)
    • Kill titanspawn to power Livia's spell (2-4)
    • Set up the gastric dispersion devices (1-2)
    • Use debris in the environment to help destroy the titan organ sacs (1-2)
    • Use medicine to reduce the titan's inflammation and open the pathway (1-2)

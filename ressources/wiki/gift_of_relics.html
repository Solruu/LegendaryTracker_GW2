Note[edit]

  • A grand total of 18,750 Lucent Crystal is required to forge the necessary amount of facets for this gift, or 187,500 Lucent Motes. If buying all (or most) of either material off the Trading Post from Sell Orders, one should calculate gold values several listings ahead in order to get the true final value, due to the sheer volume of Lucent required for this gift.

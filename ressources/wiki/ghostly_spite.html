Acquisition

[edit]
  • Found inside a chest at the end of the Old Oola Lab mini-dungeon. The start of the dungeon is found north of  Akk Wilds Waypoint — , towards the hero challenge.
    • There is a Matrix Imprint Lock which can be opened with an Energy Crystal after speaking to Researcher Orl about it. You can buy the Energy Crystal off the Trading Post, or kill random foes in the area in order to obtain in and gain access to the lab.
    • If you have the springer mount, it's possible to get to the chest (northeast of the  Akk Wilds Waypoint — ) without going through the mini-dungeon.

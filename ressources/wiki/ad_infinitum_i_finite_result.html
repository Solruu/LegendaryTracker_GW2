Ad Infinitum I: Finite Result is a Legendary Backpacks collection achievement that requires items from Fractals of the Mists in combination with some specific tasks, and can be unlocked by talking to Kelvei, the Legendary Observer in the Mistlock Observatory. Completion awards the Proof: Finite Result needed for crafting Finite Result, the first tier of the precursor to the legendary back item Ad Infinitum.

Notes[edit]

  • A total of 2 Balls of Dark Energy are needed for the entire legendary collection since salvaging the Finite Result and Upper Bound gives more Balls of Dark Energy back, and you can salvage the Fractal Capacitor (Infused) after making that for the last Ball of Dark Energy needed to buy Aetherblade Diploma in the final collection.
  • Prototype Fractal Capacitor doesn't need to be bought again if already acquired before the collection was introduced. Higher tiers will automatically unlock the collection.
  • The Prototype Fractal Capacitor needs to be kept and upgraded to Prototype Fractal Capacitor (Ascended) and Beta Fractal Capacitor (Infused) to complete Ad Infinitum II: Upper Bound
  • There is a current bug that some users are encountering that upon completion of the Aquatic & the Swampland Mote Challenge, will not grant you the achievement. Anyone experiencing the bug should first be sure they are looting from or interacting with the chest that spawns after the boss dies, as both motes are given from the final chest.

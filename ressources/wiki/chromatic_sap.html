Chromatic Sap is the map-specific collectible token from Shipwreck Strand. Chromatic Sap and Aether-Rich Sap can be converted at a 1:5 ratio with no limit:

  • Consuming 1 Chromatic Sap awards 5 Aether-Rich Sap.
  • 5 Aether-Rich Sap can be used to purchase 1 Chromatic Sap at any Castoran renown vendor.

Acquisition[edit]

Gathered from[edit]

  • Small chance to be gathered from any resource node in Shipwreck Strand with the Resourcefulness mastery unlocked.
  • Aetherlocation Treasure (1-2) (chance, in Shipwreck Strand, depending on Prosperity Unending mastery)
  • Large Obscured Chest (3) (guaranteed, from chests in Shipwreck Strand, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Long-Lost Keepsake Box (3) (guaranteed, from chests in Shipwreck Strand, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Medium Obscured Chest (2) (guaranteed, from chests in Shipwreck Strand, requires Obscured Riches mastery)
  • Small Obscured Chest (guaranteed, from chests in Shipwreck Strand, requires Obscured Riches mastery)

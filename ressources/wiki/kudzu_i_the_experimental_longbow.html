Kudzu I: The Experimental Longbow is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Leaf of Kudzu, for the legendary longbow, Kudzu. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Kudzu Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Kudzu I: The Experimental Longbow Legendary Weapons 3
Through flood, fire, and famine, the power of the plants survive. The story of its resilience and quiet power can be found in every root and stem, in every petal and leaf.
Meditate on the power of such things as you begin your journey to create the legendary precursor, Leaf of Kudzu.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Kudzu Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Blooms
Collection
 Collected 13 Items 3

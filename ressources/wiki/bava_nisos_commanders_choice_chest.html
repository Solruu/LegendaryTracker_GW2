Bava Nisos: Commander's Choice Chest is a gizmo awarded once per day per account for participating in the meta event A Titanic Voyage in Bava Nisos.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Limited to once per day per account.

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Assault the North Lung
    • Assault the South Lung
    • Assault the titanic heart

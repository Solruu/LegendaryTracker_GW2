HOPE is a legendary pistol crafted in the Mystic Forge, through various materials and tasks tied to the Heart of Thorns expansion. As a culmination of Hylek alchemy, Asuran design and traditional crafting techniques, its creation will task you with learning tribal secrets and testing lost experiments throughout Tyria.

Aura
  • The arm of the user wielding the weapon becomes slightly transparent and is covered in distinct lightning similar to that surrounding the weapon when fully drawn.
Draw/Stow effects
  • Upon drawing the weapon, the user's hands will light up.
  • When the pistol is drawn, luminescent colored fluid flows inside the tubes on each side of the weapon, filling them up. When full, one emits icy particles, the other fiery sparks; along the length of the barrel flow electric sparks.
  • The pistol emits a sound of "powering up" when drawn, and of "shutting down" when stowed.
Footfalls
  • The footprints alternate randomly between red and blue splattered squares, each containing patterns of circuitry, and emit lightning for a short time when created.
Skill effects
  • All projectile sounds are changed to "laser" sounds. Random shots have more muffled sounds, as if the pistol is overheating. With continuous use of the pistol, these occur at increased frequency and their sounds become progressively more distorted.
  • With continuous use of the pistol, the aura around the arm will grow brighter, and then dim back to its original state if the pistol ceases firing.
  • After using a skill, the fluids are expelled and refill like when drawn.
  • Projectiles are changed into pinkish-red plasma balls surrounded by blue energy particles which emit a dim green light. This effect changes the following skills:
    • Guardian: Through the Heart, Hail of Justice, Spear of Justice
    • Elementalist: Scorching Shot, Raging Ricochet, Searing Salvo including fragments, Soothing Splash, Frigid Flurry, Frozen Fusillade, Electric Exposure, Dazing Discharge, Aerial Agility (chain), Piercing Pebble, Shattering Stone including shards, Boulder Blast
      • Weaver: Molten Meteor, Frostfire Flurry, Purblinding Plasma, Echoing Erosion, Enervating Earth
    • Engineer: Fragmentation Shot, Poison Dart Volley, Static Shot, Grenade Barrage, Grappling Line (downed skill), Throw Junk (downed skill), and the projectiles from Static Shot that bounce between enemies
    • Thief: Vital Shot, Sneak Attack, Bola Shot, Repeater, Shadow Strike, Unload, Head Shot, Black Powder, and Trail of Knives (downed skill)
    • Mesmer: Magic Bullet and Phantasmal Duelist
    • Necromancer: Vicious Shot, Weeping Shots, Vile Blast, Life Blast, Dhuumfire, and Dark Path
      • Harbinger: Dark Barrage, Tainted Bolts.

Notes[edit]

  • It is recommended that you do not attempt to stat change your precursor item with the standard stat swap recipe in the Mystic Forge. While you will be provided with an item with the desired stats, it will have been replaced with a generic ascended item that cannot be used as a legendary precursor.
  • HOPE is an acronym for Hylek Optimized Projectile Experiment.
  • The red fluid in the piping is the Transcendent Energy, and the blue liquid is the Alchemical Catalyst. Both are from the HOPE IV: The Catalyst collection.
  • The Barrel of the pistol appears to be the Advanced Ammunition Cylinder from the Prototype precursor.
  • As with other legendary weapons, many of the Engineer skills have no visual effects when using kits.
  • You cannot use a Gift of Desert Mastery to craft HOPE.

See also[edit]

  • HOPE I: Research - the first step towards crafting the precursor, whereby you apprentice yourself to a Hylek master alchemist.
  • HOPE II: Development - where you hone your crafting skills through iteration.
  • HOPE III: Prototype - here you will research Asuran technologies and wild energy sources.
  • HOPE IV: The Catalyst - On this final phase you will create the last 3 components for the legendary pistol: the Transcendent Energy, the Alchemical Catalyst and the Catalytic Converter.

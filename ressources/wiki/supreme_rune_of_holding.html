Used in[edit]

Crafting or buying 24, 28, and 32 slot bags:

24-slot bag
  • 1 20-slot bag of the desired type (Pillager's bag type) / 2 20-slot bags of the desired type (other bag types)
  • 1 Supreme Rune of Holding
  • Other materials depending on bag type
28-slot bag
  • 1 24-slot bag of the desired type
  • 3 Supreme Rune of Holding (Boreal or Pillager's bag type) / 5 Supreme Rune of Holding (other bag types)
  • Other materials depending on bag type
32-slot bag
  • 1 28-slot bag of the desired type
  • 8 Supreme Rune of Holding (Boreal or Pillager's bag type) / 12 Supreme Rune of Holding (other bag types)
  • Other materials depending on bag type

Acquisition

[edit]
  • The goggles can be found east of Buried Archives. Players can reach them by starting from  Shipwreck Rock Waypoint — . Head west and climb up the cliff to end up at the vista. Follow the cliff line to the north until you spot the diving goggles on the ground.
    • While the area the goggles are in is actually The Shipyard and not the Mausollus Sea, jumping off from where they are will award the item.
    • To perform the trick, use the skills in the following order: 1 - 2. It is necessary to let your character complete the animation before using the next skill, but it is not needed to finish the channeling part.

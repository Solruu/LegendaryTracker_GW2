Notes[edit]

Bug:
The effect does not trigger on single target projectile attacks.
Anomaly:
The internal cooldown will trigger if the target is hit, even if no boons are removed.
  • Since the June 27, 2023 patch this effect also applies to defiant foes.
  • There is only a superior version of this sigil.
  • 25 Needed to construct the initial set of Requiem Armor.

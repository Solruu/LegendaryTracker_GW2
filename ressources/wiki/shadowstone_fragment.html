Rewarded by[edit]

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Clear enemies from the ruins (2)
    • Clear the poison vines (2)
    • Defend the cocoons from beetles (2)
    • Destroy the Inquest bridge (2)
    • Help Steward Cecco unfuse animals of their seer influence (2)

    • Shackles of the Ancients (5) (Repeatable chest, doubled with Prosperity Unending)
    • Use Imren's spellbook to aid the Inquest-controlled stewards (2)
  • Completing the Assist the Stewards of Galdra around the forge (2).

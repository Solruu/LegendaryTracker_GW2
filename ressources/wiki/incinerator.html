Graphics[edit]

Incinerator is a dagger whose blade is made of a flame like that of a welding torch. The blade also emits light when drawn, illuminating the area around the user in dark areas.

Aura
  • The arm wielding Incinerator is on fire.
Draw/Stow effects
  • When drawn, fire twirls around the user and a furnace-like sound plays.
Footfalls
  • The user leaves behind footprints that looks like the ground is cracking from lava.
Kill animations
  • Enemies killed by Incinerator will display burning death animations.
Skill effects
  • Attacks cause a cosmetic burst of flame on the target.
  • Attacks with projectiles will use a unique sound.
  • Ranged skills will throw Incinerator instead of the default projectile. This effect changes the following skills:
    • Spellbreaker: Throw Rock (downed skill).
    • Ranger: Crippling Talon and Throw Dirt (downed skill).
      • Soulbeast: Any projectile from Soulbeast pet skills such as Narcotic Spores.
    • Thief: Dancing Dagger, Death Blossom's whirl finishers, Shadow Shot, projectiles acquired through Steal, and Trail of Knives.
    • Necromancer: Deathly Swarm, Dhuumfire, Dark Path and Life Blast.
      • Harbinger: Tainted Bolts and Dark Barrage.
    • Elementalist: Dragon's Claw and Vapor Blade.
      • Tempest: Overload Fire's whirl finishers.
    • Virtuoso: Flying Cutter, Bladecall, Bladesong Harmony, Bladesong Sorrow, and Bladesong Dissonance.
Anomaly:
Necromancer: Necrotic Bite loses the Bite animation.

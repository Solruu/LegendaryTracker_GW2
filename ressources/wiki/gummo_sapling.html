Gummo Saplings are resource nodes found in level 15-45 areas. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Soft Wood Log (1-2) (guaranteed)
  • Cinnamon Stick (1-2)
  • Hidden Stash
  • Walnut (1-2)
  • Ruined Log (guaranteed) - if an Iron Logging Axe or better is not used

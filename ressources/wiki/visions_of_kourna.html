Visions of Kourna is a Legendary Trinkets achievement that is a part of the Vision I: Awakening collection.

Achievement[edit]

Visions of Kourna Legendary Trinkets 10
Explore the Domain of Kourna for greater insight.Eternal life, either blessing or curse, squandered by greed and the thirst for power.

Note: Completion of this achievement requires completion of the Riding Skyscales achievements.
Unlock Item: Trance Stone
Collection
 7 Steps Completed 10

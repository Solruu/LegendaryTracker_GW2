Mist Shard armor refers to a group of armor sets that can be acquired through content related to the War Eternal release.

Acquisition[edit]

  • A single set can be obtained as an achievement reward for completing the following achievements:
    • Chasing Waterfalls (Helmet)
    • Putting Out Fires (Shoulders)
    • Championship Bout (Coat)
    • My Beautiful Infrastructure (Gloves)
    • Life in the Underworld (Leggings)
    • Cutting Weeds (Boots)

Additional pieces can be crafted using the recipes gained from the same achievement(s). Each piece requires 25 Mistborn Mote.

Related achievements[edit]

  • War Eternal: Championship Bout — Defeat all champions in Dragonfall. (5)
  • War Eternal: Chasing Waterfalls — Look behind 9 waterfalls in Dragonfall. (9)
  • War Eternal: Cutting Weeds — Complete 10 events for the Mist Wardens in Melandru's Lost Domain. Required to gain access to the Mist Warden quartermaster. (3)
  • War Eternal: Life in the Underworld — Complete 10 events for the Olmakhan in the Underworld. Required to gain access to the Olmakhan quartermaster. (3)
  • War Eternal: My Beautiful Infrastructure — Participate in 10 bridge defense or bridge repair events in Dragonfall. (3)
  • War Eternal: Putting Out Fires — Complete 10 events for the Order of the Crystal Bloom in the Burning Forest. Required to gain access to the Crystal Bloom quartermaster. (3)
  • Visions of Dragonfall: Vision of Equipment: Dragon Champion Armor — Complete one weight class of Tier 1 Mist Shard armor. Purchased from the Traveling Elonian Trader in Dragonfall.

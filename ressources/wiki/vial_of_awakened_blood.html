Notes[edit]

Anomaly:
Hovering over it will display "0/x in Material Storage" in its description even though there is no slot for it there.
  • Despite being flagged as a crafting material, this item is not used directly in any crafting recipes.
  • Blood collection count from killing Awakened is random and not a guaranteed drop.

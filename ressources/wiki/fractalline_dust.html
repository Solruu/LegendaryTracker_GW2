Fractalline Dust is a trophy earned from quickplay in Fractals of the Mists and achievements related to Fractal Incursions. It is automatically consumed and provides progress for Incursive Investigation achievements required for the HLegendary gloves Eikasia, Mists-Grasper. After that, further Fractalline Dust can be obtained to get Fractalline Sparks.

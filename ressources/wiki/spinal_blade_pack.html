Spinal Blade Pack is a back item created in the Mystic Forge using components earned while participating in Escape from Lion's Arch. It can be upgraded numerous times, with each upgrade step adding another pair of blades to the pack. It is important to note that transmuting this item will NOT prevent it from being upgraded contrary to the item's description.

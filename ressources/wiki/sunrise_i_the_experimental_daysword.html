Sunrise I: The Experimental Daysword is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Dawn, for the legendary greatsword, Sunrise. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Dawn Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Sunrise I: The Experimental Daysword Legendary Weapons 3
Tyria is a land filled with peoples worth protecting. Defend the innocent, rescue the threatened,
and consider your role as a hero in order to take your first steps in creating the legendary precursor known as Dawn.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Sunrise Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Hope
Collection
 Collected 15 Items 3

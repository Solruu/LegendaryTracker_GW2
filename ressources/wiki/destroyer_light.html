Acquisition

[edit]
  • Bring the Destroyer Mirror to the western end of the Churrhir Cliffs. To get there, head northeast from  Champion's Dawn Waypoint — , then use a springer to climb the cliffs.

Notes

[edit]
  • This item does not enter the player's inventory.
  • The interaction spot can be a bit buggy, try logging out to character select and back if the interaction point does not appear.

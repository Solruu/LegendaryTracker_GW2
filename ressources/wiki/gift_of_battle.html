Notes[edit]

  • Can be given to a cat in the Mistwrought Vault, as part of the Hungry cat scavenger hunt.

Trivia[edit]

  • Prior to the Spring 2016 Quarterly Update, this item was purchasable for 500 Badges of Honor, but was subsequently changed to be only obtainable through the Gift of Battle Item Reward Track.
  • This item originally had a different description: "A gift used to create legendary weapons."

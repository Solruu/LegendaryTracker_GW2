Acquisition

[edit]
  • With the Jade Maw's Eye Lens and a Crystal Lodestone in inventory, interact with the Metal Forest hero challenge at  The Wealdwood — and hold the crystal to the light orb.

Notes

[edit]
  • It is suggested that all of the other crystals are brought at the same time since they all have to be used with the same hero point.
Bug:
You can't have Quartz Crystal in your inventory. If you have, the point won't offer you the option to interact the crystal, only to transform quartz crystals.

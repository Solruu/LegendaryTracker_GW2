Rich Mithril Veins are resource nodes found in level 70-80 areas. They can be mined ten times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Mithril Ore (1-2) (guaranteed)
    • Ruined Ore Chunk (guaranteed) — if a Mithril Mining Pick or better is not used
  • Chance for one of the following:
    • Beryl Crystal
    • Chrysocola Crystal
    • Emerald Crystal
    • Opal Crystal
    • Ruby Crystal
    • Sapphire Crystal
  • Chance for Auric Sliver (with Exalted Gathering in Auric Basin)
  • Chunk of Granite (in the Crystal Desert)

  • Mount Maelstrom
    • The Mire Sea - Guaranteed Rich Vein deep underwater north-west of the Restless Deeps POI
Ruins of Orr
  • Cursed Shore
    • Cathedral of Silence - possible Rich Vein next to the hero challenge
    • Cathedral of Silence - possible Rich Vein east of the Buried Archives point of interest
    • Cathedral of Verdance - Rich Vein east of waypoint
    • Compass Plaza - possible Rich Vein west of waypoint
    • The Shipyard - Rich Vein underwater west of Meddler's Waypoint
    • Mausollus Sea - Guaranteed Rich Vein West of Shipwreck Rock Waypoint
    • The Narthex - possible Rich Vein west of waypoint
    • Winterknell Isle - Rich Vein next to the Corrupted Tree
  • Malchor's Leap
    • Jinx Isle - Guaranteed Rich Vein southwestern corner
    • Midwater Hollows - Guaranteed Rich Vein northeast of Upsilon Test Facility
    • Mirror Bay - possible Rich Vein southeast of waypoint
    • Valley of Lyss - possible Rich Vein east of Hope Falls
    • Karst Plains - possible Rich Vein north-east of Doric's Waypoint, directly north of the hero challenge
    • Pyrite Peninsula - possible Rich Vein at the cliffs north-west of the POI The Wealdwood
  • Straits of Devastation
    • Terzetto Bay - Rich Vein at the end of The Long Way Around
    • Triumph Plaza - possible Rich Vein near Chapel of Lady Glaive point of interest
    • Lightfoot Passage - possible Rich Vein on the floor of the vista cave.
Shiverpeak Mountains
  • Frostgorge Sound
    • Bore Lynch - possible Rich Vein in southwest
    • Dimotiki Waters - Rich Vein west of Zuckermaloo
    • Frostwalk Tundra - possible Rich Vein northwest of waypoint
    • Grimstone Mol - Guaranteed Rich Vein at the Iron Horse Mines point of interest, accessed through an underwater tunnel that starts to the southwest of Iron Horse Mines or a tunnel south of the Slough of Despond Waypoint (see two image guides in gallery)
    • Grimstone Mol - possible Rich Vein underwater west of Slough of Despond Waypoint (not the same one as the one in the underwater tunnel, both can be present)
    • Grimstone Mol - possible Rich Vein underwater south-east of the Iron Horse Mines point of interest, roughly at the height of the "Despond Precipice" text on the map (not the same as the other two rich vein locations at Grimstone Mol)
    • Shattered Ice Floe - Rich Vein end of Shattered Ice Ruins jumping puzzle
    • Trionic Lattice - posssible Rich Vein northeast part of area
    • Trionic Lattice - possible Rich Vein southeast of the "Trionic Lattice" text on the map
    • Snow Climb - possible Rich Vein far south-west of Path of Starry Skies Waypoint, directly north of phase 1 of the Defeat the Claw of Jormag event
Crystal Desert
  • Crystal Oasis
    • The Sinking Ruins - Possible Rich Vein near Hero Point.
  • The Desolation
    • Shadow Arcade - Rich Vein slighly NW of the POI.
Jahai Bluffs
  • Crescent's Prosperity

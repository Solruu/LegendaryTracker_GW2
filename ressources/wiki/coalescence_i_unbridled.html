Coalescence I: Unbridled is a Legendary Trinkets collection achievement to gain the Hateful Sworl, a requirement for creating legendary ring Coalescence. This collection is the first step in obtaining this item.

Achievement[edit]

Coalescence I: Unbridled Legendary Trinkets 9
Your predilection for violence has attracted the attention of malevolent energy. Perhaps you can sooth its bloodlust somehow.
Prerequisite: Defeat a raid boss. (Only available to owners of Path of Fire.)
Reward: Hateful Sworl

Copper Ores are resource nodes found in level 1-15 areas. They can be mined at least three times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Copper Ore (1-3) (guaranteed)
  • Garnet Pebble
  • Malachite Pebble
  • Tiger's Eye Pebble
  • Turquoise Pebble
  • Zinc Ore (Only if the collection Incinerator I: The Experimental Dagger is active)

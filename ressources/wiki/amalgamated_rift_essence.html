“

An amalgamation of rift essences used for crafting.
The recipe can be purchased from Lyhr in the Wizard's Tower.

Hint: Refined from 250 Fine Rift Essences, 100 Masterwork Rift Essences, and 50 Rare Rift Essences. Essences can be found by hunting rifts.

— In-game description

Amalgamated Rift Essence is an Fexotic crafting material obtained by crafting using raw Rift essence. It's used in the crafting of the open world legendary armor, the legendary spear Klobjarne Geirr, the legendary backpack Orrax Manifested and the legendary ring Endless Summer.

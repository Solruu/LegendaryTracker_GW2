The Predator I: The Experimental Rifle is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Hunter, for the legendary rifle, The Predator. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, The Hunter Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

The Predator I: The Experimental Rifle Legendary Weapons 3
The rifle, whether it fires bullets or magical rays, is one of the most complex weapons in Tyria. Understand its wonders by researching the technology of such weapons as you take your first steps
in the personal journey to create the legendary precursor known as The Hunter.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: The Predator Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Technology
Collection
 Collected 17 Items 3

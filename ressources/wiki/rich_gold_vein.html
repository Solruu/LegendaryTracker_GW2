Rich Gold Veins are resource nodes found in level 40-55 areas. They can be mined ten times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • If a Steel or better mining pick is used
    • Gold Ore (1-2) (guaranteed)
    • Amethyst Lump
    • Carnelian Lump
    • Lapis Lump
    • Peridot Lump
    • Spinel Lump
    • Sunstone Lump
    • Topaz Lump
  • If a Steel or better mining pick is not used
    • Ruined Ore Chunk (guaranteed)

Detailed locations guide[edit]

Ascalon
  • Blazeridge Steppes
    • Behem Gauntlet - Possible Rich Vein inside the Behem Gauntlet jumping puzzle.
    • Gastor Gullet, Possible Rich Vein near Behemian Grand Kraal POI
    • Heretic Plain, Possible Rich Vein east of Expanse Waypoint in Wolf Cave (North of Degun Shun).
    • Kindling Bridge/Monument Grounds - Possible Rich Vein south of Kindling Bridge and west of Monument Grounds, amidst the ruins of the Northern Wall.
    • Relliatus Canyon - Possible Rich Vein east of Brandview Waypoint, across the river.
    • Relliatus Canyon - Possible Rich Vein south west of Brandview Waypoint in spider cave.
    • Splinter Hills - Possible Rich Vein at the northeast end of the river, in a cave guarded by a Veteran Canyon Spider.
    • Tawny Ridge - Possible Rich Vein southwest of Fina Duskmaker.
    • Terra Carorunda [&BAECAAA=] - Guaranteed Rich Vein far to the northeast of Craze's Folly, in the forest area guarded by a Veteran Boar.
    • Tumok's Last Stand - Possible Rich Vein behind a small waterfall southeast of Tumok's Waypoint.
  • Fields of Ruin
    • Highden Caves [&BEwBAAA=] - Guaranteed Rich Vein near the dead River Drake Broodmother, guarded by five Shadow Imps; killing all the imps summons a Veteran Shadow Imp.
Kryta
  • Harathi Hinterlands
    • Trebuchet Bend - Possible Rich Vein at Drakken's Den.
    • Modniir Gorge [&BLEAAAA=] - Guaranteed Rich Vein at War King's Greatcamp.
    • Hidden Ourobon - Possible Rich Vein in the second part of the cave, south of where the wall is opened during Help Shining Blade Kimber find her husband.
Shiverpeak Mountains
  • Dredgehaunt Cliffs
    • Heimdahl's Last Stand - Possible Rich Vein in a small forest directly north of the Sorrow's Embrace Waypoint, guarded by a patrolling Veteran Snow Troll.
    • Kapellenburg [&BFsCAAA=] - Guaranteed Rich Vein north of the Tomb of Blackpowder, guarded by a Veteran Dredge Mining Suit.
    • Wyrmblood Lake - Possible Rich Vein immediately north of the Leopard's Snarl Shrine.
    • Kolkorensburg - Possible Rich Vein south of the Ghost Stones.
    • Ratatosk
      • Possible Rich Vein near the Shiniest Skritt Treasure.
      • Possible Rich Vein north of Nottowr Fault Waypoint.
    • Tribulation Rift - Possible Rich Vein southwest of the Sorrow's Embrace Waypoint.
    • Ulukk's Hunger - Possible Rich Vein inside the cavern.
  • Lornar's Pass
    • Demon's Maw [&BOYAAAA=] - Guaranteed Rich Vein directly south of Demon's Maw Waypoint, guarded by a Veteran Basilisk.

Rich Platinum Veins are resource nodes found in level 45-70 areas. They can be mined ten times.

Detailed locations guide[edit]

Please note that zones can have one or more "guaranteed" (permanent) Rich Platinum Vein (node) locations as well as one or more "possible" (random) Rich Platinum Vein (node) locations. The total of each type ("guaranteed" or "possible") are listed next to the zone name once known and confirmed. Each of the "possible" (random) Rich Platinum Vein locations in a zone are chosen from the list by the server and can change with each map instance. All of the characters on your account can mine the Rich Platinum Veins (they appear per character not per account). The locations below are all confirmed locations.

Ascalon
  • Fireheart Rise
    • Onager Bivouac [&BB4CAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein at the end of the cave usually guarded by a Veteran Fleshreaver.
    • Havoc Steppes [&BB0CAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein at Atrox Castrum.
    • Rebel's Seclusion - [&BBoCAAA=]
      • Guaranteed Rich Vein east of the waypoint.
      • Possible Rich Vein can also spawn in addition to the "guaranteed" (permanent) one above.
    • Pig Iron Quarry - Possible Rich Iron Vein halfway through the jumping puzzle (climb to the top) on top of a column at the southeast corner of the island. Often appears with 1-2 regular nodes.
  • Iron Marches - Two Guaranteed (Permanent) Rich Platinum Nodes and Two Possible (Random) Rich Platinum Nodes
    • Ebbing Heart Run [&BO8BAAA=] - Guaranteed Rich Vein at Heartsond Falls.
    • Crystalwept Groves [&BOoBAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein on the southeast corner at the edge of map and north of hero challenge.
    • The Infestation [&BO4BAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein on the northern part of the Branded Devourer cave, at the Grundallion's Lair point of interest.
    • Gladefall Run - Guaranteed Rich Vein next to vista.
    • Lamprey Grottoes - Possible Rich Vein at the end of the Lamprey Grottoes cave, behind Zzzig hero point.
    • Soldier Mesa - Possible Rich Vein next to the Imp Power Essence hero challenge
    • Victium Moors [&BO0BAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein far south of The Blasted Hearth POI at the border between Victium Moors and Scorchlands.
Kryta
  • Bloodtide Coast
    • Mentecki Pass [&BKsBAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein in southern area of cavern
    • Mole's Head [&BKoBAAA=] south of Glorious Drill Collective #4 - Guaranteed Rich Vein accessed via the green area to the south which contains a Veteran Spider. You can also encounter a Giant Blood Ooze inside the cave or a Champion Canyon Spider Queen outside the cave.
    • Moriarity's Hold [&BKcBAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein south of heart.
    • Jelako Cliffrise [&BK8BAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein in the passage full of Eagle Griffons (between Jelako Waypoint and the Vista) at the back behind a vine curtain.
    • Challdar Gorges [&BKoBAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein in the tunnel north-east of the Assist Crusader Aisling heart.
    • Laughing Gull Island [&BKgBAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein at the bottom of the ship where Taidha occasionally teleports to during Kill Admiral Taidha Covington meta event.
    • Flooded Castavall [&BK4BAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein at bottom. May be guarded by Champion Risen Commander. Also Hero Challenge (commune).
    • Remanda Waypoint [&BKcBAAA=] - Possible Rich Vein at Bloody Bill's Base POI.

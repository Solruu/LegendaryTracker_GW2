The Druid Stone is an achievement surrounding the story of the druids in Draconis Mons. Completion will unlock the corresponding achievement Awakening the Druid Stone.

Achievement[edit]

The Druid Stone Flashpoint 3
Melandru's blessing to the most devout of her followers was to become one with nature and leave behind their physical construct.
Pay tribute to and learn from the druid spirits in Draconis Mons to cultivate your relationship with nature through the Druid Stone.
Reward: Druid Stone
Collection
 Collected 7 Items 3

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Notes
1 Volcano's Heart Item Consumable Consume the Volcano's Heart earned from completing Heart of the Volcano, the fifth and final story step of the Flashpoint episode.
2 Druid Runestone Item Trophy Obtain a Druid Runestone by combining 5 Druid Runestone Fragments. Fragments can be bought once a day in Draconis Mons from each of the 4 Renown Heart NPCs for 1,050  and Supplymaster Hanjo for 1,000 .
3 The Druid's Story Item Trophy Bring a Druid Runestone to Kodama, located inside  Druid's Grotto — in the Savage Rise.
4 A Flower for Kodama Item Trophy Bring a Fire Orchid Blossom to Kodama, located inside  Druid's Grotto — in the Savage Rise.
5 A Flower for Liriodendron Item Trophy Bring a Fire Orchid Blossom to Liriodendron, located in  Peace Grounds — in the Savage Rise.
6 A Flower for Broadleaf Item Trophy Bring a Fire Orchid Blossom to Broadleaf, located on a high ledge in the western end of the Savage Rise.
7 A Flower for Rosewood Item Trophy Bring a Fire Orchid Blossom to Rosewood, located upon a rock bridge above  Ancient Hollow Waypoint — in the Ancient Hollow.

Notes[edit]

  • The single Druid Runestone needed for this collection can be immediately crafted by buying the daily Druid Runestone Fragment from all 5 merchants, requiring the 4 renown hearts to be completed.
    • A total of 16 Druid Runestones are needed for the full collection chain - only one can be obtained per day.

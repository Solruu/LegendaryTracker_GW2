Acquisition[edit]

  • Fractal Research Pages (28) can be combined into a journal by double clicking on the stack.

A single Fractal Journal requires 28 Fractal Research Page which can be acquired from these sources:

  • 1  per Daily recommended fractals (Tier 3 and higher player has access to 3  per day)
  • 2  per Weekly Fractal Fighter's Cache (Tier 4 player has access to at most 8  per week, tier 3: 6 )
  • 1  per Challenge Mode Fractals for Kinfall Fractal, Nightmare Fractal, Shattered Observatory Fractal, Sunqua Peak Fractal, Silent Surf Fractal and Lonely Tower Fractal. (Tier 4 player has access to 6  per day)

Notes[edit]

  • Fractal Journals can be traded in for the following collection items:
    • Ascalonian Fractal Field Research Paper in the Urban Battleground Fractal for Ad Infinitum I: Finite Result
    • Molten Alliance Fractal Field Research Paper in the Molten Boss Fractal for Ad Infinitum II: Upper Bound
    • Thaumanova Fractal Field Research Paper in the Thaumanova Reactor Fractal for Ad Infinitum III: Unbound
    • Aetherblade Fractal Field Research Paper in the Captain Mai Trin Boss Fractal for Legendary Backpack: Ad Infinitum
  • They must be traded in the correct order. Ad Infinitum I is required to be complete before II is unlocked. So, the first journal you make has to be turned in at the end of the Urban Battleground fractal.

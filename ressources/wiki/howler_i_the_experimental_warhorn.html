Howler I: The Experimental Warhorn is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Howl, for the legendary warhorn, Howler. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Howl Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Howler I: The Experimental Warhorn Legendary Weapons 3
You hear their cries on the wind in the mountain valleys and the far places of the world. They are the wolves, fierce as individuals, and united as a pack.
Consider their combined power as you channel the wolf's hunting spirit in your initial steps to attack the legendary precursor, Howl.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Howler Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Prey
Collection
 Collected 22 Items 3

Notes[edit]

Anomaly:
It is not necessary to kill the ambient creature to get the associated collection trophy. Landing a hit while in the wolf form is sufficient.
  • Only ambient creatures count for the collection. If an ambient creature has a level, it will not count towards the collection.
  • For maximum efficacy, Queensdale has 14 of the 21 and Diessa Plateau has 9 out of 21 of the needed ambient creatures. Additionally, many of the creatures can appear randomly in the Toxal Bog within Brisban Wildlands.

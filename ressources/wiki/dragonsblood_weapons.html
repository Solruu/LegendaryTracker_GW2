Dragonsblood weapons are level 80 FExotic weapons introduced in All or Nothing. Acquiring these weapons is part of the Journeyman of the Forge collection achievement. These weapons are required to obtain respective Heroic Dragonsblood weapons.

Acquisition[edit]

Recipes[edit]

Dragonsblood Weapon Recipe Books can be purchased for 500  and 5  each, only after completing the Dragonsblood story step. The spear, Dragonsblood Impaler, does not have a recipe and cannot be crafted.

  • Recipe: Diviner's Orichalcum-Imbued Inscription is required to craft the inscription for each weapon.
Item Level Vendor Area Zone Cost Notes
 Dragonsblood Weapon Recipe Book Forge Master Hilina The Forge Thunderhead Peaks 5  + 500  Requires completing the story step Dragonsblood.
 Dragonsblood Weapon Recipe Book Kynon The Forge Thunderhead Peaks 5  + 500  Requires completing the story step Dragonsblood.
 Recipe: Diviner's Orichalcum-Imbued Inscription Kynon The Forge Thunderhead Peaks 3  + 500 

Once the recipes are unlocked, weapons can be made by Artificers, Huntsmen, and Weaponsmiths. Each requires:

  • 10 Branded Masses
  • A Diviner's Orichalcum-Imbued Inscription (recipe requires 3 Exquisite Serpentite Jewels and 50 Branded Masses)
  • Two T6 weapon components (such as Orichalcum Pistol Barrel and Ancient Pistol Frame for the pistol)

Notes[edit]

  • A minimum of 960 Branded Mass, 48 Exquisite Serpentite Jewel, 8000 Volatile Magic, and 80 Laurel is needed for the full FExotic weapon-set.
    • 500 Volatile Magic and 5 Laurel per weapon recipe.
    • 60 Branded Mass and 3 Exquisite Serpentite Jewel per FExotic weapon.
Anomaly:
The weapons can not be salvaged with a Research Kit

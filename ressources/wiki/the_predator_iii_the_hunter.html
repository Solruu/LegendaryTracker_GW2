The Predator III: The Hunter is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Hunter, for the legendary rifle, The Predator. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

The Predator III: The Hunter Legendary Weapons 3
One hunts by stealth and slyness, and one targets only the most valuable prey. Show your ability to stalk your prey and use the knowledge gained as you craft the legendary precursor The Hunter.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Rifle
Reward: Chest of Bounty Hunting
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 24 Items 2

Notes[edit]

  • There is another spy kit (Order of Whispers Spy Kit) that is cheaper than the Spy Kit which only costs 16 . But the Order of Whispers Spy Kit does not advance this achievement.

Notes[edit]

  • Having a Skyscale is required for certain parts of the collection. Requires Rift Repair mastery.
  • You will need 6 Memory Essence Encapsulator, which in total cost
    • Glob of Ectoplasm x 300
    • Orichalcum Filigree x 60
    • Lesser Vision Crystal x 6
    • Xunlai Electrum Ingot x 18
  • The Memory Essence Encapsulators must be held for the following 6 time-based metas
    • Domain of Istan: Sunspear Uprising
    • Sandswept Isles: The Specimen Chamber
    • Domain of Kourna: Defeat the Legendary bounty (Troopmarshal Olori Ogun) - bounty not timegated meta
    • Jahai Bluffs: Destroy the Death-Branded Shatterer
    • Thunderhead Peaks: Thunderhead Keep (meta event)
    • Dragonfall: Defeat ley-infused champion after Break the Crystal Dragon meta event
  • The following Living World Season 4 achievements or the PvP/WvW Reward Tracks that correspond to each of their awarded items must be completed:
    • "Daybreak" Mastery (or Domain of Istan Reward Track unlocked by S4E1C6 Daybreak The First City)
    • "A Bug in the System" Mastery (or Sandswept Isles Reward Track unlocked by S4E2C3 A Kindness Repaid)
    • "Long Live the Lich" Mastery (or Domain of Kourna Reward Track unlocked by S4E3C2 Forearmed Is Forewarned)
    • "A Star to Guide Us" Mastery (Palawa Joko's Staff Head cannot be obtained from any reward track)
    • "All or Nothing" Mastery (or Thunderhead Peaks Reward Track unlocked by S4E5C1 Scion & Champion)
    • "War Eternal" Mastery (or Dragonfall Reward Track unlocked by S4E6C1 The End)
  • The following items from each Living World season 4 episode must be acquired
    • Unlock the skins for any six Astral weapons (and prerequisite time-gated achievement), which at minimum cost a total of:
      • Kralkatite Ore x 3,060
      • Powdered Rose Quartz x 3,000
      • Glob of Ectoplasm x 330
      • Eye of Kormir x 300
      • Pulsing Brandspark x 12
    • Reinforced Olmakhan Bandolier
      • Received from A Bug in the System: Lasting Bonds: What Comes Next — Collect 8 items to help Efi. (5)
    • Banner of the Commander, crafted using recipe from Lady Camilla, which requires
      • Inscribed Shard x 200
      • Orichalcum Ingot x 25
      • Unidentified Dye x 20
      • Glob of Ectoplasm x 15
      • Ancient Wood Log x 10
      • Bolt of Gossamer x 5
      • Spool of Gossamer Thread x 100
      • Olmakhan Latigo Strap x 5
    • Elegy armor
      • Received from completing A Star to Guide Us: The Convergence of Sorrow I: Elegy — Console an Awakened armorsmith and change the heart of a Sunspear. (5)
    • Any six Dragonsblood weapons, requiring a minimum of
      • Branded Mass x 360
      • Exquisite Serpentite Jewel x 18
      • Glob of Ectoplasm x 30
      • Laurel x 30 and Volatile Magic x 3000 in total for weapon recipes.
    • A full set of Mist Shard armor of a single weight class, which are rewards from the following achievements:
      • Mist Shard Helm Box — War Eternal: Chasing Waterfalls — Look behind 9 waterfalls in Dragonfall. (9)
      • Mist Shard Shoulders Box — War Eternal: Putting Out Fires — Complete 10 events for the Order of the Crystal Bloom in the Burning Forest. Required to gain access to the Crystal Bloom quartermaster. (3)
      • Mist Shard Coat Box — War Eternal: Championship Bout — Defeat all champions in Dragonfall. (5)
      • Mist Shard Glove Box — War Eternal: My Beautiful Infrastructure — Participate in 10 bridge defense or bridge repair events in Dragonfall. (3)
      • Mist Shard Leggings Box — War Eternal: Life in the Underworld — Complete 10 events for the Olmakhan in the Underworld. Required to gain access to the Olmakhan quartermaster. (3)
      • Mist Shard Boot Box — War Eternal: Cutting Weeds — Complete 10 events for the Mist Wardens in Melandru's Lost Domain. Required to gain access to the Mist Warden quartermaster. (3)

Frenzy I: The Experimental Harpoon Gun is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Rage, for the legendary harpoon gun, Frenzy. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, Rage Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

Frenzy I: The Experimental Harpoon Gun Legendary Weapons 3
Fishing in Tyria is no pleasant day spent safely on the shore, waiting for a fish to take the bait. It is fought on the fish's home turf, a battle of fisherman against fish. And the fish fight back.
Consider this as you take your first steps in crafting the legendary precursor Rage.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: Frenzy Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Fish
Collection
 Collected 6 Items 3

Mists Research is a World vs World achievement. Completing it unlocks the ability to purchase the Mistwalker Infusion from Dugan.

Walkthrough[edit]

First talk to Dugan and go through the dialogue chain starting with Hey, what's that strange glow around your armor?. This unlocks the rest of the achievement. Now visit all the Hero challenges in the areas indicated in the achievement and interact with them. Select the dialogue option Kneel down and let the mists surround you. to complete the objective. If the Hero point has not yet been gained, you will only get the option to do so ( Absorb what you can from this place of power.). Then only after interacting with the Hero challenges a second time will the dialogue option appear. If you have not yet charged your daily Charged Quartz Crystal you will be prompted to do so before you can select the dialogue option.

Objective Notes
1 Speak to Dugan. Can be found in the spawn area of any team in Eternal Battlegrounds or any of the three Borderlands.
2 Investigate the Krait Reliquary in Darkrait Inlet. Interact with the hero point in the south of Eternal Battlegrounds.
3 Investigate the Dredge Furnace in Molevekian Delve. Interact with the hero point in the north-west of Eternal Battlegrounds.
4 Investigate the Harpy Nesting Grounds in Orgath Uplands. Interact with the hero point in the north-east of Eternal Battlegrounds.
5 Investigate the Heart of the Stone inside Stonemist Castle. Interact with the hero point in Stonemist Castle, in the center of Eternal Battlegrounds.
6 Investigate the Centaur Totem in the Spiritholme. Interact with the hero point in the north-east of Blue Alpine Borderlands.
7 Investigate the Skritt Shiny in the Titanpaw. Interact with the hero point in the north-west of Green Alpine Borderlands.
8 Investigate the Mysterious Energy in Darra's Maze. Interact with the hero point in the south of the central area of Red Desert Borderlands.

Dialogue[edit]

Talking to Dugan
Greetings, Mist Warrior!
Just let me see what you have for sale.
Hey, what's that strange glow around your armor?
Ah, yes. Are you familiar with the places of power scattered around these battlegrounds?
Why, of course! I know these lands like the back of my hand!
Well, some time back, I noticed a strange sort of mist coming from them. After some careful investigation, I was able to find a way to contain it and add it to my armor.
Any chance you could do the same for my armor?
I suppose I could, although you'll probably want to go get a feel for the mist yourself first. Just to make sure you don't have any...odd reactions.
Odd reactions? Why do you say that?
Well, I seem to be pretty unaffected. But there was this one fellow who tried the same thing as me, and lets just say it didn't turn out so well for him.
Go on...
As soon as the mist touched him—POOF! He turned into a rabbit! Actually, I think I saw him hopping about around here yesterday...
Uhhh... Well, maybe I'll be back. Or maybe not. Thanks.
Good luck!
Leave.
Uh, yeah. Definitely. For sure. (Same as "Why, of course! I know these lands like the back of my hand!")
Leave.
Interacting with the Hero challenges
You've gained all you can from this place of power.
Kneel down and let the mists surround you. (Temporarily give the character the same look as the Mistwalker Infusion, while kneeling down and have the same pulsing animation as when completing a Hero challenges.)
Leave.

Notes[edit]

  • The kneeling animation can be interrupted and does not need to be completed to gain the objective.

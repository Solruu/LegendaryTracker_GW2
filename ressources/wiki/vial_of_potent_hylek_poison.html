Acquisition

[edit]
  • To acquire the recipe, interact with the Alchemy Supplies at the end of the Tears of Itlaocol mini-dungeon in Wychmire Swamp, Caledon Forest. The recipe will automatically be learned on your account. The entrance is south from  Falias Thorp Waypoint — in the  Dengatl Grounds — .

Notes

[edit]
  • Crafting this item will briefly poison your character.
  • This item is removed from your inventory after finishing the test with Atlalli.
  • This recipe may have been soulbound at the time of the original release, but should now be account-wide. If you find your Huntsman does not have the recipe after taking a different character through the dungeon, you will have to complete it on the appropriate character.
  • The Vial may only be used near Atlalli, Master Alchemist. It grants the effect  Hylek Poison Coating that lasts for 2 minutes and 30 seconds. The effect has no actual combat effect beyond completing Atlalli's test.

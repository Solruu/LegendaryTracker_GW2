Visions of Sandswept Isles is a Legendary Trinkets achievement that is a part of the Vision I: Awakening collection.

Achievement[edit]

Visions of Sandswept Isles Legendary Trinkets 10
Explore the Sandswept Isles for greater insight.Sandswept Isles, where the shortsighted seek immediate power and knowledge.

Note: Completion of this achievement requires completion of the Riding Skyscales achievements.
Unlock Item: Trance Stone
Collection
 7 Steps Completed 10

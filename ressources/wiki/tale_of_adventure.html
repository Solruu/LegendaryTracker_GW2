  • First playthrough rewards 1 Tale of Adventure per chapter, the final episode of the additional chapter of Guild Wars 2: Janthir Wilds rewards 2 Tales of Adventure.
    • 14 total for the complete End of Dragons story.
    • 13 total for the complete Secrets of the Obscure story.
    • 23 total for the complete Janthir Wilds story.
  • Replaying a story chapter afterwards rewards 5 Tales of Adventure once per day.
    • 70 total per day if all chapters of End of Dragons story were replayed.
    • 65 total per day if all chapters of Secrets of the Obscure story were replayed.
    • 95 total per day if all chapters of Janthir Wilds story were replayed.
Bug:
A Legacy Damned doesn't reward addtional Tales on replay.
  • Story steps that have a "skip to checkpoint" option will still give you Tales (without having to replay the entire story step).

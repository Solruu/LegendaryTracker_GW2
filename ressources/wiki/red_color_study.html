Acquisition

[edit]
  • Take a Canvas, Paint Brush, and a Jar of Red Paint to the Scenic Vantage Point inside  Maelstrom's Core — , northwest of  Maelstrom's Waypoint — in Maelstrom's Bile, Mount Maelstrom.
    • The waypoint near the Scenic Vantage Point can often become contested, especially during the Megadestroyer event; if this happens, use  Magmatic Waypoint — or  Avernan Waypoint — instead.

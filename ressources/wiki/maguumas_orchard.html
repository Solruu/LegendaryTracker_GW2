Acquisition

[edit]

Plant 3 Fire Orchid Seeds in the fertile soil in the garden, maintained by a centaur and skritt, of Uplands Oasis in Dry Top.

Location

[edit]

Travel north from  Vine Bridge Waypoint — to find the fertile soil.

Notes

[edit]
  • After planting the seeds, 3 Primordial Orchids will be available for daily harvest in the garden.

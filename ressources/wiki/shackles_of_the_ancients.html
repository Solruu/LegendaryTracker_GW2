Shackles of the Ancients is a level 80 meta event that occurs in Eternity's Garden, around The Forge and The All Seer.

Objectives[edit]

  • The All Seer is calm.
  • The All Seer has awoken! Alliance forces are preparing to seize the forge.
  • Allies are gathering at Pilgrim's Rest.
  • Preparations begin in: 5:00.

The Forge[edit]

  • Allies are attempting to sieze the forge from Inquest Control.
    • [Group Event] Capture and hold the western chamber (80)
    • [Group Event] Capture and hold the northern chamber (80)
    • [Group Event] Capture and hold the eastern chamber (80)
    • Take control of all three chambers in the forge.
    • Defeat the augmented Inquest champions.: x/3
  • The Inquest will succeed in: 15:00
  • The Alliance has taken the forge and are attempting to repair it.
    • [Group Event] Repair the western energy conduits (80)
    • [Group Event] Repair the southern energy conduits (80)
    • [Group Event] Repair the eastern energy conduits (80)
  • The damaged forge will explode in: 15:00.
  • Gather at the center of the forge to cast the Light of the Forge Masters.
    • [Group Event] Defeat Kela's minions and destroy the All Seer's chains (80)
  • The All Seer will lock down the forge in: 15:00.

The All Seer[edit]

  • Put an end to the All Seer's watch.
  • Structural integrity of the All Seer
    • [Group Event] Release souls from the feretory (80) x6
      • Feretories breached: x/3
      • Avatars engaged: x/3
    • [Group Event] Defeat the enhanced creature to sever the chain (80) x6
      • Champions defeated: x/3
  • Destroy the chains.
  • The Light of the Forge Masters will fade in: 15:00
Success
  • The All Seer has been subdued. Its watch has concluded.
  • The forge will power down in: 10:00
Failure
  • The Inquest has retaken the forge and the All Seer holds dominion over Castora.
  • The forge will power down in: 10:00

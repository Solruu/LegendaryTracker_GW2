Visions of Istan is a Legendary Trinkets achievement that is a part of the Vision I: Awakening collection.

Achievement[edit]

Visions of Istan Legendary Trinkets 10
Explore the Domain of Istan for greater insight.Istan stinks of death and deception only to those who fail to see...

Note: Completion of this achievement requires completion of the Riding Skyscales achievements.
Unlock Item: Trance Stone
Collection
 7 Steps Completed 10

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Notes
1 Heavy Corsair Turban Skin Heavy headgear Complete "Daybreak" Mastery or Domain of Istan Reward Track
2 Vision of Dragons: Aurene Item Trophy Hint: Somewhere near the Mordant Crescent Great Hall in the Domain of Istan.
The Memory Fragment is located above Plaza of Resurgence, easiest reached by flying onto the gate first and ascending from there.
3 Vision of Allies: Taimi Item Trophy Hint: Somewhere near Chalon Docks Waypoint in the Domain of Istan.
Acquired by interacting with Partially Corrupted Data Entry, located south of Chalon Docks Waypoint, next to Alaleh on top of a barrel.
4 Vision of Enemies: Warden Amala Item Trophy Hint: Defeat Warden Amala in the Mordant Crescent Great Hall while holding a Memory Essence Encapsulator in your inventory.
Has to be the Defeat Warden Amala event, not the story fight.
5 Vision of Landscapes: Domain of Istan Item Trophy Hint: Use your skyscale to access a rift near the Astralarium in the Domain of Istan.
The rift is located on top of the big telescope at Astralarium Waypoint, right next to the vista.
6 Vision of Equipment: Astral Weapons Item Service Purchased from Yasna for 5 after crafting 6 Astral weapons.
7 Vision of Action: Domain of Istan Item Trophy Hint: Stop the supply of deathly materials.

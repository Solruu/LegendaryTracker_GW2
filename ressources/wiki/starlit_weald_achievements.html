Notes[edit]

  1. ^ A Hunt for the Ages: Despite specifying the Tyrannus Man-Eater, it is the Augmented Man-Eater which grants credit. (Possible bug)
  2. ^ Escape Artist: Defense Systems: Turret Trap may refer to: Arcane Seeker, Arcane Spreadshot, and Seer Sticker.
  3. ^ Grilling Buddies: Wait for the choya dance event, put on the costume, run up the right stone above the fire to where a choya stands, hit any dance button to get credit
  4. ^ Inquest Training Speed Runner: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Inquest Training Speed Runner.
  5. ^ No-Hit Run: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: No-Hit Run.
  6. ^ Project Bodyguard: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Project Bodyguard.
  7. ^ Sebb Subverter: Escort animals during Rescue the animals from the Inquest and escort them to safety at Specimen Containment
  8. ^ Sketch Artist: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Sketch Artist.
  9. ^ Skimming off the Top: Qualifying events: Help Arcanist Malane gather magic samples, Help Corporal Vikar gather magic samples.
  10. ^ Soup's On: This achievement can only be acquired while the heart is not completed.
  11. ^ Wavehawk Delivery: At the  Lake of Lillies — , while riding a skimmer and collecting the four types of food in the water at the PoI. You must be on your skimmer and interact with the Freshwater Clams, Edible Seaweed, Edible Coral, and eligible fish under the water showing the hand icon over them. The heart Assist your allies in establishing camp and getting along (80) must not be completed.
  12. ^ Wavehawk Savior: Despite the description, wavehawks can only be freed during the Stop the Inquest and save the wild wavehawks event.

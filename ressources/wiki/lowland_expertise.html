— In-game description

Lowland Expertise is an effect obtained by purchasing the Lowland Kodan Expertise service from renown heart NPCs in Janthir after completing their heart.

Mechanics

[edit]
Stacking
  • Each purchase of Lowland Kodan Expertise gives one stack and adds 4 hours to its timer, up to the maximum of 7 stacks and a 24 hour duration.
  • The effect duration does not decrease while logged out, making it easy to build up and maintain the stacks over several days.
Additional rewards
  • The material gained by having 4 stacks depends on the zone the cache is opened in:
    • Hearth's Glow Homestead: None.
    • Lowland Shore: Honey Flower or Lowland Pine Log.
    • Janthir Syntri: Charged Titan Ore or Rotted Titan Amber.
    • Mount Balrior: None.
    • Mistburned Barrens: Mursaat Obsidian Chunk or Titan Heatstone.
    • Bava Nisos: None.
  • Having 6 stacks will give an additional Ancient Coin (1) for all caches except for the Mythical, which gains Ancient Coin (2).
  • Because the effect only grants bonuses while in Janthir, opening a cache container anywhere else will not provide additional materials or coins.
  • The source map of containers does not matter, only the current location upon opening.

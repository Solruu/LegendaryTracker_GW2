Lore[edit]

Eikasia, Mists-Grasper
A mysterious pair of gloves that seek to hold the very essence of the worlds between. Open your hands, and harness the energies of a deteriorating fractal to bring that destruction to your foes.

Graphics[edit]

  • The base appearance is identical to the Mists-Grasper Gloves, but with the addition of animated particle effects (these do not appear when previewing the gloves).
  • Upon drawing or stowing weapons a colored swirl occurs around the character's body. By default, this is a deep magenta color.
  • When weapons are wielded three additional effects are added:
    • a slightly reflective, non-transparent, static cone or orb shape (depending on the weapon type) at the tip of the weapon,
    • a radiating, transparent, non-static effect around that cone or orb,
    • and a trail effect following the cone or orb when moving.
  • On stowing a weapon, a six segment aperture will open. The effects around the weapons disappear and a small swirl begins around the aperture as it closes. The swirl persists until weapons are drawn again.
  • The color of the energy effects can be changed with different Agony Lens infusions.
With Agony Lens infusions
  • The infusions will take effect as long as the Eikasia, Mists-Grasper skin is applied. It means that even if you apply the skin to any other rarity gloves, the default and infusion effects will still appear.
  • The draw swirl effect is smaller or not present at all, if vanishing agony lenses are equipped and bi-colored, or if the colors assigned two both hands are different.
    • In case of applying the legendary skin to any other rarity gloves that are lower than ascended, you have to equip the infusion on a different armor, weapon, or trinket piece with an available infusion slot for it to take effect.
    • The effect is still shown even if the visibility of the gloves is toggled off in the equipment panel.
  • Preliminary testing suggests that if two Agony Lenses for the same side but of different colors are equipped, the active color is chosen depending on the shop order in the vendor's inventory.
    • It means that the infusion effects do not merge, but rather overwrite each other. For example, using an Amber Agony Lens (Right) along with Azure Agony Lens (Right) will make the Azure Agony Lens effect an active one. Removing the Azure Agony Lens will make Amber an active one instead.

Agony Lens infusions

Applying left or right Agony Lens infusions alters the effect color on the left or right glove respectively. If both left and right vanishing agony lenses are used, the appearance reverts to that of Mists-Grasper Gloves.

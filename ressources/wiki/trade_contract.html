Rewarded by[edit]

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Defeat the champion bounty (2-5)
    • Defeat the sand lion pride (3)
    • Defeat Trunt (3)
    • Disperse the wild magic that escaped from Augury Rock (3)

Other[edit]

  • 50 Trade Contracts are rewarded for completing any of the five Crystal Desert maps.
  • Map bonus reward from Crystal Oasis, Desert Highlands, Elon Riverlands, The Desolation, and Domain of Vabbi

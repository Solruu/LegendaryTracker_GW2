The Minstrel III: The Bard is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Bard, for the legendary focus, The Minstrel. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

The Minstrel III: The Bard Legendary Weapons 3
From the forge of criticism emerges the glory of performance, where one learns of the vast wonder of the world of music. Tyria is alive with music if one only takes the time to focus upon it.
Gather these songs in your journey to create the legendary precursor The Bard.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Focus
Reward: Chest of Performance
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 33 Items 2

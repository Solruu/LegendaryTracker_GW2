Aether-Rich Sap is a currency obtained by completing events throughout Shipwreck Strand. They can be spent at various vendors across Castora. Chromatic Sap and Aether-Rich Sap and can be converted at a 1:5 ratio with no limit:

  • Consuming 1 Chromatic Sap awards 5 Aether-Rich Sap.
  • 5 Aether-Rich Sap can be used to purchase 1 Chromatic Sap at any Castoran renown vendor.

Acquisition[edit]

Contained in[edit]

  •   Chromatic Sap (5) (guaranteed)

Rewarded by[edit]

  • Completing events in Shipwreck Strand.

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Defeat Operative Neel (5)
    • Defeat the rat king (15)
    • Help Acolyte Alex return the beached whale to the shelter of the sea (15)
    • Help Astral Ward Operative Chesh Steelmind hack Inquest datapads and analyze the intel (5)
    • Help Fisher Sorcha pull the beached whale back into the water (15)
    • Investigate the tide pools to help the Hullgardeners find resources (5)
    • Keep the Hullgardeners comfortable while they work on their tans (5)
    • Return the castaways' stolen supplies to Corin (5)
    • Use turtle bait to help Audine guide the baby turtles to the safety of deep water (5)
    • Use turtle bait to help Nara guide the baby turtles to the safety of deep water (5)
    • Use your skimmer to help gather fresh food for the castaways in Breezy Cay (5)
    • Use your skimmer to help gather fresh food for the castaways in Glimmering Arches (5)
    • Use your skimmer to help gather fresh food for the castaways in Jetsam Point (5)
  • Completing any of the following renown hearts:
    • Assist the castaways around Hullgarden (10)
    • Help out with research and expedition tasks around Pilgrim's Rest (10)
    • Help Pub Canach grow into a lucrative business (10)
    • Help the Tyrian Alliance keep tabs on the Inquest and learn more about Castora's natural magics (10)

Magnetite Shards are a currency awarded by killing bosses and completing events in raids from previous expansions. Encounters from the most recent expansion (Visions of Eternity) will reward Gaeting Crystal instead. They are stored in the wallet and can be traded with various raid vendors for boss-specific rewards and generic ascended equipment.

There is a weekly limit of 800 Magnetite Shards earned directly from encounter rewards. All other acquisition methods will ignore this cap, and do not count towards it, including: opening chests or containers, Challenge Mode rewards, trading in minis to raid vendors, salvaging ascended raid drops, and achievements (both weekly and "Return to").

Acquisition[edit]

Gathered from[edit]

  • Grand Frost Legion Chest (1-3) (guaranteed)
  • Stone Summit Chest (5-10) (guaranteed)
  • Vigil Emissary Chest (6-8) (guaranteed, if opened with 20 Magnetite Shards)
  • Vigil Emissary Chest (11-13) (guaranteed, if opened with 40 Magnetite Shards)
  • Vigil Emissary Chest (10-22) (guaranteed, if opened with 60 Magnetite Shards)

Contained in[edit]

  •   Steel Warband Weapon Box (5) (choice)
  •   Visions of Eternity Relic Set 2 Container (25) (choice, once all relics are unlocked.)

Rewarded by[edit]

  • Awarded for every succesful encounter until the weekly limit is reached.
    • 8, 10, 12, 14, or 16 shards, depending on the difficulty and length of the encounter.
  • Awarded for unsuccessful attempts at defeating bosses, based on the progression in the fight.
    • 1 shard is awarded below 75% health.
    • 3 shards total are awarded below 50% health.
    • 5 shards total are awarded below 25% health.
  • Awarded for killing bosses with the Challenge mode (CM) activated (10 shards each).

Anomalies:
  • Some event encounters no longer reward any shards.
  • Cold War's Base Difficulty implies its encounter should reward 10 shards.
  • Quickplay mode sometimes rewards half, and sometimes the full amount.
  • After a successful completion in Cosmic Observatory and Temple of Febe, a full raid wipe will still give an additional failure reward of 3 shards. This is often intentionally triggered by all players resigning after the kill.

Sold by[edit]

  • Trading raid dropped minis (e.g. Mini Vale Guardian) to raid vendors (40 shards each).
    • Mount Balrior bosses will instead reward 40 additional shards if the miniature is already unlocked.
  • Trading White Mantle Portal Device to raid vendors (250 shards).

Salvaged from[edit]

For a full list of salvageable equipment, see: Magnetite Shard/Salvaged from
  • Salvaging raid-exclusive ascended weapons, armor and trinkets dropped by bosses (e.g. Assaulter's Sparking Dagger) or bought from a raid vendor (e.g. Assaulter's Razor).
    • Each item gives 60 shards.
    • Items that have been stat changed in the Mystic Forge cannot then be salvaged for Magnetite Shards.

Notes[edit]

  • The  Map Bonus Rate Increase effect increases the yield of Magnetite Shards from encounter rewards, since raid technically uses a reward track.[1]
  • The number of shards earned towards the weekly limit can be seen by hovering over the currency in the wallet.
  • The Magnetite Shard weekly cap has seen several updates, including a stealth change from 105 to 100, an increase to 150 in June of 2016, to 300 in July of 2022, and to 800 in February of 2026
  • The July 19, 2022 game update converted all Gaeting Crystals to Magnetite Shards. The February 3, 2026 game update did the same for Prophet Shards.
    • Upon logging in after the mentioned updates, players received these messages in their mail:

Scholar Glenna

Your gaeting crystals have been converted to magnetite shards!

Dear <Character name>,

I discovered that gaeting crystals eventually turn into magnetite shards! I know you may be very worried, but rest assured, I spoke with the merchants throughout the Aerodrome and we all agreed to accept magnetite shards in place of gaeting crystals. Well, when you think about it, if there are no more gaeting crystals around, we all have to accept magnetite shards, right?

Happy raiding!

—Glenna, Durmand Priory Scholar

Scholar Glenna

Magnetite Shard Exchanges

Greetings, Commander!

I'm writing to inform you that you've been upgraded from the Prophet Shard class of Raiding, to the wonderful new* Magnetite Shard Class! You'll notice that your Blue Prophet Shards have been exchanged for Magnetite Shards. This is great because you can now spend all of those shards at vendors in the Lion's Arch Aerodrome! You'll also notice that your Gaeting Crystals have been exchanged for Magnetite as well, because, who doesn't want more Magnetite? Am I right?

Okay, that's enough excitement for one day. I will see you in the Aerodrome!

—Scholar Glenna

*Please note that "new" Magnetite Shards are the same as "old" Magnetite Shards.

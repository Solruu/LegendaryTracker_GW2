Hinterlands Liberator is a trophy required for the Sunrise I: The Experimental Daysword legendary weapon collection.

Notes

[edit]
  • Intercept the prisoner caravan found southwest of the  Wynchona Rally Point Waypoint — , or break the captives out of the centaur slave cage at the Confinement Camp.
    • If the first event fails, the captives will have to be rescued. Both events, however, contribute towards the achievement.

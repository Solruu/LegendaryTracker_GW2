Memories of Battle are crafting materials used to craft legendary components to create precursor weapons. They are available from WvW reward tracks and can be purchased on the trading post.

Acquisition[edit]

  • WvW Reward Track (usually the 2nd reward (5) and the 22nd reward (10) for a total of 15 per track)
    • The Gift of Battle Item Reward Track gives 25 and Guildrider Warclaw Skin Reward Track gives 50.
    • Dragon Bash Reward Track, Halloween Reward Track, and Festival of the Four Winds Reward Track only give 10 each.
  • List of all reward tracks containing Memories of Battle can be found here.

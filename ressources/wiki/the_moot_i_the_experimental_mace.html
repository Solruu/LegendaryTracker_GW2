The Moot I: The Experimental Mace is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Energizer, for the legendary mace, The Moot. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, The Energizer Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

The Moot I: The Experimental Mace Legendary Weapons 3
First comes the hunt—the matching of martial prowess against the bestial cunning. Mighty creatures roam the land, and as you stalk them, consider their place,
in these initial steps to creating the legendary precursor known as The Energizer. Hurry! Before someone gets eaten!
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: The Moot Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of the Hunt
Collection
 Collected 14 Items 3

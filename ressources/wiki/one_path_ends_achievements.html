Notes[edit]

  1. ^ Pruning the Weed: Is intended to be completed after Risen Research, as the dialogue to trigger combat with the Veteran Unchained Kingpin is only available after completing Risen Research. However, it is possible to complete Pruning the Weed early if someone else triggers the Kingpin for you. This will not hurt your ability to complete The Withered Rose, but you will still need to complete Risen Research before you are able to give the journal to Dahlia.
  2. ^ The Withered Rose: After completing Risen Research and Pruning the Weed, search the bushes northeast of Overgrown Gazebo to find a tattered journal on the ground. Take it, then return to Dahlia.

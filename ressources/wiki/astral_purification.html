Astral Purification is an achievement helping Sunsmith Ouissal with her research. Completing the achievement will unlock a second merchant tab from Yasna, allowing you to purchase the recipes for Stellar weapons.

To begin this collection, craft any astral weapon, open your mail and give the Note from Yasna item to Ouissal.

Mail[edit]

Researcher Yasna

Kralkatite breakthrough!

My esteemed friend, I've had a breakthrough with the Kralkatite. It was only a matter of time, and it happened. We can upgrade the Astralarium to infuse energy into vision crystals!

We'll need quite a few specialized mirrors. Do me a favor–take these notes to Ouissal in the Sunspear Base. She designs various telescope components and should–I think–be able to make mirrors for us.

—Researcher Yasna

PS: Congratulations on your new astral weapon.

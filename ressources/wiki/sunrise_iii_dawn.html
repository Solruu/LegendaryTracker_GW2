Sunrise III: Dawn is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Dawn, for the legendary greatsword, Sunrise. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Sunrise III: Dawn Legendary Weapons 3
A shining light of a new day reveals all and banishes the darkness to the deeper haunts of Tyria. Use your crafted Illuminator to gather the light throughout the land in your journey
to create the legendary precursor known as Dawn
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Daysword
Reward: Chest of Illumination

 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 35 Items 2

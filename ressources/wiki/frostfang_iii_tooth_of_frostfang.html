Frostfang III: Tooth of Frostfang is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to obtain the precursor weapon Tooth of Frostfang, for the legendary axe Frostfang. This collection is the third step to obtaining the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Frostfang III: Tooth of Frostfang Legendary Weapons 3
Within the halls of the norn hang the Fang of the Serpent, a fragment of the great ice Elder Dragon. Frostfang was the greatest of Jormag's champions, and Asgeir Dragonrender slayed Frostfang with but a single blow.
Channel this power over ice into your efforts to create the legendary precursor Tooth of Frostfang.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Axe
Reward: Chest of Freezing
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 34 Items 2

Notes[edit]

  • Only one Glacial Imbued Jar is required for the entire collection. It is not consumed for any of the collectibles.
  • Take note that in the maps to collect Living Ice from Ice Elementals, there may also be similarly looking Icebrood Elementals. These are considered a different entity and will not drop the items for the collection!

In the Mystic Forge, combine 500 Hydrocatalytic Reagents, 250 Thermocatalytic Reagents, and 250 exotic Essences of Luck to create a Gift of Research.

Hint: Lyhr in the Wizard's Tower can also help create this.

— In-game description

Gift of Research is a legendary crafting component used for crafting generation 3 legendary weapons, legendary Obsidian armor, Klobjarne Geirr, Aetheric Anchor, Selachimorpha, Stella Radians, and the legendary relic. It is stackable.

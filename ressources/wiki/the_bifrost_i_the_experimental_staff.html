The Bifrost I: The Experimental Staff is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Legend, for the legendary staff, The Bifrost. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, The Legend Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

The Bifrost I: The Experimental Staff Legendary Weapons 3
The Jotun Star, fabled relic of the ancient mystical race, was said to call down a rainbow to the land. The stone, and its power, was broken long ago.
Help bring it to rest as the first part of your journey to craft the legendary precursor The Legend.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: The Bifrost Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Ancient Mysticism
Collection
 Collected 16 Items 3

Notes[edit]

  • The Jotun star fragments only drop once. Afterwards, you can sell them. It is not necessary to have them in your inventory to complete The Jotun Star.
  • It is possible for multiple fragments to drop if you have not picked any of them up yet.
  • Fusing the fragments does not remove them from your inventory, but all you can do with them at that point is sell them to a merchant.
  • The collection requires a total of 900 Bloodstone Dust. In order to leave a tribute at one of the Jotun sites, you must have at least 100 dust in your character's inventory; it will not be pulled from the bank or shared inventory slots.

Acquisition

[edit]
  • Plant a Fire Orchid Seed in the Fertile Soil south of Kodama inside the Druid's Grotto in its southernmost part, in the Savage Rise.
    • A Fire Orchid Seed can be obtained as a random drop by harvesting a Primordial Orchid

Location

[edit]

 Heathen's Hold Waypoint — From the waypoint, head west until you reach the edge. Drop down until you reach a ledge with several Grubs. The entrance to the Druid's Grotto is on this ledge just to the east. Inside the grotto, keep heading south-east and up a few rocks to find the fertile soil, just south of a ring of Flax nodes.

Notes

[edit]
  • After planting the seed, a Primordial Orchid will be available for daily harvest on the fertile soil.

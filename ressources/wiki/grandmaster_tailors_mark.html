Grandmaster Tailor's Mark is a Grandmaster Mark token used to acquire stat-selectable GAscended armor, crafted by the Tailor discipline. It provides an alternative method of obtaining ascended gear through certain content and game modes compared to full ascended crafting. Combination of Grandmaster Tailor's Marks and the content-specific currencies is used to purchase ascended items from the vendors, with currencies replacing part of the crafting costs.

Grandmaster Marks are currently accepted by vendors in Structured PvP and World versus World game modes, as well as the Fractals of the Mists dungeon. Ascended armor purchasable with Marks allows selecting any prefix, but the expansion-specific attributes are available only if the relevant game editions are owned.

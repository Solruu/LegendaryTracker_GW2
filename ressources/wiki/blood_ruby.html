Acquisition[edit]

Gathered from[edit]

  • Bloodstone Chest (3) (guaranteed, after defeating the spirit of Justiciar Hablion)
  • Bloodstone Chest (guaranteed, after escorting Alari Doubleblade on her quest for vengeance)
  • Bloodstone Crystals (chance, in Bloodstone Fen)
  • Bloodstone Crystals (guaranteed, home instances)
  • Large Bloodstone Chest (2) (guaranteed, after defeating the jade constructs before they drain the bloodstone)
  • Large Bloodstone Chest (4) (guaranteed, after dispersing the Unbound Guardian)
  • Large Bloodstone Chest (guaranteed, at the end of Confessor's Stronghold)
  • Known gathering amounts from the above are as follows:
    • 3 from the home instance Bloodstone Crystal Node (once per account, per day)
    • Defeat the spirit of Justiciar Hablion - 3 from the Bloodstone Chest (once per character, per day)
    • Bloodstone Maw meta event:
      • Defeat the jade constructs before they drain the bloodstone! - 2 from the Large Bloodstone Chest (once per character, per day)
      • Disperse the Unbound Guardian - 4 from the Large Bloodstone Chest
    • The spirits mostly come out at night. Mostly... meta event:
      • Escort Alari Doubleblade on her quest for vengeance - 1 from the Bloodstone Chest (once per character, per day)
    • 1 from the arena event at the end of the Confessor's Stronghold story step

Red Oak Saplings are resource nodes found in level 70-80 areas. They can be logged at least 3 times.

Gathering results[edit]

  • Elder Wood Log (1-2) (guaranteed)
  • Hidden Trove
  • Foxfire Cluster
  • Ruined Log (guaranteed) - if a Mithril Logging Axe or better is not used

Shooshadoo is a legendary shield crafted in the Mystic Forge, connected to the Heart of Thorns expansion through the required tasks and materials. Shooshadoo was first teased on November 15th, 2016 and introduced in the game on November 21st, 2016 alongside the Living World Season 3 episode A Crack in the Ice.

Lore[edit]

Shooshadoo
The tiny quaggan Shooshadoo has made up their mind to follow in your footsteps as a champion of Tyria. You'll need to carry them until their stride is a bit longer.

Graphics[edit]

Aura
  • The arm wielding Shooshadoo turns white and gets covered with frost.
Draw/Stow Animation
  • Upon drawing the weapon, it creates an explosion of snow and ice shards.
Footfalls
  • While wielding Shooshadoo with no other legendary weapon in the main hand, the user leaves behind white, snowy footsteps that subtly explode with frost.
Skill effects
  • Chronomancer: Tides of Time has a copy of Shooshadoo spinning in the center.
  • Engineer: Throw Shield throws Shooshadoo instead.
  • Herald: Envoy of Exuberance throws Shooshadoo instead.
Sound effects

All of the sound effects are tied to the "effects" volume slide.

  • When drawing the weapon, a very faint wrestling ring bell sound plays, together with an icy whirlwind sound.
  • When the weapon is stowed, a sound of water splashing may play.
  • The quaggan will sometimes coo when stowing the weapon.
  • The quaggan will rarely coo while the player is idle, possibly sleeping.
  • The quaggan always coos when mounting or dismounting from any mount.

Notes[edit]

  • It is recommended that you do not attempt to stat change your precursor item with the standard stat swap recipe in the Mystic Forge. While you will be provided with an item with the desired stats, it will have been replaced with a generic ascended item that cannot be used as a legendary precursor.
  • Both Shard of Friendship and Mystic Curio (All Variants) recipes are contained within Recipe: Trust.

A gift of fortune used to create legendary weapons.

Made by combining these items in the Mystic Forge:

  • 77 Mystic Clovers
  • 250 Globs of Ectoplasm
  • 1 Gift of Might
  • 1 Gift of Magic

— In-game description

Gift of Fortune is a gift used to craft the first set of legendary weapons, as well as legendary back pieces. Second generation legendaries require a Mystic Tribute instead.

Trivia[edit]

  • The Gift of Might and Magic ingredients are likely a reference to the RPG series Might and Magic.
  • The 77 Mystic Clovers needed is likely a reference to the fact that 7 is considered a lucky number.
  • Between 1 December, 2015 and 12 September, 2023, this item could be salvaged to return all base materials.

Ad Infinitum III: Unbound is a Legendary Backpacks collection achievement that requires items from the Fractals of the Mists in combination with some specific tasks, and can be unlocked by Kelvei in the Mistlock Observatory after Ad Infinitum I and II. Completion awards the Proof: Unbound needed for crafting Unbound, the third and final tier of the precursor to the legendary back item Ad Infinitum.

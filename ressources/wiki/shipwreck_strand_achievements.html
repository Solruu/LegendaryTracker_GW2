Notes[edit]

  1. ^ Await the Offgoing!: Hidden Choya will appear after you drink 'Mysterious Nectar Mixture' near Ritual Clothing Rack.
  2. ^ Help the Thirsty Old Captain: The captain is a "Castaway" (man or woman of any race), can be asked "Are you a leader of some kind?" when spoken to, and can be one of the heart NPCs with a dialog bubble above their head. Bring them a Breezy Cay Honey Ale.
  3. ^ Juke Jessica: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Juke Jessica.
  4. ^ Juke Jimmy: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Juke Jimmy.
  5. ^ Not Even a Scratch: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Not Even a Scratch.
  6. ^ Paxx's Favorite Patron: You must interact with the Bar Tip Jar in Pub Canach to progress this achievement.
  7. ^ Seafood Connoisseur: Forage every type of foods during the "Use your skimmer to help gather fresh food for the castaways in..." events: Glimmering Arches (80), Breezy Cay (80), and Jetsam Point (80)
  8. ^ Swearing Off the Poison: Achievement eligibility tracked by the effect  Achievement Eligibility: Swearing Off the Poison.

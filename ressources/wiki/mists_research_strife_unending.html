Mists Research: Strife Unending is a World vs World achievement. Completing it grants 2 Gift of the Warclaw which is a component needed to craft Strife Unending.

Walkthrough[edit]

Objective Notes
1 Check in with Dugan. Can be found in the spawn area of any team in World vs. World maps (except Edge of the Mists). Requires the Mists Research achievement.
2 After equipping a Mistwalker Infusion, kill 100 enemy players. The infusion can either be bought on the trading post, or purchased from Dugan. Note that the kill count is preserved, even if using the same infusion across multiple characters. Track by the  Mists Research: Strife Unending
3 Find the warclaw tamer in Zraith's Beacon. Phongluamthan the Tamer and his two warclaws can be found in an area nearby but under the end of the jumping puzzle, near the Orichalcum Ore node.
4 Capture the forge in the Edge of the Mists and talk to the smith there. Talk to Champion Norn Smith at the Forge (EotM blue side, Frostreach Region)
5 Capture and commune with the altar in the Edge of the Mists. Interact with Altar (object) (EotM green side, Overgrowth Region)
6 Capture the workshop in the Edge of the Mists and talk to the mechanic there. Talk to Champion Charr Mechanic at the Workshop (EotM red side, Badlands Region)
7 Capture the keep of an enemy team in Eternal Battlegrounds. The Warclaw Spirit will spawn by the lord's rooms upon capture. (enemy team EBG keeps only)
8 Capture Askalion Hills in Alpine Borderlands. East side of Blue Alpine Borderlands. Warclaw outside the lord's room (BBL Hills)
9 Capture Dreadfall Bay in Alpine Borderlands. West side of Green Alpine Borderlands. Warclaw Spirit will spawn outside the lord's room right next to the stairs. (GBL Bay)
10 Capture Stoic Rampart in Desert Borderlands. Warclaw West side of the lords area. (RBL Earth Keep)
11 Return to Dugan. Requires all previous steps to be completed.

Notes[edit]

  • For Warclaw Spirits that spawn in Keeps and Edge of the Mists objectives, you do not need to be present during the capture of the Keep or objective. This means that one can simply wait for their team to acquire a specific color to more easily speak to specific Spirits, except for the Spirit that spawns in non-home Keeps in Eternal Battlegrounds.

Antiquated Ducats are a currency obtained by completing events throughout Starlit Weald. They can be spent at various vendors across Castora. Raw Enchanting Stone and Antiquated Ducat and can be converted at a 1:5 ratio with no limit:

  • Consuming 1 Raw Enchanting Stone awards 5 Antiquated Ducats.
  • 5 Antiquated Ducats can be used to purchase 1 Raw Enchanting Stone at any Castoran renown vendor.

Acquisition[edit]

Contained in[edit]

  •   Raw Enchanting Stone (5) (guaranteed)

Rewarded by[edit]

  • Completing events in Castora

  • Completing any of the following events:
    • Dance with the choya (10)
    • Defeat the champion enchanted weapon (25)
    • Defend the priory camp from the Inquest raid (15)
    • Find and rescue the missing skimmers (5)
    • Find and Rescue the Missing Skimmers (5)
    • Gather the loose magic for Isgarren (25)
    • Help Klorrk subdue the Inquest-enhanced creatures (5)
    • Help repair and defend the bird nest (5)
    • Help the Durmand Priory rebuild the camp (15)
    • Reach the finish line in the Enchanting Grottoes (10)
  • Completing any of the following renown hearts:
    • Assist the Durmand Priory in establishing and protecting their expedition (10)
    • Assist the hunters in maintaining their camp and protecting the wildlife from the Inquest (10)
    • Assist your allies in establishing camp and getting along (10)

Notes

[edit]
Bug:
It can be used while dead, if so, the next time you respawn you will have the bundle in your hands.
  • The kit does not trigger any skills or traits related to stealth, since it grants camouflage.
    • Movement prematurely ends camouflage.
  • Does not work in World versus World.
  • Can be used in Fractals, and Mistlock Singularities will reset its cooldown.
  • Can be used to commune with Hero challenges while under stealth.
  • Does not require The Predator III: The Hunter collection to be active or completed to be bought.

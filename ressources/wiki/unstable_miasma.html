Acquisition

[edit]
  • With a Mordrem Thrasher Gas Bladder and 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust in inventory, swim into the underwater  Iron Horse Mines — in Grimstone Mol, Frostgorge Sound, and head to the bottom of the Skelknest Borehole inside, under the giant turbine in the center of the mine. You will receive the following prompt:
The weight of the ocean creates a high-pressure environment here.
Combine a Mordrem Thrasher Gas Bladder with 10 Piles of Bloodstone Dust. (ends dialogue and gives Unstable Miasma)
Leave.

Notes

[edit]
  • If you do not accept the prompt, it disappears. Simply swim up and back down to be re-prompted.
  • A Mordrem Thrasher Gas Bladder requires obtaining 2 Pieces of Mordrem Thrasher Gas Bladder to create.

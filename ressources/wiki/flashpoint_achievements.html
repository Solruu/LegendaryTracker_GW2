Notes[edit]

  1. ^ Burnin' Down the House: Around the  Mercenary Encampment — near  Heathen's Hold Waypoint — are tall turrets that can be destroyed by blowing up the pile of Cannonballs next to it. Use the adjacent Oil Barrel to   Spill Oil and create a trail from the cannonballs to a nearby fire exhaust or lava.
  2. ^ Crypt Keeper: The achievement is awarded upon seeing the tombstone inside the  Burial Chamber — . The point of interest is unlocked without even entering the room.
  3. ^ Hot Splashes: From  Heathen's Hold Waypoint — , take the northwest Thermal Tube up toward Titan's Throat. Although the description hints that the goggles are near the Gate of Fire, they are located much closer to  Searing Ascent — , on the edge of the ledge at the start of the Searing Ascent jumping puzzle. The bucket to dive into is in the boiling sea at the very bottom of Titan's Throat, just north of the Searing Ascent icon. Fall directly off the edge, passing the first ledge, then veer slightly left through a small gap between the crystal and the ledge just before the sea.
  4. ^ Nature's Wrath: The Greater Wardbough appears at the end of an event chain starting from Escort the Vigil squad to the ancient ruins (80) by talking to Squad Leader Perun at  Scout's Clearing — , next to Renown Heart NPC Tactician Tunelle.

Trivia[edit]

  • The achievement "White Hat" is a reference to the term white hat which refers to people skilled in computer security and are hired to help to test the security of various organizations.
    • Additionally, the flavor text of this achievement is a reference to a scene from the TV series CSI:NY,
  • The "Burnin' Down the House" achievement shares its name with a Talking Heads song from their 1983 album, Speaking in Tongues. The flavor text includes lyrics from the song.

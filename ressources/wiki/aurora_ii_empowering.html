Aurora II: Empowering is a Legendary Trinkets collection achievement to gain the Spark of Sentience, a requirement for creating Aurora. This collection is the second step in obtaining this item.

Achievement[edit]

Aurora II: Empowering Legendary Trinkets 10
Collect all 21 items to create a Spark of Sentience.
Prerequisite: Aurora: Awakening
Reward: Spark of Sentience
Collection
 Collected 21 Items 10

Collection items[edit]

  • Each of the steps only requires you to activate a specific Mastery Insight with a Xunlai Electrum Ingot in your inventory. If you have not yet communed with the respective Mastery Insight, you will need to do so first.

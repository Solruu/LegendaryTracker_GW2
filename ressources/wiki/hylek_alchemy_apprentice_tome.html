An empty notebook for containing your notes on hylek alchemy.

Hint: Purchased from Atlalli, the Master Alchemist in the Hunting Banks of Timberline Falls.

Hylek Alchemy Apprentice Tome is a trophy that tracks your progress in the early stages of learning how to make the legendary weapon HOPE. As such, it is advised that you keep it in your inventory as you accomplish tasks related to the first collection.

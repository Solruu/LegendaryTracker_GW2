Rich Silver Veins are resource nodes found in level 15-40 areas. They can be mined ten times.

Detailed locations guide[edit]

Note that the nodes listed below are not guaranteed to be there in any given map instance unless marked so.

Ascalon
  • Diessa Plateau
    • The Blasted Moors - Guaranteed, at the top of the Grendich Gamble jumping puzzle.
    • The Blasted Moors - Possible Rich Node inside the Grendich Ruins
    • Breachwater Lake - Inside a cave by Burntfur's Pool.
    • Holystone Caves - Inside the grawl caves southeast of  Sanctum Waypoint — .
    • Incendio Templum - Possible Rich node in NE corner.
    • Reaper's Corridor - In the Crimson Plateau jumping puzzle.
  • Fields of Ruin
    • Axedrag Cut - South of  Fangfury Watch Waypoint — .
    • Bento Canyon - Northwest of  Tyler's Bivouac Waypoint — .
    • Flameroot Caverns - In the southeast corner near the harpy nests.
    • Halkor Meadows - Guaranteed, northeast of Rosko's Campsite.
    • Hunter's Gorge - Northeast of the Foulbear Kraal POI.
    • Tenaebron Lake - Immediately south of the Sentinel's Perch POI.
    • Vulture's Sweep - Under a cliff northeast of  Vulture's Waypoint —
Kryta
  • Gendarran Fields
    • Icegate Gorge - Guaranteed, in the southeast corner.
    • Lake Bounty - At the end of the Swashbuckler's Cove jumping puzzle.
    • Provern Shore - Within a small cave just east of  Provern Shore Waypoint — .
  • Kessex Hills - One node is guaranteed, usually the one at Mafic Core, but will be a different one if there's a Rich Iron Vein at Mafic Core.
    • Cereboth Canyon - South of the Mafic Core POI in the destroyer cavern. A Champion Destroyer Harpy spawns here, making mining the node difficult.
    • Dominion Killing Zone - Next to a mountain west of  Cereboth Waypoint — .
    • Cereboth Canyon - Near Cereboth Waypoint.
    • Eukaryan Caves - Northeast of waypoint.
    • Viathan's Arm - On top of the Eukaryan Caves peninsula, southeast of the hero point.
Maguuma Jungle
  • Brisban Wildlands
    • Brilitine Swath - Near the Enrav Exploration Post POI.
    • The Gallowfields - Several possible locations?
    • Karston Chambers - South of  Watchful Source Waypoint — .
    • Lionshead Outcrops - Guaranteed, in a ravine west of a vista.
    • Nemeton Grove - Northeast of Beldame's Rise POI.
    • The Shattered Henge - Immediately west of  Mirkrise Waypoint — .
    • Vandal's Claim - At the pond next to Fort Vandal.
    • Venlin Vale - Northwest of a renown heart.
Shiverpeak Mountains
  • Lornar's Pass
    • Durmand Priory - Guaranteed, in Posternus Caverns underneath the Durmand Priory POI.
    • Mistriven Gorge - South of  Mistriven Waypoint — .
    • Reaper's Gate - South of a hero challenge.
    • Venison Pass - Just west of the Wolfpaw Shrine POI.
  • Snowden Drifts
    • Hrothgar's Pass - Southeast of  Snowdrift Haven Waypoint — .
    • The Loneholme Range - West of  Soderhem Steading Waypoint — .
    • Lornar's Gate - On a hill northeast of  Lornar's Waypoint — .
    • Oros Spurs - On a ridge, Southeast of the Lashoosh POI
    • Valslake - Guaranteed, in a cave near the Moldary Point POI.

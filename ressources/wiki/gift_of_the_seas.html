Combine a Gift of the Seas, a Gift of Adventure, a Bloodstone Shard, and 250 Obsidian Shards in the Mystic Forge to create a Gift of Castoran Mastery. Purchased from Visions of Eternity map vendors in exchange for: • 1 Gift of the Tides • 2 Gifts of Research • 2 Gifts of Condensed Might • 2 Gifts of Condensed Magic — In-game description

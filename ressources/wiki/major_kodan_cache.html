Mechanics

[edit]
Opening
  • Each chest can be opened once per day per account.
  • Kodan Cache Keys are required to open the chests.
  • Contains additional rewards while under the effect of 4 and 6 stacks of  Lowland Expertise.
Locating
  • Each section is limited to one spawned chest. Opening a cache will cause other instances' chest in that section to be unopenable.
    • This puts the maximum available chests at 10 for Lowland Shore and 11 for Janthir Syntri.
    • Each section has 4 possible spawn locations, grouped by colors in the maps below.
  • The chests can be located using Sniff and show the same icon as Detected Treasures.
    • They do not need to be uncovered like Detected Treasures, and are shown on the map as with Treasure Hunter Protocol equipped.

Chak Eggs are tokens which can be obtained in Tangled Depths. They can be exchanged for Chak weapons at Nuhoch Mastery Vendors.

  • A total of 60 would be needed to buy all Chak weapons. This number can be reduced with the free weapon from completing the Buried Insight story step.
  • 30 are required for each set of Perfected Envoy armor; a total of 90 for all three sets.

The Flameseeker Prophecies I: The Experimental Shield is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, The Chosen, for the legendary shield, The Flameseeker Prophecies. This collection is the first step to obtain the first tier of the precursor, The Chosen Experiment.

Achievement[edit]

The Flameseeker Prophecies I: The Experimental Shield Legendary Weapons 3
Tyria is a land of heroes and villains locked in eternal struggle. Attain the "Anthology of Villains" and record the tales of the defeat of the most dastardly of their lot.
Meditate upon the nature of villainy as you take the first steps in creating the legendary precursor known as The Chosen.
Prerequisite: Revered Antiquarian
Unlock Item: The Flameseeker Prophecies Vol. 1
Reward: Chest of Villains
Collection
 Collected 14 Items 3

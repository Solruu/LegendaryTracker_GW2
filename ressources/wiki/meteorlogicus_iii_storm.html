Meteorlogicus III: Storm is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Storm, for the legendary Scepter, Meteorlogicus. This collection is the third step to obtain the precursor.

Achievement[edit]

Meteorlogicus III: Storm Legendary Weapons 3
Power, regardless of origin, is only of use when it is properly mastered, and to control the elements, one must understand the nature of control itself.
Consider the nature of control, those who control, and those who are controlled, in your quest to attain the legendary precursor Storm.
Prerequisite: Historian of the Armaments
Unlock Item: Perfected Scepter
Reward: Chest of Control
Collection
 Collected 1 Item 1
Collected 29 Items 2

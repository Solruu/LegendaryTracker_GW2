Acquisition

[edit]
  • Purchase a Stein of Ancient Deldrimor Ale from Arcanist Vance in the ruins of the Granite Citadel in Dredgehaunt Cliffs after helping him study the Dwarven relics. Without consuming it, bring the ale to Ogden Stonehealer in the Durmand Priory Library.

Notes

[edit]
  • Ogden won't accept the Stein of Ancient Deldrimor Ale in any other instance (such as Arcana Obscura).

Notes[edit]

  • Each of the four stones take turns in lighting up, going clockwise, every few seconds.
  • After being destroyed or used in a recipe, Standing Stones Timepiece Remnant with the same Mystic Forge functionality can be bought from renown heart vendors in Lowland Shore.
  • This back item can be dyed. It has 4 dye channels.
  • This item's description was updated from "This back item is used for crafting Janthir Wilds legendaries. Further copies can be purchased in the near future." with the June 24, 2025 game update.

Acquisition

[edit]

Bring your Raven Egg to the top of the forgotten pillar near the Broken Altar of Dragon's Stand, which is reached by:

  • With Itzel Poison Lore: From the  Northern Forward Camp Waypoint — , head directly north a short distance and then north-west into a small cave (to the lower left of a big cave entrance) to two Bouncing Mushrooms. At the top, jump up a small ledge just behind the hole you bounced out of, and go north through some vegetation. Glide to the top of a pillar with a waterfall on it.
  • Without Itzel Poison Lore: From the  Northern Forward Camp Waypoint — , head north a short distance then west up a hill. Make your way north until you find the top of the hole (with the two Bouncing Mushrooms), then continue as above.

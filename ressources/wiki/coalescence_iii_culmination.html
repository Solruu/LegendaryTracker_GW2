Coalescence III: Culmination is a Legendary Trinkets collection achievement to obtain the Gift of Patience, an item used to create the Legendary ring Coalescence.

Achievement[edit]

Coalescence III: Culmination Legendary Trinkets 10
Your energy friend is keenly interested in your decisions.
Prerequisite: Coalescence II: The Gift
Reward: Gift of Patience
Collection
 Collected 8 Items 10

Collection items[edit]

Collectible Type Subtype Notes
1 Twisted Essence of Generosity Item Service Hint: To exist, we must destroy those around us.
To acquire, defeat Cardinal Adina in the Key of Ahdashim raid.
2 Twisted Essence of Resolve Item Service Hint: Fight every battle.
To acquire, defeat Cardinal Sabir in the Key of Ahdashim raid.
3 Twisted Essence of Trust Item Service Hint: Success is solitary.
To acquire, defeat Qadim the Peerless in the Key of Ahdashim raid.
4 Worn Meditation Logbook Item Trophy Hint: This book has shared much time with its companion.
Talk to Hasara, found hidden in Abaddon's cave in Silent Vale in the Desolation.
5 Purified Essence of Generosity Item Trophy Hint: The ink on this page glows brightly and reads, "To exist, we must share ourselves with those around us."
Go to the Sunspear Sanctuary in Domain of Vabbi and interact with the Strange Energy.
6 Purified Essence of Resolve Item Trophy Hint: The ink on this page glows brightly and reads, "Not every battle must be fought. Not every battle must be won."
Go to the Sunspear Sanctuary in Domain of Vabbi and interact with the Strange Energy.
7 Purified Essence of Trust Item Trophy Hint: The ink on this page glows brightly and reads, "One cannot succeed by themselves in this world."
Go to the Sunspear Sanctuary in Domain of Vabbi and interact with the Strange Energy.
8 Infused Meditation Log Book Item Service Hint: This book hums as you flip through its pages.
  • The Twisted Essences are acquired in the weekly bonus chest of Ahdashim's key bosses. Make sure you enable the part III of the collection before doing your weekly clear so you don't have to wait another week.
  • You must have all three Twisted Essences unlocked in the Collection to successfully interract with the Strange Energy.

Acquisition

[edit]

Ponder the Victory Cenotaph erected at Charr's Triumph in the Plains of Ashford. Southeast of  Temperus Point Waypoint — , stand before the statue at Victory Cenotaph and type /ponder.

Notes

[edit]
  • The Victory Cenotaph can be destroyed if the Destroy the effigy before it reaches the Victory Cenotaph event fails. If that happens, the following event Protect the engineers repairing the Victory Cenotaph, will need to be completed in order to emote for the item.

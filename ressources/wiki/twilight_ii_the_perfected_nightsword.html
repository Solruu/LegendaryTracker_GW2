Twilight II: The Perfected Nightsword is a Legendary Weapons collection achievement to gain the precursor weapon, Dusk, for the legendary greatsword, Twilight. This collection is the second step to obtain the second tier of the precursor, Perfected Nightsword.

Achievement[edit]

Twilight II: The Perfected Nightsword Legendary Weapons 3
Your first time attempt at crafting the legendary precursor Dusk has been modestly successful but has not yet yielded the weapon you ultimately desire.
This journey has only just begun. Find a teacher, research your procedures, and hone your skills before you try a second time.
Prerequisite: Magister of Legends
Unlock Item: Dusk Experiment
Reward: Tricks and Tips for Advanced Nightsword Crafting
Collection
 Collected 1 Items 1
Collected 16 Items 2

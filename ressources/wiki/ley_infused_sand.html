Used in crafting rare Crystal Desert gear and components.
Also sought after by Priory Historian Elisa.

— In-game description

Ley-Infused Sand is a crafting material obtained by completing Bounty events in the Crystal Desert. Ley-Infused Sand can be double-clicked to help locate Priory Historian Elisa, who also trades them for various rewards, including Bounty Hunter's armor recipes.

Notes[edit]

  • When used in the map in which Priory Historian Elisa can be found, the following message is shown in chat: "Bright ley energy flickers through the sand, attracted to Priory Researcher Elisa's presence on this map."
  • When used outside of the map in which Priory Historian Elisa can be found, the following message is shown in chat: "Energy pulses weakly through the sand, suggesting Priory Researcher Elisa is in a different area of the Crystal Desert."

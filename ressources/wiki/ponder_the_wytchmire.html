Acquisition

[edit]

Ponder the twisted faces formed by the rotting trees of Wychmire Swamp in Caledon Forest. Nearby waypoints that can be used to reach the swamp are  Falias Thorp Waypoint — and  Twilight Arbor Waypoint — .

Notes

[edit]
  • The trees are located just to the west of where the Great Jungle Wurm spawns.
  • If the client graphics settings for Environment is set to LOW, the faces do not appear.
